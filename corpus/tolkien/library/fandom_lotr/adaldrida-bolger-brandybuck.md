# Adaldrida (Bolger) Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Adaldrida_(Bolger)_Brandybuck

Adaldrida Brandybuck (née Bolger ) was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Adaldrida was born in SR 1218 to Gundahar Bolger and Dina Diggle . She married Marmadoc Brandybuck and had two children, the later well-known Gorbadoc and Orgulas Brandybuck , and two other unnamed daughters. Her date of death is unknown.
