# Adalgar Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Adalgar_Bolger

Adalgar Bolger ( SR 1215 - SR 1314 ) was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Adalgar was the eldest child and son of Gundahar Bolger and Dina Diggle . He had one sister named Adaldrida Bolger . He married and had three children: Rudigar , Rudibert , and Ruby . He died in TA 2914 (SR 1314) at the age of ninety-nine. References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C , "Bolger of Budgeford"
