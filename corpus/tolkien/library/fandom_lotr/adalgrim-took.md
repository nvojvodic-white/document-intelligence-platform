# Adalgrim Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Adalgrim_Took

Adalgrim Took was a Hobbit of the Shire , and the grandfather of Meriadoc Brandybuck and Peregrin Took . Biography Adalgrim was the son of Hildigrim Took and Rosa Baggins . He had four daughters and one son. The youngest of his daughters was Esmeralda (Took) Brandybuck , the mother of Meriadoc Brandybuck. Adalgrim's son was Paladin II Took , the father of Peregrin Took. Adalgrim was a cousin of Bilbo Baggins , although he did not attend Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party in TA 3001 . Although Adalgrim was never Shire-thain , his son Paladin became Shire-thain in TA 3015 , when Ferumbras III Took , Paladin's cousin, died. Adalgrim died in TA 2982 (SR 1382).
