# Adamanta (Chubb) Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Adamanta_(Chubb)_Took

Adamanta Took, née Chubb was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Adamanta was born in TA 2817 (SR 1217). She was the wife of Gerontius "The Old" Took . Adamanta and Gerontius had a large family, Adamanta becoming the mother of Isengrim III Took , Hildigard Took , Isumbras IV Took , Hildigrim Took , Isembold Took , Hildifons Took , Isembard Took , Hildibrand Took , Belladonna (Took) Baggins , Donnamira (Boffin) Took , Mirabella (Took) Brandybuck and Isengar Took . She died in TA 2923 (SR 1323).
