# Alfrida (of the Yale) Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Alfrida_(of_the_Yale)_Bolger

Alfrida of the Yale was a hobbit of the Shire . Presumably from the Yale , she at some point married Gundolpho Bolger , to whom she bore Gundahar , Rudolph , and Gundahad . Nothing further of her life is known. References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C : "Bolger of Budgeford"
