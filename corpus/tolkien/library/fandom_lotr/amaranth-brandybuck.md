# Amaranth Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Amaranth_Brandybuck

Amaranth Brandybuck was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Amaranth was born in TA 2904 (SR 1304) to Mirabella Took and Gorbadoc Brandybuck . Amaranth's older brother, Rory , was the grandfather of Meriadoc Brandybuck . She also had five younger siblings one of which, Primula , was the mother of Frodo Baggins . Her other siblings were Saradas , Dodinas , Asphodel , and Dinodas . Amaranth Brandybuck died in TA 2998 (SR 1398). Name " Amaranth " is a poetic term for a flower that never withers. It is also the name of a family of 60-70 plants found globally, used as a food source and in ornamental gardens. Certain members of this family are considered weeds.
