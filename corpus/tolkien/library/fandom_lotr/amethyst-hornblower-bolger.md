# Amethyst (Hornblower) Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Amethyst_(Hornblower)_Bolger

This article refers to the wife of Rudibert Bolger . For other namesakes, see Horn (disambiguation) . Amethyst Hornblower was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography At some point between SR 1260 and SR 1301 she married Rudibert Bolger , to whom she bore Adalbert Bolger . Presumably she lived in or near Budgeford with her husband, though nothing else of her life is known. References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C , "Bolger of Budgeford"
