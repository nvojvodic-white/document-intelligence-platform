# Anson Roper

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Anson_Roper

Anson Roper was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography He was born in TA 2961 ( SR 1361 ), and was the only recorded son of Andwise Roper of Tighfield . Little else is known of his life, save that he may have stayed in Tighfield and followed in his father's occupation as a roper. He was the cousin of Samwise Gamgee . In adaptations In The Lord of the Rings Online , Anson Roper lives in Tighfield and is indeed in the rope-making business.
