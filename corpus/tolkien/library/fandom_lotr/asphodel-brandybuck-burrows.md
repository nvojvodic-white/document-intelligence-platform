# Asphodel (Brandybuck) Burrows

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Asphodel_(Brandybuck)_Burrows

Asphodel Burrows (née Brandybuck ) was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Asphodel was born in TA 2913 (SR 1313). She was the fifth child of Gorbadoc Brandybuck and Mirabella Took , the wife of Rufus Burrows , and mother of Milo Burrows . She attended Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party in TA 3001 . She died in TA 3012 (SR 1412). Notes Asphodel is also the name of a group of yellow and white flowers found by Frodo and Sam when they were in Ithilien .
