# Balbo Baggins

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Balbo_Baggins

Balbo Baggins was a hobbit of the Shire and the first recorded member of the Baggins family. He was the great-grandfather of Bilbo , great-great-grandfather of Frodo and great-great-great grandfather of Merry and Pippin . Biography Balbo Baggins married Berylla Boffin and had five children: Mungo , Pansy , Ponto , Largo , and Lily . Balbo was the first recorded Baggins in the Shire, and the ancestor of the Baggins family of Hobbiton . Note On Balbo's dates: In the published Baggins genealogy only Balbo's birthdate is shown, but by looking at his children it is possible to estimate his date of death.
