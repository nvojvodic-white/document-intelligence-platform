Battle The Haradrim were passing through Ithilien and were suddenly assailed at range. Hooded men, the Rangers of Ithilien, led by Faramir son of Denethor , were shooting at them from the thick cover. The Haradrim began to scatter as soon as the Rangers of Ithilien shot down their Mûmak . The Rangers pursued them until they had slain nearly all of the Haradrim. Some escaped, but were hunted down later that night. The Mûmak likely escaped.
