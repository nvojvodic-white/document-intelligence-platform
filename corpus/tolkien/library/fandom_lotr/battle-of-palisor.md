The War of Palisor was a terrifying war in the First Age 's eastern Middle-earth . History A servant of Melko called Fankil turned most Men against the Elves , making them bow to Melko in the House of the Lord . Fankil led these Men along with Orcs and jealous Dwarves in a war against rebellious Men who had sided with the Dark Elves . Following the war, Men were scattered in all directions. One large host of repenting Men, the Edain , fled to the Westlands .
