# Battle of Talath Dirnen

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Talath_Dirnen

The Battle of Talath Dirnen was the name of a battle which represented a series of large scale military battles, in which Túrin , along with the Elves of Nargothrond ,  rid the region of Talath Dirnen of orcs , wargs , and trolls . This took place shortly after Turin was brought to Nargothrond by Gwindor . References ↑ The Children of Húrin , Ch. 10, Túrin in Nargothrond
