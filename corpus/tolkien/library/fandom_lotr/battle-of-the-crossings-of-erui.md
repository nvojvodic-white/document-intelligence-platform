# Battle of the Crossings of Erui

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_the_Crossings_of_Erui

The Battle of the Crossings of Erui was the last battle of the Kin-strife of Gondor . History After a decade of cruel rule by Castamir the Usurper , the people of Gondor rebelled, and the rightful king Eldacar of Gondor returned to his Kingdom with an army from Rhovanion in TA 1447 . Castamir and his men faced Eldacar at the Crossings of Erui , on the road from Minas Tirith to Pelargir , and Eldacar personally killed Castamir there. After this victory, Castamir's heirs were then besieged in Pelargir, eventually fleeing by sea to Umbar . However, many Dúnedain of pure Númenórean blood were killed in the battle.
