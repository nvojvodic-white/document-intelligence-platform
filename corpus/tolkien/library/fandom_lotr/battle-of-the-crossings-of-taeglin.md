# Battle of the Crossings of Taeglin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_the_Crossings_of_Taeglin

The Battle of the Crossings of Taeglin occurred in the year 495 of the First Age , with Orcs making a great attack on the forest of Forest of Brethil and killing the leader of Brethil , Handir . No further details are recorded. After the battle, Handir's son Brandir became leader of the Haladin .
