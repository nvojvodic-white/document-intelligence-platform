History On March 15 , 3019 , the realm of Thranduil was invaded by the forces of Dol Guldur. The majority of the Orcs were sent to attack Lothlórien , with the remainder attacking the Woodland Realm, trying to secure the Elvenking's flank. Easterlings were supposed to arrive and overwhelm the Elves, but they were bogged down in battle with the Dwarves and the Dale-men at Erebor . The Elvenking marshalled his forces and marched to meet the invaders. Upon doing so, there was a long and vicious battle from which ensued a "great ruin of fire" among the woods. The Elves however had the advantages of superior discipline and tactics and their intimate knowledge of their forested home territory. In the end, King Thranduil and his people emerged as victors, and he announced a New Year of the Elves. Aftermath On the day of the New Year, Thranduil and Celeborn met in the forest where they renamed Mirkwood as Eryn Lasgalen, The Wood of the Greenleaves. Thranduil gave the southern part of his realm to Celeborn, and kept the northern part as far as the mountains for himself and his people. The woodlands in between were given to the Beornings and the Woodmen of Western Mirkwood .
