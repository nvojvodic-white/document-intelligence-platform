# Belba (Baggins) Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Belba_(Baggins)_Bolger

Belba Bolger (née Baggins ) was the second child and elder daughter of Mungo and Laura Baggins , and was Bilbo's aunt. She married Rudigar Bolger and had one son, Herugar .
