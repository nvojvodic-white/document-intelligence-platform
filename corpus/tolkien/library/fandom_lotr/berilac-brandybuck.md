# Berilac Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Berilac_Brandybuck

Berilac Brandybuck was a hobbit of the Shire . Berilac was born in TA 2980 ( SR 1380 ) in Buckland to Merimac Brandybuck and his unnamed wife.
