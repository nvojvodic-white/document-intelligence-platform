# Bilbo Gardner

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Bilbo_Gardner

"Can you see anything?" "Nothing. There's nothing." The descriptive majority of this article's text is unsourced , and should be supported with references . Bilbo Gardner was a Hobbit of the Shire . He was the tenth child and fifth son of Samwise and Rosie (Cotton) Gamgee . He was born in FO 15 , or 1436 by the Shire Reckoning , and named after Bilbo Baggins , his father's former master. He and Bilbo Baggins never would have met however, as Bilbo Baggins had already sailed away to the Undying Lands .
