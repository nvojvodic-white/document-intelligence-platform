# Bingo Baggins

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Bingo_Baggins

Bingo Baggins was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Bingo was born in TA 2864 (SR 1264) and was the brother of Bungo . He married Chica Chubb and they had one son, Falco Chubb-Baggins . Falco was most notable as Bilbo's cousin. He died in TA 2960 (SR 1360). Behind the scenes Originally, Frodo was going to be called Bingo, but after a time, Tolkien decided that that name was too silly for The Lord of the Rings , which was becoming ever more serious. This name may be a callback to Frodo's earlier name. References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C , "Baggins of Hobbiton"
