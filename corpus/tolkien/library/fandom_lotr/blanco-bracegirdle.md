# Blanco Bracegirdle

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Blanco_Bracegirdle

Blanco Bracegirdle was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Blanco was the child of unnamed Bracegirdles . He related himself with the Boffin family when he married Primrose Boffin . They had two children: Bruno and Lobelia .
