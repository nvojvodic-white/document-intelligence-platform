# Blanco

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Blanco

Blanco was a Fallohide - hobbit adventurer and one of the two recognized founders of the Shire . Biography Originally from Bree , Blanco and his brother Marcho obtained permission from the King of Arthedain , Argeleb II , and with a large following of Hobbits ventured westward in search of new lands to settle. By TA 1601 , they reached a rich and fertile land between the Brandywine River and the Far Downs , where they founded the Shire.
