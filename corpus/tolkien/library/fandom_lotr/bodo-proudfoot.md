# Bodo Proudfoot

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Bodo_Proudfoot

Bodo Proudfoot was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Bodo was born around TA 2862 (SR 1262). He married Linda Baggins and they had a son, Odo .
