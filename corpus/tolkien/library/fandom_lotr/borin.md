# Borin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Borin

Borin was a Dwarf of Durin's Folk , the second son of King Náin II and brother of Dáin I . Biography After his younger nephew, Frór , and brother were slain, Borin departed from the Ered Mithrin with his younger nephew, Thrór , to the Lonely Mountain in eastern Middle-earth , and lived there until his death. Thrór would someday be King of the Lonely Mountain , until the dragon Smaug assaulted it. Borin was an ancestor of Balin , of Thorin Oakenshield 's company , and Gimli of the Fellowship of the Ring .
