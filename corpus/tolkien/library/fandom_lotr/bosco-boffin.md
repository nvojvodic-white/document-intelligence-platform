# Bosco Boffin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Bosco_Boffin

Bosco Boffin was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Bosco was the eldest child and son of Buffo Boffin and Ivy Goodenough . He had three other siblings: Basso , Briffo , and Berylla . He married and had one son in TA 2812 (SR 1212), named Otto Boffin . He died in TA 2858 (SR 1258) at the age of ninety-one.
