# Bowman Cotton

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Bowman_Cotton

Bowman " Nick " Cotton was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Bowman was born in TA 2986 (SR 1386), the fourth child and third son of Tolman Cotton Sr. and Lily Cotton , of the Browns Family . He had three older siblings: Tolman (Tom) Jr ., Rose (Rosie) and Wilcome (Jolly) . He also had a younger brother, Carl (Nibs) , who was three years his junior. He fought along side his brothers and father in the Battle of Bywater . His older sister, Rosie, married Samwise Gamgee , who had been a long time family friend. In adaptations In The Lord of the Rings Online , Nick Cotton can be found on his family farm, working the soil alongside his brother Jolly. He plays no role in the game.
