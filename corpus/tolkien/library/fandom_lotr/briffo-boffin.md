# Briffo Boffin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Briffo_Boffin

Briffo Boffin (b. TA 2770 (SR 1170) was a hobbit of the Shire and of Bree . Biography Briffo was the third child and son of Buffo Boffin and Ivy Goodenough . He had three other siblings: Bosco , Basso , and Berylla . He originally lived in the Yale as the Boffins did but moved to Bree in TA 2810 (SR 1170) forty years after his birth. He did not marry.
