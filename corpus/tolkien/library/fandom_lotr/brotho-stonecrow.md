# Brotho Stonecrow

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Brotho_Stonecrow

Brotho Stonecrow was a hobbit of Bywater .  He was a regular at The Green Dragon Inn .  He was a friend of the Sackville-Baggins as well as a shirriff .  In TA 3018 he met two Black Riders on the road to Hobbiton . He was killed by a single slash to the stomach.
