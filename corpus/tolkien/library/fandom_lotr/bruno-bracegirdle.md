# Bruno Bracegirdle

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Bruno_Bracegirdle

Bruno Bracegirdle was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Bruno was the first child and only son of Blanco Bracegirdle and Primrose Bracegirdle . His younger sister was Lobelia Sackville-Baggins , who hated Bilbo Baggins . He married an unnamed woman and had two children: Hugo and Hilda .
