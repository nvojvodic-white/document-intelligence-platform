# Bucca

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Bucca

Bucca of the Marish was a Hobbit of the Shire and the first Shire-thain . He presumably originated from the Marish region of the Shire. Biography Being a well-respected hobbit, Bucca became Shire-thain in place of the King at Fornost in TA 1979 (SR 379). He founded the family of the Oldbucks , who later became the Brandybucks , after settling in Buckland . Later portions of his life, as well as his cause of death, is unknown. Etymology The name Bucca comes from the Old English bucc ("male deer") or bucca ("he-goat").
