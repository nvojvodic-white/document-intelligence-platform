# Buffo Boffin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Buffo_Boffin

Buffo Boffin was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Although not officially recorded, it is believed Buffo may have lived around 250 years before the War of the Ring . Buffo was the first recorded Boffin according to the Boffin family tree. He lived in The Yale which would become the traditional home of the family. He was the husband of Ivy Goodenough . With her he had four children - Bosco , Basso , Briffo and Berylla . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C : Family Trees, Boffin of the Yale
