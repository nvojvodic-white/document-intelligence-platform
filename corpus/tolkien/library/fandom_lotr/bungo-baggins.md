# Bungo Baggins

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Bungo_Baggins

Bungo Baggins was a Hobbit of the Shire and the father of Bilbo . Biography Bungo was born in TA 2846. He was the eldest of the five children of Mungo and Laura (Grubb) Baggins . His siblings were named Belba , Longo , Linda , and Bingo . Bungo married Belladonna Took sometime in S.R. 1280 and built a luxurious Hobbit-hole called Bag End in the Hill at Hobbiton for her. They had one child, Bilbo, who was born in TA 2890 . Bungo died in TA 2926 . Aside from fathering Bilbo, Bungo is most remembered for the construction of the famous Bag End, which had become a topic of great discussion and a place of important meetings and gatherings concerning the world. Quotes " Third time pays for all. " — Bilbo quoting his father just before he enters the mountain tunnel . " Every worm has his weak spot. " — Bilbo quoting his father before reentering the mountain tunnel. " While there's life there's hope. " — Bilbo quoting his father after the Dwarves were in despair at their being trapped in the Lonely Mountain by Smaug.
