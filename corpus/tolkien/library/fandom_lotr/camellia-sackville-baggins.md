# Camellia (Sackville) Baggins

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Camellia_(Sackville)_Baggins

Camellia (Sackville) Baggins was a hobbit of the Shire . Camellia was the wife of Longo Baggins , and the mother of Otho Sackville-Baggins . She was thus the aunt of Bilbo Baggins . Biography Camellia was born around TA 2860 (1260). She was originally Camellia Sackville of the Sackvilles . She was best known for creating the Sackville-Baggins family by her marriage to Longo Baggins. Details of her personal life are for the most part not known, but she may have come from Michel Delving .
