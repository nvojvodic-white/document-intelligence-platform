# Carl Cotton (Cottar's son)

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Carl_Cotton_(Cottar's_son)

Carl was a hobbit of the Shire and the son of Cottar , founder of the Cotton family. Biography Carl was born in TA 2863 ( SR 1263 ), and was the brother of Cotman . Little else is known of his life.
