Carl "Nibs" Cotton , born in SR 1389 was the youngest of the five children of Tolman Cotton Sr . and Lily (née Brown) Cotton . History Carl had three older brothers: Tolman (Tom) Jr ., Wilcome (Jolly ), and Bowman (Nick) along with his dad fought in the Battle of Bywater . Being the youngest, he was exempt from the battle. His older sister, Rose (Rosie) married Samwise Gamgee , a good family friend. In adaptations In The Lord of the Rings Online , Nibs Cotton appears at his family farm only during the annual Spring Festival, during which he is involved in a festival quest. He has a pet cat named "Waldy".
