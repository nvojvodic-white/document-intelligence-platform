# Celandine Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Celandine_Brandybuck

This article is about the hobbit . For the flower, see Celandine . Celandine Brandybuck was a Hobbit of the Shire , a second cousin of Meriadoc Brandybuck . Biography Celandine was born in TA 2994 (SR 1394), the third child of Seredic Brandybuck and Hilda Bracegirdle , who were long descendants and inheritors of the Masters of the Hall of Buckland . Her brothers were Doderic and Ilberic . She was a cousin of Frodo Baggins and one of the many Hobbits who attended Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party . Portrayals in adaptations In The Lord of the Rings Online , Celandine and Mundo Sackville-Baggins are captured by Blackwold brigands. They are then rescued by Aragorn and taken to the village of Archet where he leaves them with the player. After an assault on Archet by the Blackwolds, she returns home to Newbury in Buckland .
