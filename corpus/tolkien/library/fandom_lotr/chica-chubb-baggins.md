# Chica (Chubb) Baggins

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Chica_(Chubb)_Baggins

Chica (Chubb) Baggins was a hobbit of the Shire . At some point, she married Bingo Baggins , to whom she bore Falco Chubb-Baggins . She was additionally known as the aunt of Bilbo Baggins . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C , "Baggins of Hobbiton"
