# Cora (Goodbody) Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Cora_(Goodbody)_Bolger

Cora (Goodbody) Bolger was a hobbit of the Shire and the wife to Rudolph Bolger . She was born in the year SR 1178, and became the mother of Fastolph Bolger in SR 1210.
