# Dáin I

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Dáin_I

This article is about the Dáin I, son of Náin II . For the King under the Mountain, see Dáin II . Dáin I was a Dwarf and a King of Durin's Folk in the Third Age . He was the son of Náin II and had a younger brother, Borin . He had three sons: Thrór , Frór , and Grór . Biography Dáin I's reign was plagued by a " war on the Dwarves " by dragons in the Ered Mithrin . In TA 2589 , Dáin I and his second son Frór were slain at the doors of their hall by a Cold-drake . He was the last King of Durin's Folk to live in the Grey Mountains; shortly after Dáin I was laid to rest in the tombs of the mountain halls, the Grey Mountains were abandoned by the Dwarves. Dáin I was succeeded by his son Thrór, who returned to the Lonely Mountain and unsuccessfully attempted to reclaim Moria . His younger son Grór founded his own realm in the Iron Hills .
