# Daisy Gamgee

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Daisy_Gamgee

Daisy Gamgee was a hobbit of the Shire . She was the third child and first daughter of Hamfast (the Gaffer) Gamgee and Bell Goodchild . Biography Daisy was born in TA 2972 (SR 1372) and had two older brothers: Hamson and Halfred . She also had three younger siblings: May , Samwise , and Marigold . Samwise would later on become one of the greatest hobbits in the history of Middle-earth . Little else is known about Daisy, but she may have been close with her younger brother Sam as speculated from the fact that Sam named his seventh child and fourth daughter Daisy .
