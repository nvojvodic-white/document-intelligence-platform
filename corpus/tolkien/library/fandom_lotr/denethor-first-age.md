# Denethor (First Age)

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Denethor_(First_Age)

This article is about the king of the Laegeldrim . For the tenth Ruling Steward of Gondor , see Denethor I . Additionally for the twenty-sixth and last Ruling Steward of Gondor , see Denethor II . Denethor was the Nandorin Elf and son of Lenwë . He lived during the Years of the Trees and was the first and last King of the Laegeldrim of Ossiriand . Biography Hearing of the power of Thingol , Denethor led many of his people over the Blue Mountains to Beleriand . They settled in Ossiriand, renaming it Lindon , in the east of Beleriand, and Denethor became their king. They called themselves Laegeldrim , or Green-elves. Sometime later, after his return from Aman , Morgoth loosed an army on Beleriand beginning the First Battle . The lightly armed Laegeldrim were driven back to the great hill of Amon Ereb , where Denethor was slain. The Laegeldrim became a reclusive people, seldom taking up arms, and never again naming a king. Etymology The name Denethor comes from the Nandorin words dene ("strong, lithe") and thara ("tall, slender"), and gives the name a meaning of "Lithe and lank".
