# Diamond Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Diamond_Took

Diamond Took or Diamond of Long Cleeve was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Diamond was born in Long Cleeve in the Northfarthing to unnamed parents who were possibly one of the North-Tooks , descended from Bandobras "Bullroarer" Took . She became the wife of Thain Peregrin Took in FO 6 (SR 1427) about eight years after the War of the Ring . She had one son Faramir , named for the Steward of Gondor . Her date of death is not known, but it is probable Diamond probably died sometime before the year FO 63 when Pippin left the Shire to live in Gondor . In adaptations The Lord of the Rings trilogy Diamond was portrayed by Emma Deakin in The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King .
