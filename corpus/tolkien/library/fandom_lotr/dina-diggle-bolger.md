# Dina (Diggle) Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Dina_(Diggle)_Bolger

Dina Diggle was a hobbit of the Shire . History Dina was born around the year TA 2774 (SR 1174). She married Gundahar Bolger and they had two children, Adalgar and Adaldrida . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C : "Bolger of Budgeford"
