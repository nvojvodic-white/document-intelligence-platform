# Dinodas Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Dinodas_Brandybuck

Dinodas Brandybuck was a Hobbit of the Shire. Biography Dinodas was born on SR 1316 . He was the sixth and youngest son of Gorbadoc Brandybuck and Mirabella Took . His siblings were Rorimac (who was the grandfather of Meriadoc ), Amaranth , Saradas , Dodinas , Asphodel , and Primula (who was the mother of Frodo Baggins ). He died on SR 1410 at the age of 94. He had no recorded children.
