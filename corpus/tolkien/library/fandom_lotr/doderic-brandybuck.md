# Doderic Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Doderic_Brandybuck

Doderic Brandybuck was a hobbit of the Shire . Doderic was born in TA 2989 (SR 1389) to Seredic Brandybuck and Hilda Bracegirdle . He had two siblings: Ilberic Brandybuck and Celandine Brandybuck . In adaptations In The Lord of the Rings Online , Doderic Brandybuck had ventured into the Old Forest along with his friend Rollo, son of Farmer Maggot . Saradoc Brandybuck asks the player to find them and send them back.
