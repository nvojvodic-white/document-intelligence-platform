# Dodinas Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Dodinas_Brandybuck

Dodinas Brandybuck was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Dodinas was the fourth son of Gorbadoc Brandybuck and Mirabella Took . His siblings were Rorimac (who was the grandfather of Merry ), Amaranth , Saradas , Asphodel , Dinodas , and Primula (who was the mother of Frodo Baggins ).
