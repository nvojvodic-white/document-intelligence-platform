# Donnamira (Took) Boffin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Donnamira_(Took)_Boffin

Donnamira Boffin (née Took ) was a Hobbit of the Shire and an aunt of Bilbo Baggins . Biography Donnamira was the tenth child of Gerontius "the Old" Took and Adamanta Chubb . Donnamira had nine older siblings: Isengrim III , Hildigard , Isumbras IV , Hildigrim , Isembold , Hildifons , Isembard , Hildibrand , and Belladonna ; and two younger siblings: Mirabella and Isengar . She married Hugo Boffin and had two children Jago and Jessamine . She died in TA 2948 (SR 1348).
