# Druda (Burrows) Boffin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Druda_(Burrows)_Boffin

Druda Burrows Boffin , formerly Druda Burrows, was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Druda was born sometime before TA 2860 (SR 1260). She married Rollo Boffin and the two had an unknown number of children. References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C : Family Trees, Boffin of the Yale
