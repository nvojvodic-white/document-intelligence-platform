# Dudo Baggins

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Dudo_Baggins

Dudo Baggins was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Dudo was the youngest son of Fosco and Ruby Baggins . He had one daughter, Daisy. Dudo attended Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party and died eight years later.
