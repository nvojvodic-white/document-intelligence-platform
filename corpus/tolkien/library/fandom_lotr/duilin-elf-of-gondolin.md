# Duilin (elf of Gondolin)

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Duilin_(elf_of_Gondolin)

This article refers to the Elf-captain of Gondolin . For other namesakes, see Duilin (disambiguation) . Duilin was the Elf -lord of the House of the Swallow in Gondolin . He was said to be the greatest bowman of Gondolin, and the swiftest runner and leaper amongst them. He died in the year FA 510 during the Fall of Gondolin , when he was shot by a fiery bolt from a Balrog .
