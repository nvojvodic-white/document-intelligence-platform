# Durin II

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Durin_II

Durin II was a King of Durin's Folk , who ruled between the late First Age and early Second Age . Little else is known of his life, though he was thought by many Dwarves to be the reincarnation of the elder Durin I , being greatly like him in manner and appearance. In adaptations In The Lord of the Rings Online , Durin II appears briefly in a flashback sequence. He ruled in Gundabad during the early Second Age . He invited the Zhelruka dwarves to dwell in Gundabad after their own ancestral hall was destroyed during the late First Age , but the refugees have never made it to Gundabad, becoming lost in Ered Mithrin along their way.
