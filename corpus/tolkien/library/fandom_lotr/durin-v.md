# Durin V

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Durin_V

Durin V was King of Durin's Folk and of Khazad-dûm ; the fourth descendant of Durin I to bear that name. Biography As with every king bearing the name of Durin, many considered him to be the reincarnation of Durin I (first of the Seven Fathers of the Dwarves ), greatly resembling him in both appearance and manner. His life spanned the early Third Age , ruling the great city of Khazad-dûm at the height of its glory, and without major calamity. Little else is known to have transpired during his reign. Like his ancestors since Durin III, he bore a ring of power made by Celebrimbor in the Second Age . References
