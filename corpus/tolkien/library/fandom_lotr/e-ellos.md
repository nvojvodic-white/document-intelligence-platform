# Eðellos

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Eðellos

Eðellos was the wife of Angrod , second son of Finarfin , and grandmother of Finduilas and Gil-galad , the last High King of the Ñoldor in the Second Age . Biography Eðellos was a Ñoldorin Elf , likely born in Aman . It was in Eldamar that she married Angrod and had one son named Orodreth . Orodreth later escaped to Nargothrond when his father was slain in the Dagor Bragollach . She probably went into exile and translated her name to Sindarin in Middle-earth according to sense (only the Ñoldor who did not reach it had no Sindarin names). She is not mentioned in any of the tales of Beleriand , and so nothing else is known of her fate. Etymology Eðellos was the Sindarin translation of Eldalótë , which was Quenya for "Elven-flower". The ð in her name is pronounced as the "th-" in "heather".
