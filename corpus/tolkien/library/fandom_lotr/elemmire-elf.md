# Elemmírë (elf)

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Elemmírë_(elf)

This article is about the Vanyar elf . For the star, see Elemmírë . Elemmírë was a Vanyar Elf of Valinor of unknown gender. Biography Elemmírë was best known for composing the Aldudénië , which lamented the poisoning of the Two Trees and the Darkening of Valinor , all of which Elemmírë was likely to have witnessed. Elemmírë probably lived at the base of Taniquetil as most Vanyar did. Etymology The name Elemmírë was a Quenya word that meant "star jewel", which comes from the words elen , meaning "star" and mírë , meaning "jewel". The form of the word does not specify gender.
