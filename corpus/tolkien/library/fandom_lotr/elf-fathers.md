# Elf-fathers

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Elf-fathers

"... so small a thing. Such a little thing!" This article is a stub ; please expand it if you can. The Elf-fathers were the three male Eru-begotten Elves who awoke at Cuiviénen by Eru Ilúvatar . The three were the founders of the Three Clans . Imin , Tata , and Enel were their names which translated to "One", "Two", and "Three" in Quenya respectively. Their wives were collectively known as their the Three Elderwomen of the Elves . Their followers were thus named Minyar , Tatyar , and Nelyar . These clans later became known, respectively, as the Vanyar , the Ñoldor , and the Teleri . References
