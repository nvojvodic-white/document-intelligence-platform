# Elfstan Fairbairn

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Elfstan_Fairbairn

Elfstan Fairbairn was a hobbit of the Shire , the only son of Fastred of Greenholm and Elanor Gardner (a daughter of Samwise Gamgee ). He was the second Warden of Westmarch . Biography Elfstan was born in Under-towers in the Westmarch of the Shire in FO 33 (1454 by Shire Reckoning ) - three years after his parents' marriage. He also had a younger sister, Fíriel Fairbairn . His maternal grandparents were Samwise Gamgee and Rosie Cotton .
