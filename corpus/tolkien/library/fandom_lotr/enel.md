# Enel

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Enel

Enel was an Elf of Cuiviénen . Biography Enel was the third Elf to be awakened , according to legend. Imin and Tata awakened him after they themselves awoke. When the Elves were all found, Enel's group numbered the most. His fold became the Sindar , Teleri , and Lindar , and was considered to be the third Elf-father . His wife was Enelyë .
