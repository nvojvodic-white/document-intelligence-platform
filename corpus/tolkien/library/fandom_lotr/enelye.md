# Enelyë

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Enelyë

Enelyë was an Nelyar Elf of Cuiviénen . Enelyë was awakened by Enel , who was the third of the Elves to awaken; they later married. Little else is known of her life. References ↑ The History of Middle-earth , Vol. XI: The War of the Jewels , Part Four: " Quendi and Eldar "
