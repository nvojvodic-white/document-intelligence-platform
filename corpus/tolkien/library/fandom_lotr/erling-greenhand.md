# Erling Greenhand

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Erling_Greenhand

Erling Greenhand was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Erling was the third child of Holman the Greenhanded and his unnamed wife. He had four siblings named Rowan , Halfred , Hending , and Rose . He was a working-class hobbit.
