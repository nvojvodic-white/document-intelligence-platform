The Eru-begotten , otherwise known as the First Elves , First Generation , or Unbegotten , were the very first 144 Elves who were awakened by Eru Ilúvatar on the shores of Cuiviénen during the First Age . Each of them slept beside his or her spouse, and there were 72 such pairs. The name " Firstborn " did not apply as a demonym only of the Eru-begotten, but to all Elves as a whole instead. Only six of these First Elves have known names: the three Elf-fathers and their spouses, the Three Elderwomen of the Elves : Imin , first Elf-father, and Iminyë , first elderwoman Tata , second Elf-father, and Tatië , second elderwoman Enel , third Elf-father, and Enelyë , third elderwoman History Note: This is a section stub .
