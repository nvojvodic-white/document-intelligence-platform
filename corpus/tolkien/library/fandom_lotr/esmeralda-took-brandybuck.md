# Esmeralda (Took) Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Esmeralda_(Took)_Brandybuck

Esmeralda Brandybuck , born Esmeralda Took , was a Hobbit of the the Shire . Biography Esmeralda was born to Adalgrim Took and his unnamed wife. She was the youngest of their five children. She was a descendant of Gerontius Took , and younger sister to Thain Paladin II Took . She married Saradoc Brandybuck , the Master of Buckland , and removed to Brandy Hall . Being a Took and marrying into the Brandybuck family, she was both Peregrin Took 's aunt and Meriadoc Brandybuck 's mother.
