# Estella (Bolger) Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Estella_(Bolger)_Brandybuck

Estella Brandybuck , born Estella Bolger , was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Estella was born in TA 2985 (SR 1385), and was the sister of Fredegar "Fatty" Bolger , the companion that Frodo , Merry , Pippin , and Sam left behind in Crickhollow . Estella was the daughter of Odovacar Bolger and Rosamunda Took . She married Meriadoc Brandybuck of Buckland , and had at least one son. Estella probably died sometime before the year 63 of the Fourth Age when Merry left the Shire to live in Gondor . References ↑ 1.0 1.1 The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C , "Bolger of Budgeford"
