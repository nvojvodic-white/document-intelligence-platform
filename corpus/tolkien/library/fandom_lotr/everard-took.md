# Everard Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Everard_Took

Everard Took was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Everard Took was born in TA 2980 (SR 1380) to Adelard Took . He had an older brother, Reginard Took . In 3001, Everard was in his tweens. He had a girlfriend named Melilot Brandybuck . At Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party in September of TA 3001 , as Bilbo made a speech, Everard and Melilot falsely assuming he had finished, and dancing the "Springle-ring" dance on a table together. Bilbo continued speaking, undeterred, and soon she and Everard stepped down from the table embarrassed. His fate is not told.
