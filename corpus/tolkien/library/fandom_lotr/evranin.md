# Evranin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Evranin

! This content is considered pre-canon. While the subject of this article is based on official information, it was replaced, emended, or abandoned in later stages of the legendarium . ! Evranin was an Elf of the First Age and a nurse of Elwing 's who, in an early version of the tale of the Nauglamír , helped steal the Necklace away from the reach of Fëanor 's sons, alongside one named Gereth .
