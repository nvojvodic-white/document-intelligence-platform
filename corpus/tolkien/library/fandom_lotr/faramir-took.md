# Faramir Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Faramir_Took

This article refers to the Faramir of the Shire . For other namesakes, see Faramir (disambiguation) . Faramir Took was a Hobbit of the Shire and the son of Peregrin Took and Diamond of Long Cleeve . He was also the thirty-third Shire-thain . Biography Faramir was born in FO 10 (SR 1430) and he later married Goldilocks Gardner in FO 42 (SR 1463). He inherited the title of Shire-thain in FO 63 (SR 1484), after Peregrin and Meriadoc left for Edoras and Gondor in that year. Pippin named Faramir Took after Faramir , Steward of Gondor and Prince of Ithilien .
