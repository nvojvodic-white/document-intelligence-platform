# Fastolph Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Fastolph_Bolger

Fastolph Bolger was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Fastolph Bolger was the son of Rudolph Bolger and Cora Goodbody . He married Pansy Baggins . Etymology The name Fastolph is composed by the words fast , which means “firm”, and wulf , which means “wolf”.
