# Ferdibrand Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Ferdibrand_Took

Ferdibrand Took was a hobbit of the Shire . Ferdibrand was born in TA 2983 (SR 1383) and was the son of Ferdinand Took and his unnamed wife. He attended Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party on September 22 , 3001 . In adaptations In The Lord of the Rings Online , Ferdibrand Took lives in Staddle but plays no role in the storyline.
