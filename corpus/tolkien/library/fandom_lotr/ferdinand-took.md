# Ferdinand Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Ferdinand_Took

Ferdinand Took was a hobbit of the Shire . Ferdinand was born in TA 2940 (SR 1340) to Sigismond Took and his unnamed wife. He was the father of Ferdibrand Took . He and his son attended Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C , "Took of Great Smials"
