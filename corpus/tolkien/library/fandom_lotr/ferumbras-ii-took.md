# Ferumbras II Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Ferumbras_II_Took

Ferumbras II Took was a Hobbit of the Shire and the twenty-fourth Shire-thain . Biography Ferumbras was the eldest son of Isumbras III Took and was born in SR 1101 . Despite being a Shire-thain, Ferumbras was eclipsed by his younger brother, Bandobras , who won the Battle of the Green Fields . His son, Fortinbras succeeded him as Shire-thain.
