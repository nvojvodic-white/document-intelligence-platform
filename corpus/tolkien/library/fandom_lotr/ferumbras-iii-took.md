# Ferumbras III Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Ferumbras_III_Took

Ferumbras III Took was a Hobbit of the Shire and the thirtieth Shire-thain . Biography Ferumbras III, while not otherwise particularly famous, was the thirtieth Shire-thain at the time of the Farewell party of Bilbo Baggins at the beginning of The Lord of the Rings . He was the son of Fortinbras II Took ( TA 2878 – TA 2980 , a cousin of Bilbo) and Lalia (Clayhanger) Took . He never married because no one wanted Lalia for a mother-in-law. After his death in TA 3015 (SR 1415) at the age of ninety-nine, the Thainship passed to his second cousin Paladin II Took .
