# Filibert Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Filibert_Bolger

Filibert Bolger was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Filibert was a distant cousin of Frodo Baggins , and nephew of Bilbo Baggins , through marriage to Poppy Chubb-Baggins (married TA 2944 ( SR 1344). He was a guest at Bilbo's Farewell Party . He died in FO 22 (SR 1443) at the age of one-hundred and one. In adaptations In The Lord of the Rings Online , Filibert Bolger lives in Needlehole . He asks the player to find his cow who had become lost in the Rushock Bog .
