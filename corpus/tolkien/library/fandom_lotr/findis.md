# Findis

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Findis

Findis was a Ñoldorin elf , the first daughter of Finwë , the High King of the Ñoldor and Indis ; sister of Fingolfin , Finarfin , and Írimë and half-sister of Fëanor . Biography Findis was the first child of Finwë and Indis. Little is known about her except that she stayed with her mother and younger brother Finarfin in Valinor while her sister Írimë went to Middle-earth with her elder brother Fingolfin. Findis apparently continued to dwell in Aman and did not join the Rebellion of the Ñoldor . It remains unclear if the third daughter survived in the final conception of Finwë's family tree.
