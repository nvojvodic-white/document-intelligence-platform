# Finrún

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Finrún

! This content is considered pre-canon. While the subject of this article is based on official information, it was replaced, emended, or abandoned in later stages of the legendarium . ! Finrún Felageómor was a Ñoldorin Elf in earlier versions of J.R.R. Tolkien 's legendarium . History Finrún was the fourth and youngest son of Finwë Ñoldóran . Perhaps like his brothers, Fëanor , Fingolfin , and Finrod , he was born in Aman , but nothing else is told of him. In other versions In later writings, the character of Finrún was removed and Finarfin replaced Finrod as Finwë's youngest son.
