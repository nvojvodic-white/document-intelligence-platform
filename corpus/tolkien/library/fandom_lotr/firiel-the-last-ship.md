# Fíriel (The Last Ship)

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Fíriel_(The_Last_Ship)

This article refers to the character from The Last Ship . For other namesakes, see Fíriel (disambiguation) . Fíriel was a mortal woman who lived at an unspecified time in Middle-earth . This version of Fíriel appears only in the poem The Last Ship , written by J.R.R. Tolkien . Etymology Fíriel is a Quenya name meaning 'mortal woman'. References
