# Flambard Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Flambard_Took

Flambard Took was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Flambard was born in TA 2887 (SR 1287) to Isembard Took and he had a son named Adelard Took . He died in TA 2989 (SR 1389). References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C , "Took of Great Smials"
