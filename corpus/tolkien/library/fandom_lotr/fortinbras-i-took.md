# Fortinbras I Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Fortinbras_I_Took

Fortinbras Took I was a Hobbit of the Shire and the twenty-fifth Shire-thain . Biography Fortinbras was the son of Ferumbras II Took and was born on the Shire Reckoning SR 1145 . Upon his father's death in SR 1201, he succeeded as Shire-thain and upon his death the office passed on to his son, Gerontius Took .
