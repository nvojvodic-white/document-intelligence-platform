# Fortinbras II Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Fortinbras_II_Took

Fortinbras II Took was a Hobbit of the Shire and the twenty-ninth Shire-thain . Biography Fortinbras was born in TA 2878 (SR 1278). He married Lalia (Clayhanger) Took some time in his life and had one child, Ferumbras III Took . He was one of the cousins of Bilbo Baggins . He became Shire-thain in TA 2939 (SR 1339) and ruled until his death in TA 2980 (SR 1380).
