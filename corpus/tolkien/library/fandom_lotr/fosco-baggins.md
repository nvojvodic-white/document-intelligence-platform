# Fosco Baggins

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Fosco_Baggins

Fosco Baggins was a Hobbit of the Shire and the grandfather of Frodo Baggins . Biography Fosco was the son of Largo Baggins and Tanta (Hornblower) Baggins . Fosco married Ruby Bolger and they had three children Dora , Drogo and Dudo . Their son Drogo was the father of Frodo Baggins. Fosco died in TA 2960 (SR 1360).
