# Frár

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Frár

Frár was a Dwarf of Durin's Folk who accompanied Balin in an attempt to reclaim Moria in the late Third Age . Biography Frár, Flói , Óin , Ori , Lóni , and Náli were those who, with Balin, entered the abandoned dwarf-kingdom of Khazad-dûm in the year TA 2989 . On November 10 , 2994 , however, Balin was killed by an Orc archer as he looked into Kheled-zâram . His companions rescued his body and retreated through the East-gate , but many more Orcs came up through Azanulbizar , eventually breaking the gate. The Bridge of Khazad-dûm was captured next, then the Second Hall, where Frár, Lóni, and Náli were slain. Nothing else is known of Frár's life. References
