# Frerin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Frerin

Frerin was a Dwarf of Durin's Folk . Biography Frerin was the second son of Thráin II , older brother of Dís , younger brother of Thorin Oakenshield , grandson of Thrór , and uncle of Fíli and Kíli . He lived in the Lonely Mountain . Little is known of him except for that he perished at a young age when he joined in the Battle of Azanulbizar in the climactic battle of the War of the Dwarves and Orcs , beneath the East Gate of Khazad-dûm in a wood near the Mirrormere . Appearance In The Hobbit , Thorin said to Fíli that he reminded him very much of his late younger brother. In adaptations In The Lord of the Rings Online , Frerin appears in an extended flashback depicting the Battle of Azanulbizar that shows his eventual demise. In the Thorin's Halls in Ered Luin the largest courtyard is named " Frerin's Court " in his honour.
