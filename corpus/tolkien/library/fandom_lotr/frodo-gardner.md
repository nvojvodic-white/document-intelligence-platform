# Frodo Gardner

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Frodo_Gardner

Frodo Gardner , also known as Frodo Gamgee , was the son of Samwise Gamgee and Rosie Gamgee . He was named after Frodo Baggins , and took the last name of Gardner after his father became the Shire 's gardener. He owned Bag End for a time. Biography The son of Sam Gamgee, Frodo was born in the year FO 2 (SR 1423) and was the second child and eldest son of Sam and Rosie Cotton Gamgee. He had an older sister, Elanor , and eleven younger siblings: Rose , Merry , Pippin , Goldilocks , Hamfast , Daisy , Primrose , Bilbo , Ruby , Robin , and Tolman . Sam Gamgee came to be known as Sam Gardner because of his skill at gardening and his role in replanting the Shire after the War of the Ring . His son Frodo took Gardner as his family name. Frodo Gardner probably inherited Bag End after his father left Middle-earth in the year FO 61 . From Frodo were descended the Gardners of the Hill including his son Holfast Gardner, born in FO 41 , and his grandson Harding of the Hill . In adaptations Frodo Gardner appeared in the arms of Rosie (played by Sarah McLeod ). He is played by Maisy McLeod-Riera , who is the real-life daughter of Sarah McLeod. Video games Frodo Gamgee is a playable character in the Wii and PlayStation 3 version of Lord Of The Rings: Aragorn's Quest , particularly in the Shire overworld apart from Aragorn's eight story levels.
