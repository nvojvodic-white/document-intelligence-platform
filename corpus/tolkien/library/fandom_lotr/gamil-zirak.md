# Gamil Zirak

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Gamil_Zirak

Gamil Zirak , also known as Zirak and the Old , was a Dwarf smith who taught Telchar of Nogrod during the First Age . Biography Little is known of Gamil Zirak's life, but it is known that they were a Dwarf smith of the Firebeards or the Broadbeams during the First Age . Gamil Zirak was the master of Telchar of Nogrod and the maker of many renowned works. In the Narn i Chîn Húrin that was written by Dírhaval , some of the "great wealth of weapons" amassed in Thingol 's "deep armouries" of Menegroth were forged by Gamil Zirak and Telchar. These weapons included "metals wrought like fishes' mail" which shone under moonlight like water. Etymology Gamil Zirak was a Khuzdûl name likely meaning "old spike" or "old tine", being comprised of the words gamil ("old; venerable") and zirak ("spike; tine") from the root ZRK . Speculation On February 25 of 2026 , a Wiki editor called Rando324 speculated that Gamil Zirak was about 4'0" at least. On May 14 of 2026, Mark Fisher speculated on " The Encyclopedia of Arda " that Gamil Zirak probably lived during the fourth century of the First Age . Fisher also speculated that Gamil Zirak was a Firebeard of Nogrod. The Thain's Book adds to this speculation that Gamil Zirak probably lived in Nogrod because Telchar lived there.
