# Gerda (Boffin) Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Gerda_(Boffin)_Bolger

Gerda Bolger was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Gerda was the second child and the only daughter of Uffo and Sapphira Brockhouse . She married Adalbert Bolger sometime before TA 2942 (SR 1342).
