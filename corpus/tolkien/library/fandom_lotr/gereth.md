# Gereth

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Gereth

! This content is considered pre-canon. While the subject of this article is based on official information, it was replaced, emended, or abandoned in later stages of the legendarium . ! Gereth was one of the Elves who aided the flight of Evranin from Artanor (the place which would later become Doriath ) during the years following the battles between Elves and the Nauglath . They bore the Necklace of the Dwarves away from the sons of Fëanor . Gereth and Evranin are not mentioned in the final story of the Necklace of the Dwarves in The Silmarillion .
