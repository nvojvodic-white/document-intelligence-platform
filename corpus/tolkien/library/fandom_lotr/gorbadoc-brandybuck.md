# Gorbadoc Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck

Gorbadoc "Broadbelt" Brandybuck was a hobbit of the Shire and the fifth Master of Buckland . Biography Gorbadoc was married to Mirabella Took and was the father of seven children: Rorimac, Amaranth, Saradas, Dodinas, Asphodel, Dinodas, and Primula. He was the grandfather of Frodo Baggins and great-grandfather of Meriadoc Brandybuck . His parents were Marmadoc Brandybuck and Adaldrida Bolger . Born in TA 2860 (SR 1260), he was noted by the Gaffer for "keeping a mighty generous table." Gorbadoc was the Master of the Hall ( Brandy Hall ) in Buckland . He died in TA 2963 (SR 1363).
