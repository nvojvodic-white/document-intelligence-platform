# Gorbulas Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Gorbulas_Brandybuck

Gorbulas Brandybuck was a Hobbit of Buckland . Biography Gorbulas was the only son of Orgulas Brandybuck , who was the son of Marmadoc Brandybuck and Adaldrida Bolger . He was born on SR 1308 and died some time before Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party . He had one son, Marmadas Brandybuck .
