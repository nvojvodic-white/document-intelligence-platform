# Gormadoc "Deepdelver" Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Gormadoc_"Deepdelver"_Brandybuck

Gormadoc "Deepdelver" Brandybuck was a Hobbit of Buckland and the second Master of Buckland . Biography Gormadoc was born on SR 1134 (TA 2734). He married Malva Headstrong and had three sons with her: Madoc , Sadoc , and Marroc . He was the Master of Buckland until his death on SR 1236 ( TA 2836 ) at age 102. He was an ancestor of both Frodo Baggins and Meriadoc Brandybuck .
