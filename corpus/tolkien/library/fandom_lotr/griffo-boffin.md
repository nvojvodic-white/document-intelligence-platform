# Griffo Boffin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Griffo_Boffin

Griffo Boffin was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Griffo was the son of Gruffo Boffin and his unnamed wife. He married Daisy Baggins . He was at Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C : Family Trees, Boffin of the Yale
