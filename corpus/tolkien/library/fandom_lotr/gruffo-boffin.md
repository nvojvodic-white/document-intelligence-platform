# Gruffo Boffin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Gruffo_Boffin

Gruffo Boffin was a hobbit of the Shire . History Gruffo was born in TA 2900 (SR 1300) to Uffo Boffin and Sapphira Brockhouse . He and his unnamed wife had one child, a son named Griffo Boffin . He died in TA 2999 (SR 1399). Translations Foreign Language Translated name Danish Gruffo Boffer References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C , "Boffin of the Yale"
