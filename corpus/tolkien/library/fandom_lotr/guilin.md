# Guilin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Guilin

Guilin was a noble Elf of Nargothrond and the father of Gelmir and Gwindor . Biography Though little is known about him, Guilin was the father of Gelmir and Gwindor, both of whom played major roles in the First Age . Gelmir's death initiated the Nírnaeth Arnoediad , which resulted in the captivity and enslavement of Gwindor in Angband .
