# Gundabald Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Gundabald_Bolger

Gundabald Bolger was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Gundabald was the son of Gundahad Bolger and his unnamed wife. At some point, he married Salvia Brandybuck, and together they had at least one son, Theobald . Little to nothing else is known of his life.
