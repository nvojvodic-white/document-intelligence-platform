# Gundahad Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Gundahad_Bolger

Gundahad Bolger was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography He was born in the year SR 1180 to Gundolpho Bolger and Alfrida of the Yale as the youngest of three children. Though his wife is unknown, he had one son, Gundabald . Nothing further is known of his life, though he likely resided in Budgeford .
