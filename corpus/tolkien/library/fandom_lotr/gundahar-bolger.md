# Gundahar Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Gundahar_Bolger

Gundahar Bolger was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Gundahar was born in TA 2774 (SR 1174) to Gundolpho Bolger and Alfrida of the Yale . He had two siblings: Gundahad and Rudolph Bolger . He married Dina Diggle and they had two children, Adalgar and Adaldrida Brandybuck . He died in TA 2875 (SR 1275). References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C : "Bolger of Budgeford"
