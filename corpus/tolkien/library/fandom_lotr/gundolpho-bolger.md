# Gundolpho Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Gundolpho_Bolger

Gundolpho Bolger was a hobbit of the Shire , and the patriarch of the Bolger family. Biography Born in the year TA 2731 , Gundolpho at some point married Alfrida of the Yale , who bore him three children: Gundahar , Gundahad , and Rudolph . He died in TA 2830 .
