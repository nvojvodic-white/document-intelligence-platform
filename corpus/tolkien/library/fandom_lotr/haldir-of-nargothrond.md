# Haldir of Nargothrond

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Haldir_of_Nargothrond

! This content is considered pre-canon. While the subject of this article is based on official information, it was replaced, emended, or abandoned in later stages of the legendarium . ! Haldir , or Ordhelm , was a Ñoldorin Elf in earlier versions of J.R.R. Tolkien 's legendarium . History Haldir was the elder son of Orodreth and he was trapped and hung to a tree by the orcs after the sack of Nargothrond .
