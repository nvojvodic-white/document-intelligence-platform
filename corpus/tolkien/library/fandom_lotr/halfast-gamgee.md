# Halfast Gamgee

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Halfast_Gamgee

Halfast Gamgee, more commonly known as " Hal ", was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Halfast was born in TA 2972 (SR 1372) to Halfred Gamgee of Overhill , and was the cousin of Samwise Gamgee . He was known to have worked for the Boffin family as a hunter. It was on one of his hunting jobs in the Northfarthing that he claimed to have seen a very large walking tree-like thing, that he called one of the Tree-men . This was later brought up in a debate between Sam and Ted Sandyman. It was disputed by Ted Sandyman, who voiced the opinion that Hal was apt to imagining things.
