# Halfred Gamgee

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Halfred_Gamgee

This article is about the son of Hamfast . For the father of Halfast, see Halfred of Overhill . Halfred Gamgee was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Halfred was born in TA 2969 as the second son of Hamfast and Bell Gamgee . He had one older brother, Hamson , and four younger siblings; Daisy , May , Samwise , and Marigold . He moved to the Northfarthing later on in his life and died there sometime in the early Fourth Age . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C , "The Longfather-tree of Master Samwise"
