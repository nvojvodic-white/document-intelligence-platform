# Halfred Greenhand

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Halfred_Greenhand

Halfred Greenhand was a gardener and a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Halfred was the son of Holman the Greenhanded . He had four siblings: Rowan (sister), Erling (brother), Hending (brother), and Rose (sister). He was a gardener by trade like his father. He married an unnamed woman with whom he had one child, named Holman Greenhand .
