# Halfred of Overhill

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Halfred_of_Overhill

This article is about the father of Halfast . For the son of Hamfast, see Halfred Gamgee . Halfred of Overhill was a Hobbit of the Shire and a member of the Gamgee family. Biography Halfred was the fourth and youngest child of Hobson 'Roper' Gamgee , his older siblings being Andwise , Hamfast , and May . He was the father of Halfast Gamgee , and the uncle of Samwise Gamgee . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings : Appendix C , "The Longfather-tree of Master Samwise"
