# Hamfast of Gamwich

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Hamfast_of_Gamwich

Hamfast of Gamwich was a hobbit of the Shire , and founder of the Gamwich family. Biography Hamfast of Gamwich was born in TA 2760 (SR 1160), in the village of Gamwich . He married an unnamed woman and had at least one child, Wiseman Gamwich .
