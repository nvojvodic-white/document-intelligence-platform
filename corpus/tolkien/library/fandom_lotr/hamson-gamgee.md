# Hamson Gamgee

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Hamson_Gamgee

Hamson Gamgee was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Hamson was born in Hobbiton . He was the eldest son of Hamfast and Bell Goodchild , and the brother of Halfred , Daisy , May , Marigold , and Samwise . He later moved to Tighfield .
