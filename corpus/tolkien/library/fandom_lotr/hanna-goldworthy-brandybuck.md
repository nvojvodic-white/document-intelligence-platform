# Hanna (Goldworthy) Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Hanna_(Goldworthy)_Brandybuck

Hanna Brandybuck (née Goldworthy ) was a hobbit of the Shire and Buckland . Biography Hanna was born around SR 1175 (TA 2775). She married Madoc Brandybuck and had a son named Marmadoc Brandybuck . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C : Family Trees, Brandybuck of Buckland
