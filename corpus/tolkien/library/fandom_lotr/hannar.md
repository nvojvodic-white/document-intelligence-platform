# Hannar

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Hannar

! This content is considered pre-canon. While the subject of this article is based on official information, it was replaced, emended, or abandoned in later stages of the legendarium . ! Hannar was a character in J.R.R. Tolkien 's early drafts of The Lord of the Rings in the 1940s. Along with his fellow dwarves Anar , Burin (son of Balin ), Lofar , and Nar , he accompanied Bilbo Baggins from Bag End .
