# Herugar Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Herugar_Bolger

"Can you see anything?" "Nothing. There's nothing." The descriptive majority of this article's text is unsourced , and should be supported with references . Herugar Bolger was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Herugar was Rudigar Bolger and Belba Bolger 's son and only child. He was the grandson of Mungo Baggins and Laura Grubb , and a cousin of Bilbo Baggins . He was born in TA 2895 and died in TA 2990 , aged 95. He married Jessamine Boffin and had one son, Odovacar Bolger .
