# Hilda (Bracegirdle) Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Hilda_(Bracegirdle)_Brandybuck

Hilda Brandybuck was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Hilda was born a Bracegirdle in TA 2954 (SR 1354) in Hardbottle to Bruno Bracegirdle and his unnamed wife. She had one sibling; a brother named Hugo . She married Seredic Brandybuck and had three children: Doderic , Ilberic , and Celandine .
