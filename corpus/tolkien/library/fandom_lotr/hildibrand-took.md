# Hildibrand Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Hildibrand_Took

Hildibrand Took was a hobbit of the Shire and one of the many uncles of Bilbo Baggins . Biography Hildibrand was the eighth child of Gerontius "The Old" Took and Adamanta Chubb . Hildibrand had seven older siblings: Isengrim III , Hildigard , Isumbras IV , Hildigrim , Isembold , Hildifons , and Isembard ; and four younger siblings: Belladona , Donnamira , Mirabella , and Isengar . Hildibrand married an unknown woman, and they had Sigismond Took , a son. Hildibrand died in TA 2934 . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C : Family Trees, Took of Great Smials
