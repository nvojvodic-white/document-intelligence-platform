# Hildigard Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Hildigard_Took

Hildigard Took was a hobbit of the Shire . Hildigard was the child of Adamanta Took and Gerontius Took , who was more commonly known as the Old Took. Hildigard died at a young age. Etymology and gender Though Hildigard's gender is not specified by in Appendix C , Hildigard (and the more popular spelling Hildegard ) is a Germanic female name. However, The Hobbit describes the Old Took as having only three daughters, and three of his other children ( Belladonna , Donnamira , and Mirabella ) are known to be female. The name is derived from the elements hild (battle) and gard (enclosure).
