# Hob Gammidge

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Hob_Gammidge

Hob Gammidge was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Hob was the son of Wiseman Gamwich . He was a roper in Tighfield . He married Rowan , the eldest daughter of Holman the Greenhanded . They had at least one child, Hobson Gamgee .
