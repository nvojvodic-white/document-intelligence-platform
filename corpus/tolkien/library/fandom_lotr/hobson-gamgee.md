# Hobson Gamgee

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Hobson_Gamgee

Hobson 'Roper' Gamgee was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Hobson was the son of Hob Gammidge and his wife Rowan Greenhand . He had four children: Andwise , Hamfast , May , and Halfred Gamgee . He was the grandfather of Samwise Gamgee through his son Hamfast. Little else is known of his life save that he was a rope-maker and may have been a resident of Tighfield .
