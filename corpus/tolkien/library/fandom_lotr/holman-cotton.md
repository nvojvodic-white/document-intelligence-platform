# Holman Cotton

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Holman_Cotton

Holman Cotton , also known as "Long Hom" was a hobbit of the Shire and a member of the Cotton family. Holman was born in TA 2902 ( SR 1302 ) to Cotman Cotton and Rose Greenhand . He was from the village of Bywater in the Westfarthing of the Shire, and had two children with an unnamed wife, Tolman and Wilcombe . Little else is known of his life.
