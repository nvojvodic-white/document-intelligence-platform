# Holman Greenhand

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Holman_Greenhand

This article is about the son of Halfred . For the patriarch of the Greenhand family, see Holman the Greenhanded . Holman Greenhand was a hobbit of the Shire , and the only recorded son of Halfred Greenhand .
