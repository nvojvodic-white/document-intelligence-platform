# Hugo Boffin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Hugo_Boffin

Hugo Boffin was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Hugo was the son of Otto Boffin and Lavender Grubb . He married Donnamira Took . The couple had two children: Jago and Jessamine . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C : Family Trees, Boffin of the Yale
