# Ilberic Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Ilberic_Brandybuck

Ilberic Brandybuck was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Ilberic was born in TA 2991 (SR 1391) to Seredic Brandybuck and Hilda Bracegirdle . He had two siblings: Doderic Brandybuck and Celandine Brandybuck . It is possible that he attended Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party .
