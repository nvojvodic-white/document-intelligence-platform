# Iminyë

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Iminyë

Iminyë was an Minyar Elf of Cuiviénen . According to legend, she was woken by Imin , who was the first of the Elves to awaken in Cuiviénen. She would eventually wed him; little else is known of her life. See also Awakening of the Elves References ↑ The History of Middle-earth , Vol. XI: " The War of the Jewels ", "Part Four.
