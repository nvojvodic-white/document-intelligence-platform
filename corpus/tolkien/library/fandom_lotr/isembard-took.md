# Isembard Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Isembard_Took

Isembard Took was one of the many uncles of Bilbo Baggins . Biography Isembard was the seventh child of Gerontius "The Old" Took and Adamanta (Chubb) Took . Isembard had six older siblings: Isengrim III, Hildigard, Isumbras IV, Hildigrim , Isembold, and Hildifons ; and five younger siblings: Hildibrand , Belladonna , Donnamira , Mirabella , and Isengar. He had a son named Flambard , who was born in TA 2887 (SR 1287). Isembard died in TA 2946 (SR 1346). References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C , "Took of Great Smials"
