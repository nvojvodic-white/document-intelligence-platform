# Isembold Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Isembold_Took

Isembold Took was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Isembold was the fifth child of Gerontius Took and Adamanta Chubb . He came from a large family which included eleven other brothers and sisters: Isengrim , Hildigard , Isumbras , Hildigrim , Hildifons , Isembard , Hildibrand , Belladonna , Donnamira , Mirabella , and Isengar . The names of his wife and children are not known, though he was said to have many descendants.
