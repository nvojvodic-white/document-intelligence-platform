# Isengar Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Isengar_Took

Isengar Took was a Hobbit of the Shire and one of the many uncles of Bilbo Baggins . Biography Isengar was the eleventh and youngest child of Gerontius Took and Adamanta Chubb . He came from a large family of eleven other brothers and sisters: Isengrim , Hildigard , Isumbras , Hildigrim , Hildifons , Isembard , Hildibrand , Belladonna , Donnamira , and Mirabella . He is not recorded to have married or have had any children, but is said to have 'gone to sea' in his youth. References ↑ The Lord of the Rings, Appendix C : Family Trees, Tooks of Great Smials
