# Isengrim I Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Isengrim_I_Took

Isengrim I Took was a Hobbit of the Shire , who held the title of Shire-thain . History Isengrim lived and held the title of Shire-thain in a period between SR 740 and SR 1083 . During this period there is a gap in  the Took family genealogy; thus, his existence is attested only by the fact that he was the namesake of Isengrim II and Isengrim III .
