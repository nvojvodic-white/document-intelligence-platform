# Isengrim III Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Isengrim_III_Took

Isengrim III Took was a Hobbit of the Shire and the twenty-seventh Shire-thain . Biography Isengrim was the first child of Gerontius Took and Adamanta Chubb . He came from a large family of eleven other brothers and sisters: Hildigard , Isumbras , Hildigrim , Hildifons , Isembard , Hildibrand , Belladonna , Donnamira , Mirabella , and Isengar . He did not marry and therefore had no descendants of his own.
