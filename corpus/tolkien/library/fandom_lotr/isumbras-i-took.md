# Isumbras I Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Isumbras_I_Took

Isumbras I Took was a Hobbit of the Shire and the thirteenth Shire-thain , the first of the Took line. Biography Isumbras Took was born sometime before TA 2340 (SR 740). He succeeded Gorhendad Brandybuck as Shire-thain. He was succeeded by an unknown Took. It was unknown whether had married and had children. Afterwards, the Thainship was held by the Tooks of the Great Smials . Shire-thain Preceded by Gorhendad Brandybuck Isumbras I Took Succeeded by Unknown, eventually Isengrim II Took TA 2340 - ?
