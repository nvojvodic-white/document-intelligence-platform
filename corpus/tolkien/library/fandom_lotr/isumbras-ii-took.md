# Isumbras II Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Isumbras_II_Took

Isumbras II Took was a Hobbit of the Shire who held the title of Shire-thain . History Isumbras lived and was Shire-thain for some period between SR 740 and SR 1083 . During this period, there is a gap in the Took-clan family genealogy; thus, his existence is confirmed only by him being the namesake of Isumbras III and Isumbras IV . Shire-thain Preceded by ?
