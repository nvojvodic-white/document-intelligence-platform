# Isumbras IV Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Isumbras_IV_Took

Isumbras IV Took was a hobbit of the Shire and the twenty-eighth Shire-thain . He was also one of the many uncles of Bilbo Baggins . Biography Isumbras was the third child of Gerontius Took and Adamanta Chubb . He came from a large family of eleven other brothers and sisters: Isengrim , Hildigard , Hildigrim , Hildifons , Isembard , Hildibrand , Belladonna , Donnamira , Mirabella , and Isengar . He was the father of Fortinbras Took and the grandfather of Ferumbras Took . As Shire-thain, he ruled from TA 2930 to his death in TA 2939 .
