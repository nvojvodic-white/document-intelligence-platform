# Ivy (Goodenough) Boffin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Ivy_(Goodenough)_Boffin

Ivy Boffin (née Goodenough ) was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Ivy was a member of the little known Goodenough family . She entered the Boffin family of Yale when she married Buffo Boffin . She bore four children: Bosco , Basso , Briffo , and Berylla Boffin . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C : Family Trees, Boffin of the Yale
