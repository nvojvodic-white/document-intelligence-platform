# Jago Boffin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Jago_Boffin

Jago Boffin was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Jago was the eldest child of Hugo Boffin and Donnamira Took . He had one son to an unnamed wife. He died in TA 2986 (SR 1386).
