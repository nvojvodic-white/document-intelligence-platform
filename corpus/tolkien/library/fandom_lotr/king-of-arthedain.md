# King of Arthedain

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/King_of_Arthedain

The King of Arthedain was a title created after the breakup of Arnor into the sub-kingdoms of Arthedain , Cardolan , and Rhudaur in TA 861 . History After the death of Eärendur , his three sons quarrelled over the succession causing a civil war between them and their respective realms. Amlaith , the eldest son claimed the throne of Arnor but his rule was limited only to the region of Arthedain with its capital at Fornost , while the others founded Cardolan and Rhudaur. Arthedain's kings made several attempts to reunite the kingdom; Argeleb I was even able to exert his authority over Cardolan, but corruption in the kingdoms and war with Angmar prevented any reunification of Arnor. The kingdom and the title ended with the death of Arvedui , but the House of Isildur continued in the Chieftains of the Dúnedain , ending in Aragorn II Elessar , who restored the North-kingdom.
