# Lavender (Grubb) Boffin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Lavender_(Grubb)_Boffin

Lavender (Grubb) Boffin was a hobbit of the Shire . She was born a Grubb and was the sister of Laura Grubb . She married Otto Boffin and they had four children: Hugo , Uffo , Rollo , and Primrose . Etymology Lavender is the name of a purple scented flower . Her name would be keeping in the hobbit tradition of naming women after flowers.
