# Lily (Baggins) Goodbody

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Lily_(Baggins)_Goodbody

Lily (Baggins) Goodbody was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Lily was born in TA 2822 (SR 1222) to Balbo Baggins and Berylla Boffin . Mungo Baggins , Pansy Baggins , Ponto Baggins I , and Largo Baggins were Lily's four older siblings. Lily was wed to Togo Goodbody and she changed her last name from Baggins to Goodbody . They had no known children. Lily Baggins Goodbody died in TA 2912 (SR 1312). Etymology Like many female hobbits, Lily was named after a flower, namely the Lilium genus in real life.
