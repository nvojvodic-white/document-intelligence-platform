# Linda (Baggins) Proudfoot

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Linda_(Baggins)_Proudfoot

Linda Proudfoot (née Baggins ) ( TA 2862 (SR 1261) - TA 2963 (SR 1361)) was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Linda was the sister of Bungo Baggins . She married Bodo Proudfoot , and had a son named Odo ( TA 2904 - TA 3005 ). Linda was also an aunt of Bilbo Baggins .
