# Longo Baggins

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Longo_Baggins

Longo Baggins was the third child of Mungo and Laura Baggins and the uncle of Bilbo Baggins . Biography Longo married Camellia Sackville and as a result sired the Sackville-Baggins family with the birth of their son, Otho .
