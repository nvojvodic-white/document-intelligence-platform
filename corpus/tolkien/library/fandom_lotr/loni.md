# Lóni

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Lóni

Lóni was a Dwarf of Durin's Folk . Biography Lóni entered Moria with Balin , Frár , Óin , Ori , Flói , and Náli in their attempt to form a colony there in TA 2989 . Lóni was killed in a battle some five years later, in TA 2994 , while defending the Bridge of Khazad-dûm and the Second Hall alongside Náli and Frár . His story was told in the Book of Mazarbul , which was found by Gandalf when he and the Fellowship passed through Moria during their travels. References
