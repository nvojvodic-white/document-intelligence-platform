# Mablon

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Mablon

! This content is considered pre-canon. While the subject of this article is based on official information, it was replaced, emended, or abandoned in later stages of the legendarium . ! Mablon was an Elf of the First Age in earlier versions of the legendarium . Biography Mablon was part of a host of Úmanyar , called " Ilkorindi " (precursor to the Sindar ), that Tareg led both in the War of Palisor and in the Battle of Unnumbered Tears against Melko and his armies of Goblins , Nauglath , monsters, and wicked Men . Mablon was slain in the last battle, bravely sacrificing himself by challenging a host of Balrogs to save the remainders of his people.
