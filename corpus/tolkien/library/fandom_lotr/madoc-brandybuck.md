# Madoc Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Madoc_Brandybuck

Madoc Brandybuck was a Hobbit of Buckland and the third Master of Buckland . Biography Madoc was born on SR 1175 ( TA 2775 ) and was the son of Gormadoc Brandybuck and Malva Headstrong . He married Hanna Goldworthy of the Shire and had one son with her, Marmadoc "Masterful" . Madoc was the third Master of Buckland from SR 1236 until his death on SR 1277 at age 102.
