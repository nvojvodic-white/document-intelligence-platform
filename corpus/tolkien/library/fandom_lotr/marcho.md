# Marcho

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Marcho

Marcho was a Fallohide - hobbit adventurer and one of the founders of the Shire . Biography Originally living in Bree , he and his brother Blanco , along with a large following of hobbits, obtained permission from the King of Arthedain , Argeleb II , seated at Fornost to journey westward in search of new lands to settle. By TA 1601 , he and his followers reached a rich and fertile land, between the Brandywine River and the Far Downs , where they founded the Shire.
