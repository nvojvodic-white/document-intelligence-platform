# Marigold (Gamgee) Cotton

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Marigold_(Gamgee)_Cotton

Marigold (Gamgee) Cotton was a Hobbit of the Shire . History Marigold was born on TA 2983 (SR 1383). She was the youngest child of Hamfast Gamgee ("The Gaffer") and Bell Goodchild . She was Samwise Gamgee 's younger sister. Marigold had four other siblings: Hamson , Halfred , Daisy , and May . She would later on marry Tolman Cotton , a good family friend who was three years her senior. She was also good friends with her husband's sister, Rosie , who would later go on to marry Sam. Etymology Like many female hobbits, Marigold was named after the homonymous flower.
