# Marmadas Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Marmadas_Brandybuck

This article is about the hobbit of the Shire . For the hobbit of Rhûn from The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power , see Marmadas . Marmadas Brandybuck was a Hobbit of Buckland . Biography Marmadas was the only son of Gorbulas Brandybuck . He was born on SR 1343 . He had three children, named Merimas , Mentha , and Melilot . References
