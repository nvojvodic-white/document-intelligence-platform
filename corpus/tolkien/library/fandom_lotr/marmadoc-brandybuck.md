# Marmadoc Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Marmadoc_Brandybuck

Marmadoc Brandybuck , also called " Masterful ", was a hobbit of the Shire and the fourth Master of Buckland . Biography Marmadoc was born in TA 2817 (SR 1217) and was the son of Madoc Brandybuck and his wife Hanna Goldworthy . He lived in Buckland in Brandy Hall . He married Adaldrida Bolger and had two children, the later well known Gorbadoc and Orgulas Brandybuck . On the death of his father in TA 2877 , he became Master of Buckland until his death in TA 2910 (SR 1310).
