# Marroc Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Marroc_Brandybuck

Marroc Brandybuck was a Hobbit from Buckland. Biography Marroc was the youngest son of Gormadoc Brandybuck , the Master of Buckland , and Malva Headstrong . His two older brothers were Madoc and Sadoc . He has many descendants.
