# May Gamgee (Hobson's daughter)

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/May_Gamgee_(Hobson's_daughter)

This article is about the daughter of Hobson Gamgee . For the daughter of Hamfast, see May Gamgee . May Gamgee was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography May was the third child and only daughter of Hobson Gamgee , and the younger sister of Hamfast Gamgee . She was the aunt of Samwise Gamgee , Hamfast's son. Little else is known of her life, save that she may have been a resident of Tighfield . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings : Appendix C , "The Longfather-tree of Master Samwise"
