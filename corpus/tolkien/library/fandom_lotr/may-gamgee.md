# May Gamgee

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/May_Gamgee

This article is about the daughter of Hamfast . For the daughter of Hobson, see May Gamgee (Hobson's daughter) . May Gamgee was a hobbit of the Shire , likely named after her aunt May . History May Gamgee was born in TA 2976 ( SR 1376 ) and was the fourth child and the second daughter of Hamfast Gamgee and Bell Goodchild of Bagshot Row in Hobbiton . Her youngest brother was Samwise Gamgee . She also had three older siblings: Hamson , Halfred , and Daisy , and a younger sister Marigold . Nothing further is known about her.
