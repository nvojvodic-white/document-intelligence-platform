# Meleth of Gondolin

This article is about the Elf-maiden of Gondolin . For the Woman of Brethil , see Meleth . Meleth was a Elf-maiden of Gondolin in earlier versions of J.R.R. Tolkien 's legendarium . History Meleth was a nurse of Eärendil during his infancy in Gondolin. She told him many of stories, including some about Morgoth and his evil deeds. Thus, when Eärendil saw the lights of the enemy in his chamber during the Fall of Gondolin , he began to cry. Is unknown whether she died in the Fall or managed to survive and reach the Havens of Sirion . Etymology Meleth meant "love" in Noldorin .
