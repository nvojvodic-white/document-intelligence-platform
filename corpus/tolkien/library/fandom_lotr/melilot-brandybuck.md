# Melilot Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Melilot_Brandybuck

Melilot Brandybuck was a Hobbit of Buckland . Biography Melilot was born in SR 1385 (TA 2985) and was the youngest child of Marmadas Brandybuck . Melilot's siblings were called Merimas and Mentha . At Bilbo's 111th birthday party on September 22 , 3001 , Melilot Brandybuck her boyfriend Everard Took began to dance the Springle-ring on a table when they mistakenly thought Bilbo's speech was over. She was a third cousin of Meriadoc Brandybuck .
