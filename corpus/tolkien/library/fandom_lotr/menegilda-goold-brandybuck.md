# Menegilda (Goold) Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Menegilda_(Goold)_Brandybuck

Menegilda Goold was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Menegilda was the wife of Rorimac "Goldfather" Brandybuck , the sixth Master of Buckland , and the mother of Saradoc and Merimac . Little to nothing else is known of her life, save that she was a resident of Buckland and the grandmother of Meriadoc Brandybuck ; member of the Fellowship of the Ring .
