# Mentha Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Mentha_Brandybuck

Mentha Brandybuck was a young and beautiful Hobbit who lived in the country of Buckland , which was on the eastern border from the Shire during the Third Age . Biography Born in TA 2983 (SR 1383) in Buckland , Mentha was the second child of Marmadas Brandybuck and granddaughter of Gorbulas Brandybuck . Her older brother was Merimas and her younger sister was Melilot . In TA 3001 , Mentha was already 18 years old when she and her entire Brandybuck family attended Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party .
