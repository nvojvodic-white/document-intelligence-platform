# Merimac Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Merimac_Brandybuck

This article is about the hobbit of the Shire . For the The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power character, see Merimac . Merimac Brandybuck was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Merimac was born in TA 2942 (SR 1342) in Buckland to Rorimac Brandybuck and Menegilda Goold . He was their youngest son and child. His only sibling was an elder brother named Saradoc . He married an unnamed woman and had one child named Berilac . He died in FO 9 (SR 1430).
