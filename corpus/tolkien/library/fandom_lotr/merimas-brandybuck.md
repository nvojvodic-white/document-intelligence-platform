# Merimas Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Merimas_Brandybuck

Merimas Brandybuck was a Hobbit from Buckland . Biography Merimas was born to Marmadas Brandybuck on SR 1381 . His younger sisters were Mentha and Melilot . He was present during Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party . In adaptations In The Lord of the Rings Online , Merimas Brandybuck can be found in Buckland . He occupies the role of a crafting vendor in the game and plays no role in the storyline. References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C : Family Trees, Brandybuck of Buckland
