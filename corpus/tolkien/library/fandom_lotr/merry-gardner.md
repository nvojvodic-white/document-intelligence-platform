# Merry Gardner

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Merry_Gardner

Merry Gardner was the fourth child and second son of Samwise Gamgee and his wife, Rosie (Cotton) Gamgee . He had nine younger siblings. History His namesake came from Merry Brandybuck , Master of Buckland , who had been a member of the Fellowship of the Ring along with his father. He was one of the children Frodo had prophesized for Sam.
