# Milo Burrows

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Milo_Burrows

Milo Burrows was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Milo married into the Baggins family when he married a distant cousin of Bilbo and Frodo named Peony . Together they had four children, most of which were at Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party ; they were Mosco, Moro, Myrtle, and Minto. Milo was known for his habit of failing to reply to letters, and because of this he was one of those that found a specially marked gift, a golden inkpot and pen, waiting for him in Bag End , after Bilbo's departure. Appearance in adaptations Video games Milo Burrows was voiced by Michael Reisz in The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game) in 2002 .
