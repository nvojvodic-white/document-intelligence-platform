# Mimosa (Bunce) Baggins

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Mimosa_(Bunce)_Baggins

Mimosa (Bunce) Baggins was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Mimosa was born in the late Third Age to unnamed members of the Bunce family . She married Ponto Baggins I , with whom she had two children: Rosa , who was born in TA 2856 (SR 1256), and Polo , who was born a short time later. Etymology Her name was most likely based on a diverse family of plants . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings : Appendix C , "Baggins of Hobbiton"
