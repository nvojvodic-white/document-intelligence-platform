# Moro Burrows

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Moro_Burrows

Moro Burrows was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Moro was of the Burrows family and was born in SR 1391 . He was the son of Peony (Baggins) Burrows and Milo Burrows . His siblings were Mosco , Myrtle , and Minto Burrows . He was related to the Baggins through Peony and was thus a cousin of Frodo Baggins .
