# Mosco Burrows

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Mosco_Burrows

Mosco Burrows was a hobbit of the Shire . Mosco was born in TA 2987 (SR 1387). He was the eldest son of Milo Burrows and Peony Burrows and was known to have attended Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party as a young hobbit. References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C , "Baggins of Hobbiton"
