# Mrs. Bunce

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Mrs._Bunce

Mrs. Bunce was a hobbit woman in the folklore of the Shire-folk said to inhabit Michel Delving in The Adventures of Tom Bombadil and Other Verses from the Red Book . When she met the Lonely Troll , who wandered in Michel Delving, he greeted her, but she dropped her umbrella and basket and "yelled a frightful yell".
