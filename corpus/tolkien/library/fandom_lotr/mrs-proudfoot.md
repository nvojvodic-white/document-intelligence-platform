Mrs. Proudfoot was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography She married Odo Proudfoot at some point prior to TA 2946 , when their son Olo was born. Little to nothing else is known of her life, including when she died. In adaptations In Peter Jackson's film The Fellowship of the Ring , Mrs. Proudfoot has a non-speaking role, appearing only during the "Shire Introduction" to non-verbally chastise her husband for enjoying the firework-antics of the wizard Gandalf , perceived disturber of the peace.
