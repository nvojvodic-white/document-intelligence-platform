# Myrtle Burrows

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Myrtle_Burrows

Myrtle Burrows was a Hobbit of the Shire . History Myrtle was born in TA 2993 (SR 1393), the third child and only daughter of Milo Burrows and Peony Burrows . She attended Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party with her family. Etymology Like many female hobbits, Myrtle was named after a flower or a jewel.
