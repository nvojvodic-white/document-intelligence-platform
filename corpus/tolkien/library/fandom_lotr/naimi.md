# Naimi

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Naimi

! This content is considered pre-canon. While the subject of this article is based on official information, it was replaced, emended, or abandoned in later stages of the legendarium . ! Naimi or Nelmir , also known as Earissë , was an Elf of Tol Eressëa . Biography After Ælfwine drank the limpë , he returned young and became immortal and married Naimi. The couple had two sons: Heorrenda and Hendwine . Etymology The meaning of Naimi / Nelmir in Early Quenya is unclear. In other versions According to the versions of the legendarium, Naimi was alternatively the daughter or the niece of Vairë and Lindo .
