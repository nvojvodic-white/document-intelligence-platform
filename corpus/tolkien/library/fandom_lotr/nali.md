# Náli

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Náli

Náli was a Dwarf of Durin's Folk . Biography Náli was part of an expedition led by Balin to reclaim Moria for the Dwarves of Durin's Folk. He was joined by Flói , Frár , Óin , Ori , and Lóni . Five years afterward, in TA 2994 , members of the colony were attacked by Orcs while near Mirrormere and driven through the East-gate into Moria. Náli was killed in a battle while defending the Bridge of Khazad-dûm and the Second Hall alongside Lóni and Frár. His story is told in the Book of Mazarbul , which was found by Gandalf when he and the Fellowship passed through Moria during their travels. Portrayals in adaptations Video games Nali appeared in The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring video game. Different from novel, Nali in the game was a traveler, who came to West for business, and said to Frodo he would return to Erebor after his job concluded. His fate afterwards was unknown. References
