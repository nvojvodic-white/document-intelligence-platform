# Narthseg

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Narthseg

! This content is considered pre-canon. While the subject of this article is based on official information, it was replaced, emended, or abandoned in later stages of the legendarium . ! Narthseg was an Elf of the First Age notable for his betrayal of Artaner to the Dwarves of Nogrod . History Narthseg was an Elf of Artanor (later changed in Doriath ). During the battle in the Thousand Caves , Narthseg, just like Ufedhin , chose to betray his king revealing crucial information that help the treacherous Dwarves from Belegost and Nogrod to pass the Girdle of Melian and enter in Menegroth , order to seize the Nauglamír .
