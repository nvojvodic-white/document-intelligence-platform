# Narvi

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Narvi

Narvi (originally Ngarvi or Ñarvi ) was a Dwarf of Khazad-dûm (Moria) in the mid- Second Age . He was probably of Durin's Folk . Biography Narvi was best known for his part in the making of the Doors of Durin for the West-gate of Moria, on which Celebrimbor , an Elf , wrote the inscription. He worked closely with the Gwaith-i-Mírdain ; Celebrimbor in particular was his close friend. It can be implied that the relationship between Dwarves and Elves had not yet deteriorated. In adaptations In games In the game Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor , Narvi is mentioned as being the creator, or the one who commissioned, Celebrimbor's bow, Azkâr (non-canonical and created for the game). After the player completes enough missions to forge the legend of his weapons, the game's description of the bow reads: "Azkâr. Narvi had me made for the ring-smith and for his loyal service. His arrows truly may they hit their mark [sic]." The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power Narvi appears in the second season of The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power , which marks the character's first on-screen depiction. The character first appears in the episode " Where the Stars are Strange ". He is portrayed by English actor Kevin Eldon . In the series, Narvi was not only a great smith and craftsman, but Durin III 's chief advisor and loyal servant. He was also the delvemaster of Khazad-dûm . He was the friend of Durin IV and was always close to the royal family. When Mount Doom erupted, a great earthquake spread over Middle-earth and came to Khazad-dûm. The light shafts of the mountain collapsed and the realm was surrounded in darkness. Narvi was given the task to find the solution for the problem and suggested the stone singers of the mountain, which does not work. When Durin III obtained his ring of power , the relationship with the elves was reborn and the light shafts were remade. This lead Narvi to work closer with the elves, mainly Celebrimbor . They built the Doors of Durin and the famous gateway into Moria was unveiled in Ost-in-Edhil . Narvi then returned to Khazad-dûm , there things had changed. The king's mind had been filled with greed due to his ring and invented and new law called "ring tribute". He ordered Narvi to dig deeper into the mines for more Gold and Mithril , despite Durin IV 's attempt to call it off due to Disa 's encounter with the Balrog . Narvi listened to the king and ordered his miners to dig deeper, however they could not pursue this as Durin IV and Disa block the entry to the mines. Narvi goes back to the king that has now been completely infected with greed, he then understands that he was losing his mind and disobeyed him by ignoring his instructions down in the mine, now standing with Durin and Disa. Durin III grew impatient and decided to go down himself, bringing his axe with him. He forced himself through Narvi and his miners and passed the gates into the mine. As Durin IV was about to send himself and a great host of dwarves to help the elves in the Siege of Eregion , Narvi told him to recall the army, and go into the mine. While Durin III and Durin IV would interrogate each other, Narvi stayed behind. The king later freed himself from his greed when the Balrog appeared, sacrificing himself to save the realm and his son. Narvi would remain with Durin IV after the death of the king, and told him that Durin's brother has gathered support about becoming the next king .
