# Naugladur

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Naugladur

! This content is considered pre-canon. While the subject of this article is based on official information, it was replaced, emended, or abandoned in later stages of the legendarium . ! Naugladur was the Lord of Nogrod ; the King of the Wicked dwarves of Nogrod in the earlier forms of the legendarium . Biography Naugladur, after hearing of his subject's maltreatment at the hands of Tinweilant (Thingol), hired a large band of mercenary Orcs to take Doriath , and kill Tinweilant and take the Nauglafring . After destroying Doriath and killing Tinweilant, he left, along with a caravan containing most of Tinweilant's gold and treasure. But while he was crossing the Aros river, he was waylaid by a company of Elves led by Beren . Beren and the Elves killed almost all of the Dwarves, and the rest were fleeing; but Beren challenged Naugladur to single combat. The dwarf was a formidable foe, and so the fighting lasted for nigh three hours; until Naugladur struck his foot on a rock, and Beren grabbed his enemy's sword and stabbed him with it.
