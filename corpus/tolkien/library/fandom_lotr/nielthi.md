# Nielthi

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Nielthi

Nielthi was an Elf -maiden of the First Age , who lived in Doriath . History Nielthi was an handmaid of Melian , Queen of Doriath. When the the treacherous Dwarves from Belegost and Nogrod launched their attack to Doriath , order to seize the Nauglamír , Nielthi encouraged the Queen by praising her magic that protected the realm. However, because the battle was lost by the Elves , she was forced to leave the realm. Is unknown whether she died in the flight or managed to survive.
