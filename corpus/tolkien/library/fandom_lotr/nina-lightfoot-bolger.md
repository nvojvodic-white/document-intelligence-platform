# Nina (Lightfoot) Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Nina_(Lightfoot)_Bolger

Nina Lightfoot was a hobbit of the Shire . She was the wife of Theobald Bolger and the mother of Wilibald Bolger .
