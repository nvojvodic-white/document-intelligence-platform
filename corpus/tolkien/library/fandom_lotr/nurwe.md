# Nurwë

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Nurwë

Nurwë was an Elf from Cuiviénen who refused the summons of the Valar . When the three ambassadors of the Elves, Ingwë , Finwë , and Elwë , returned from Aman , they invited the other Elves to travel there. However, Nurwë and Morwë refused, thus leading the Avari left behind. Etymology The Quenya term nur or nura means ("to separate") and -wë may have come from -wego which is frequent in masculine names.
