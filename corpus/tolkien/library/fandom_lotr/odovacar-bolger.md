# Odovacar Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Odovacar_Bolger

Odovacar Bolger was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Odovacar was the son of Herugar Bolger and Jessamine Boffin . After his father's death, he became the head of the Bolger family. He was the husband of Rosamunda Took and father of Fredegar Bolger and Estella . He died at the age of 106. In adaptations In The Lord of the Rings Online , Odovacar Bolger is a pig farmer living in Budgeford . He asks the player to investigate the rumour of a Black Rider seen at his farm at night, but it turns out to be simply another hobbit playing panks.
