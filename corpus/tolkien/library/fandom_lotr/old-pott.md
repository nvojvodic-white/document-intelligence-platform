# Old Pott

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Old_Pott

Old Pott was a character of Hobbit -folklore who was said to have lived in the Shire as a resident of Michel Delving in Samwise Gamgee 's poem Perry-the-Winkle . The Old Pott was Mayor of Michel Delving when the Lonely Troll came in town. He witnessed the meeting of Mrs. Bunce with the Lonely Troll and he was scared too and flee before the troll . However, then he learned that the Troll was kind and generous, and with other Hobbits tried to visit him, but the Troll refused those who had refused his friendship before.
