# Olo Proudfoot

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Olo_Proudfoot

Olo Proudfoot was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Olo was born in TA 2946 (SR 1346) to Odo Proudfoot and his unnamed wife . He later married and had a son Sancho with his wife, also unnamed. Both he and his son attended Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party . He died in FO 14 (SR 1435). In adaptations In The Lord of the Rings Online , Olo Proudfoot owns a farm between Hobbiton and Bywater . He occupies the role of a crafting vendor in the game and plays no role in the storyline. References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C , "Baggins of Hobbiton"
