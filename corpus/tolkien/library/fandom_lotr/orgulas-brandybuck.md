# Orgulas Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Orgulas_Brandybuck

Orgulas Brandybuck was a hobbit of the Shire . Orgulas was born in TA 2868 (SR 1268) to Marmadoc Brandybuck the "Masterful" and his wife Adaldrida Bolger in Buckland . He married and had one son, Gorbulas to an unnamed wife.
