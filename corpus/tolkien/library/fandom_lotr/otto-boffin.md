# Otto Boffin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Otto_Boffin

Otto Boffin , also known as Otto the Fat , was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Otto was the only child of Bosco Boffin and his unnamed wife. He married Lavender Grubb and they had four children: Hugo , Uffo , Rollo , and Primrose Boffin . He died in TA 2900 (SR 1300) at the age of eighty-eight.
