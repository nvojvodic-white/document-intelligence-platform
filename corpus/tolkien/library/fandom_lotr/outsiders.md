# Outsiders

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Outsiders

Outsiders was a term used by the Shire-folk to refer to all Hobbits who lived outside the Shire , including Bree-hobbits . The Shire-folk considered all Outsiders to be dull and uncouth, taking little interest in them. Outsiders were scattered across the West-lands and significantly more numerous than the people of the Shire realized. Some Outsiders were wanderers, who didn't settle for long periods of time in any particular place, instead digging holes in banks and staying there only as long as it suited them, while other Outsiders, like the inhabitants of Bree-land , were as settled, prosperous, and civilized as their distant relatives living within the Shire. In a more general sense, the term Outsiders was used by the Shire-folk to describe all people living outside the Shire. The Bounders guarded the borders of the Shire to prevent any nuisance from Outsiders of any kind, great or small. On the other hand, Bree-landers used the term Outsiders to describe all people living outside Bree-land, including hobbits of the Shire. In other versions In early drafts of The Lord of the Rings , J.R.R. Tolkien used the term "wild hobbits" to describe Hobbits who lived lonely here and there in Eriador outside of the Shire and Bree and even on the eaves of Mirkwood .
