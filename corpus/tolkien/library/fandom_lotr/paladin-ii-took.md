# Paladin II Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Paladin_II_Took

Paladin II Took was a Hobbit of the Shire and the thirty-first Shire-thain . He was the father of the famous hobbit Peregrin Took . Biography Paladin was the only son of Adalgrim Took and his unnamed wife and the brother of Esmeralda (Took) Brandybuck . Paladin married Eglantine Banks , and had four children: Pearl Took , Pimpernel Took , Pervinca Took , and Peregrin Took. Paladin lived near Whitwell , near Tuckborough , where he owned a farm. Paladin succeeded his childless second cousin Ferumbras III Took as Shire-thain in TA 3015 . When Saruman took control of the Shire through Lotho , he resisted and organized a defense against the Chief's men . Paladin Took II died in FO 13 , at the age of one-hundred and one. In adaptations Video games Paladin II Took is featured in The Lord of the Rings Online . He also appeared as a non-playable character in LEGO The Lord of the Rings: The Video Game , only in the portable versions. Middle-earth Strategy Battle Game Paladin Took, Thain of the Shire, appears in the Middle-earth Strategy Battle Game . He was first introduced in the 2005 supplement The Scouring of the Shire .
