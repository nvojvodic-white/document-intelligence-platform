# Pansy (Baggins) Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Pansy_(Baggins)_Bolger

Pansy Baggins (b. TA 2812 or SR 1212) was the daughter of Balbo Baggins and Berylla Boffin , and the great-aunt of Bilbo Baggins . Biography She was the second oldest of the four other children of Balbo and Berylla, the others being Mungo (the oldest), Ponto , Largo , and Lily (the youngest). She married Fastolph Bolger but they had no children. References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C
