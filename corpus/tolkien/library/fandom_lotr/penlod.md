# Penlod

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Penlod

Penlod was an Elf -lord of Gondolin in the First Age . Biography Penlod was the marshall of two Gondolin houses, the Tower of Snow and the Pillar , and was the tallest of the Gondolindrim . He is not mentioned in the Silmarillion chapter concerning Gondolin's fall; rather, he appears only in The Book of Lost Tales Part Two . During the Fall of Gondolin , the Elves of the city suffered a hard attack from the enemy after the death of Rog and the House of the Hammer of Wrath . Having to retreat further back into the city, Penlod perished "in a lane with his back to the wall", and with him many of his men. Etymology Penlod is clearly Gnomish , but it is not glossed. The Qenya cognate was Pendelot .
