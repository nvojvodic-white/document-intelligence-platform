# Peony (Baggins) Burrows

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Peony_(Baggins)_Burrows

Peony Burrows was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Peony was the daughter of Posco Baggins and Gilly Brownlock Baggins. Peony was born in year 2950 of the Third Age . Peony had two older brothers named Ponto and Porto . She married Milo Burrows and the two had four children: Mosco , Moro , Myrtle , and Minto . The entire family, with the exception of Minto, attended Bilbo Baggins 's Farewell Party held in TA 3001 . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings : Appendix C , "Baggins of Hobbiton"
