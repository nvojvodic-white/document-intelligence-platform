# Pervinca Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Pervinca_Took

Pervinca Took was a sister of Peregrin Took . Biography Born in TA 2985 (SR 1385), she was the third daughter of Paladin II Took and Eglantine Banks , and the sister closest in age to Peregrin Took . She attended Bilbo Baggins ' Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party . In adaptations Pervinca Took is characterized in The Lord of the Rings Online .
