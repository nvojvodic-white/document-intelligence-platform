# Pimpernel Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Pimpernel_Took

Pimpernel Took was the second daughter of Paladin Took and his wife Eglantine Banks ; thus a sister to Peregrin Took . She was a guest at Bilbo Baggins' farewell party . Etymology Pimpernel is a genus consisting of about 25 flowering plants. Its Latin root is pimpernella which means "little pepper".
