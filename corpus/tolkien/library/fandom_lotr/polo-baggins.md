# Polo Baggins

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Polo_Baggins

Polo Baggins was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Polo was born sometime after TA 2856 (SR 1256) and was the second child and only son of Ponto Baggins I and Mimosa Bunce . His sister was Rosa . Polo later married an unnamed woman and had two children; Posco and Prisca . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C , "Baggins of Hobbiton"
