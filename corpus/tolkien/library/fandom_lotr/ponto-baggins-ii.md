# Ponto Baggins II

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Ponto_Baggins_II

This article is about the the son of Posco Baggins . For the the son of Balbo Baggins , see Ponto Baggins . Ponto Baggins II was a Hobbit of The Shire . Biography Ponto was born to Posco Baggins and Gilly Brownlock in Bywater . He was named after his great-grandfather, Ponto . He had a brother named Porto Baggins and a sister named Peony Baggins . He had a daughter, Angelica Baggins , with an unarmed wife. Ponto Baggins II attended Bilbo Baggins 's Farewell Birthday Party with his family on September 22 of the year 3001 . In the first year of the Fourth Age , Ponto Baggins II presumably became the head of the Baggins family after Frodo Baggins left The Shire in the last year of the Third Age . In adaptations The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game) In the 2002 video game , Ponto Baggins lives on Number 2, Bywater Road , the first house in Bywater . References
