# Ponto Baggins

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Ponto_Baggins

This article is about the the son of Balbo Baggins . For the the son of Posco Baggins , see Ponto Baggins II . Ponto Baggins was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Ponto was the third son of Balbo and Berylla Boffin . His siblings were Mungo , Pansy , Largo , and Lily . He was most likely born in Hobbiton (the usual birthplace of the early Baggins family) between TA 2812 and TA 2820 . He married Mimosa Bunce and they had two children: Rosa and Polo . Ponto died in TA 2911 . After his death, the Baggins family started to grow in size. References
