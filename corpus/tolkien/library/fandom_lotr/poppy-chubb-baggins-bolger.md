# Poppy (Chubb-Baggins) Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Poppy_(Chubb-Baggins)_Bolger

Poppy (née Chubb-Baggins) Bolger was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography She was born in TA 2944 (SR 1344) to Falco Chubb-Baggins and his unnamed wife, and eventually married Filibert Bolger. She and her husband were present at Bilbo 's Farewell Party in TA 3001 . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings : Appendix C , "Baggins of Hobbiton" pg. 1100
