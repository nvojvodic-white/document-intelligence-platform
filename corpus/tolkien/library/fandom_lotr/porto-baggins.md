# Porto Baggins

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Porto_Baggins

Porto Baggins was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Porto was born to Posco Baggins and Gilly Brownlock Baggins. He had an older brother named Ponto Baggins and a younger sister named Peony Baggins . Porto attended Bilbo Baggins 's Farewell Party in 3001 . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C : Family Trees, Baggins of Hobbiton
