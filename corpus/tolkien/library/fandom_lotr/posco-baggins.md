# Posco Baggins

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Posco_Baggins

Posco Baggins was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography He was born in year 2902 of the Third Age to Polo Baggins and his unnamed wife. Posco eventually married Gilly Brownlock , with whom he had three children: Ponto , Porto , and Peony . It is not known if Posco lived until 3001 to attend Bilbo Baggins 's Farewell Party along with his wife and children or whether he died prior to this event. He presumably died before 3021 , when his son Ponto Baggins II became head of the Baggins Family . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C , "Baggins of Hobbiton"
