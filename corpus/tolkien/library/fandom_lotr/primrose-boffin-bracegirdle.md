# Primrose (Boffin) Bracegirdle

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Primrose_(Boffin)_Bracegirdle

Primrose Bracegirdle was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Primrose was born into the Boffin family and was the fourth child and only daughter of Otto and Lavender Grubb . She had three siblings: Hugo , Uffo , and Rollo . She married Blanco Bracegirdle , and they had two children: Bruno and Lobelia .
