# Primrose Gardner

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Primrose_Gardner

Primrose Gardner was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Primrose Gardner born in FO 14 (SR 1435) and was the ninth child and fifth daughter of Samwise Gamgee and his wife Rosie Cotton . She had four younger siblings. She was the second child of Sam's not to have a namesake (her sister Elanor being the first). Etymology Primrose is the name of a flower native to western and southern Europe. Naming her 'Primrose' was in keeping with the tradition of hobbits to name the females after flowers.
