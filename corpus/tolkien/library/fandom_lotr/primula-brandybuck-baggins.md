# Primula (Brandybuck) Baggins

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Primula_(Brandybuck)_Baggins

Primula Baggins (née Brandybuck ) was a Hobbit of the Shire , the mother of Frodo Baggins , who was one of the many bearers of the Ring , and the cousin of Bilbo Baggins . Biography Primula was a daughter of Gorbadoc Brandybuck , Master of Buckland , and Mirabella Took , the youngest daughter of the Old Took. She married Drogo Baggins , and had one child, Frodo. In TA 2980 (SR 1380), she and her husband were drowned in the Brandywine River , leaving Frodo an orphaned child. She was sixty years old. Etymology Primula is a species of the Primrose family of flowering plants.
