# Prisca (Baggins) Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Prisca_(Baggins)_Bolger

Prisca (Baggins) Bolger was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Prisca was born in TA 2906 (SR 1306) to Polo Baggins and his unnamed wife. She was the sister of Posco Baggins . She married Wilibald Bolger and they had three children; Wilimar , born in TA 2947 (SR 1347), Heribald , born in TA 2951 (SR 1351), and Nora , born in TA 2960 (SR 1360).
