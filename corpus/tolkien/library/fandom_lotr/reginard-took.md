# Reginard Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Reginard_Took

Reginard Took was a hobbit of the Shire , belonging to the Took clan . Biography Reginard was born in TA 2969 (SR 1369) to Adelard Took and his (unknown) wife. He had a younger brother named Everard . He attended Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party , held in September of TA 3001 , with his family. Nothing further of his life is known.
