# Robin Gardner

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Robin_Gardner

Robin Gardner was a Hobbit of the Shire . Born in FO 19 ( SR 1440 ), he was the twelfth child and sixth son of Samwise Gamgee and his beloved wife, Rosie (née Cotton) Gamgee . He had one younger brother . Robin's namesake appears in the third volume of The Lord of the Rings : The Return of the King . The original Robin, Robin Smallburrow was a good friend of Sam's, and was a Shirriff before, during, and after Sharkey 's reign in the Shire . The original Robin liked pubs. He had most likely fought with the Hobbits at the Battle of Bywater . Frodo did not foresee Robin Gamgee to Sam.
