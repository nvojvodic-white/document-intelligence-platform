# Rog

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Rog

Rog was an Elf -lord from the hidden city-realm of Gondolin during the First Age . Biography Rog was the Lord of the House of the Hammer of Wrath and the chief blacksmith in Gondolin. He was the strongest of the Gondolindrim , and the third greatest in valour. He died during the Fall of Gondolin , after putting up a valiant fight.
