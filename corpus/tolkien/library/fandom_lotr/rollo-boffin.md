# Rollo Boffin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Rollo_Boffin

Rollo Boffin was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Rollo was born to Otto Boffin and Lavender Grubb . He had an older brother named Hugo Boffin as well as another older brother named Uffo Boffin . He had a younger sister named Primrose , who was the mother of Lobelia Sackville-Baggins . He married Druda (Burrows) Boffin , with whom he had an unknown number of children.
