# Rosamunda (Took) Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Rosamunda_(Took)_Bolger

Rosamunda Bolger (née Took ) was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Rosamunda was born in TA 2938 (SR 1338) to Sigismond Took . Rosamunda Took had a younger brother named Ferdinand Took . Her mother's name is unknown. Rosamunda married into the Bolger family when she wedded Odovacar Bolger and the two had Fredegar Bolger and Estella Brandybuck . They moved to Budgeford in Bridgefields in the Eastfarthing , later to attend Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party of September 22 , 3001 .
