Rose Gardner , also known as Rosie-lass [ citation needed ] , was a Hobbit from the Shire and the third child of Samwise Gamgee and Rosie Cotton . Biography Rose was born in SR 1425 and had twelve siblings; Elanor and Frodo being her seniors, and Merry , Pippin , Goldilocks , Hamfast , Daisy , Primrose , Bilbo , Rudy , Robin , and Tolman her juniors. She was one of the children Frodo Baggins prophesied for her father, Sam, and was named after her mother at Frodo's suggestion.
