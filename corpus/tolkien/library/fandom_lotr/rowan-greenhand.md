# Rowan Greenhand

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Rowan_Greenhand

Rowan Greenhand was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Rowan was the first and oldest child of Holman the Greenhanded of Hobbiton . She had four younger siblings Halfred , Erling , Hending , and Rose . She married Hob Gammidge of Tighfield and had one son named Hobson , or Roper, Gamgee in TA 2885 (SR 1285).
