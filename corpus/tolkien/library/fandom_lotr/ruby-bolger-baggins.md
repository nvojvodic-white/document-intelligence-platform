# Ruby (Bolger) Baggins

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Ruby_(Bolger)_Baggins

Ruby Bolger (b. TA 2864 (SR 1264)) was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Ruby was married to Fosco Baggins , with whom she had three children: Dora , Drogo , and Dudo . She was Frodo's paternal grandmother. References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C , "Bolger of Budgeford" ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C, "Baggins of Hobbiton"
