# Ruby Gardner

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Ruby_Gardner

Ruby Gardner , born in SR 1438 , was the eleventh child and sixth and last daughter of Samwise Gamgee and his wife, Rosie (née Cotton) Gamgee . She had two younger brothers: Robin and Tolman . She was not foreseen by Frodo before his departure from Middle-earth .
