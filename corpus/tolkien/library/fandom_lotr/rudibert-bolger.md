# Rudibert Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Rudibert_Bolger

Rudibert Bolger was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Rudibert was born in TA 2860 (SR 1260), the second son of Adalgar Bolger and his unnamed wife. He married Amethyst Hornblower and had one child, a son named Adalbert Bolger .
