# Rudigar Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Rudigar_Bolger

Rudigar Bolger was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Rudigar Bolger was the son of Adalgar Bolger and the husband of Belba Baggins . He became the head of the Bolger family as the eldest heir. He died in SR 1348 at the high age of 93.
He had a son, Herugar . Etymology The name Rudigar contains hrod , ‘fame’, and gar , “spear”. It is the Middle High German form of Old English Hrothgar and of the modern name Roger. References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C : Family Trees, Bolger of Budgeford
