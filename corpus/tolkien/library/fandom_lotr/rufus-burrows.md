# Rufus Burrows

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Rufus_Burrows

Rufus Burrows was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Rufus married Asphodel of the Brandybuck family and had one son with her, Milo Burrows , who was born in TA 2947 (SR 1347).
