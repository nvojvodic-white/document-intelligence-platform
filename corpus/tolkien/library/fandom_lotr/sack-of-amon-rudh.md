# Sack of Amon Rûdh

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Sack_of_Amon_Rûdh

The Sack of Amon Rûdh was a minor battle of the First Age and the War of the Great Jewels . History The Sack was caused by Mîm , the Petty-dwarf who betrayed Túrin 's friend and allies to the Orcs of Morgoth . The battle took place in FA 489 in the House of Ransom . The sack marked the end of Dor-Cúarthol , the "Land of Bow and Helm" and the near capture of Túrin and Beleg by the forces of Morgoth who able to escape due to the bravery of Andróg , who later died of an arrow -wound.
