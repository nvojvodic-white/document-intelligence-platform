# Sadoc Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Sadoc_Brandybuck

Sadoc Brandybuck was a Hobbit from Buckland . Biography Sadoc was the second son of Gormadoc Brandybuck , the Master of Buckland , and Malva Headstrong . His brothers were Madoc and Marroc . Sadoc had three children: two unnamed sons and a daughter Salvia Bolger .
