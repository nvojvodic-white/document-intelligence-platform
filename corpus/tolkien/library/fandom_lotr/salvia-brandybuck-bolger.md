# Salvia (Brandybuck) Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Salvia_(Brandybuck)_Bolger

Salvia Bolger (née Brandybuck ) was a hobbit of the Shire . Born in TA 2826 (SR 1226), she was the third child and only daughter of Sadoc Brandybuck . She married Gundabald Bolger and they had one child, Theobald Bolger .
