# Sandyman

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Sandyman

Sandyman was a hobbit of the Shire , and the miller of Hobbiton . Biography Sandyman was born sometime in the late Third Age . He became the owner of the Old Mill in Hobbiton sometime before TA 3001 (SR 1401). He was the father to Ted Sandyman . His fist name was never told in the story. He was always ready to pass on tall tales and was an opinionated fellow who was not well liked by Samwise Gamgee 's father Hamfast Gamgee . He was present in the Ivy Bush drinking at table with The Gaffer and discussing happenings and rumors in the Shire, just before Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party . It seems that he died sometime in the years before the War of the Ring , with his Mill being inherited by Ted, who sold it to Lotho Sackville-Baggins during his takeover of the Shire. In adaptations Video games In The Hobbit: The Prelude to The Lord of the Rings , Sandyman was given the full name of "Carl Sandyman" and was the son of the miller in the game. He held his father's hammer and nails which Bilbo had to get before the bridge over The Water could be repaired. In Tales of the Shire: A The Lord of the Rings Game (2025), Sandyman is the only miller in the area and is notorious for high prices for all mill products. The Sandymans and Cottons have been feuding for generations and the same is true between him and Farmer Tom Cotton .
