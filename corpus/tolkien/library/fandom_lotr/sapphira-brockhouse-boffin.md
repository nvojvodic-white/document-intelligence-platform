# Sapphira (Brockhouse) Boffin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Sapphira_(Brockhouse)_Boffin

Sapphira Boffin , formerly Sapphira Brockhouse , was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Nothing of her early life is known. She eventually would marry Uffo Boffin , to whom she would bear two children, a son and daughter they named Gruffo and Gerda . Behind the scenes The name Sapphira was probably derived from Sapphire , a blue gemstone of worth. References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C , "Boffin of the Yale"
