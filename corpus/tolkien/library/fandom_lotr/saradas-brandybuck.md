# Saradas Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Saradas_Brandybuck

Saradas Brandybuck was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Saradas was the third son of Gorbadoc Brandybuck and Mirabella Took . His siblings were Rorimac (who was the grandfather of Merry ), Amaranth , Dodinas , Asphodel , Dinodas , and Primula (who was the mother of Frodo Baggins ). He was born on SR 1308 and had one son called Seredic Brandybuck , born on SR 1348 . Saradas was present on Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party and died on SR 1407 ( TA 3007 ) at the age of 99.
