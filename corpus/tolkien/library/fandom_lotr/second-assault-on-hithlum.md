Morgoth's assaults on Hithlum was were several military offensives launched by Morgoth during the First Age , within the context of the War of the Jewels . The second assault on Hithlum occurred in FA 462 , seven years after the devastating Dagor Bragollach . The battle took place in Hithlum , a region west of Angband , bordered by the Ered Wethrin . Morgoth launched a two-pronged attack. One force besieged the passes of the Ered Wethrin, targeting the fortress of Eithel Sirion . Another force came from the north, crossing icy terrain to attack Hithlum from an unexpected direction. Galdor of Dor-lómin , a leader of the Edain , was killed during the siege. His son, Húrin , took command and led a successful counterattack, driving the Orcs back across the Anfauglith (formerly Ard-galen). Fingon , High King of the Noldor , was initially overwhelmed but received crucial reinforcements from Círdan the Shipwright, who sent a force up the Firth of Drengist to flank the Orcs. The combined forces of Elves and Men broke the Orc-host, which fled back toward the Iron Mountains . Though a victory for the Free Peoples , the battle revealed Morgoth’s growing power and the increasing difficulty of defending Beleriand . This assault demonstrated Morgoth’s evolving tactics and the increasing reliance on surprise and multi-front attacks. It also highlighted the valor and leadership of figures like Húrin and Fingon, and the importance of alliances, such as that with Círdan.
