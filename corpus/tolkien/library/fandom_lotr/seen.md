# Seen

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Seen

"... so small a thing. Such a little thing!" This article is a stub ; please expand it if you can. The Seen was a term referring to all normal living creatures and material objects as opposed to appearing in the Unseen or wraith-world. All things in Eä either counted as a part of the Seen world or the Unseen world. Some beings could exist in both, such as the Ainur and the Calaquendi , the Elves who dwelt in Valinor . All descriptions of things were part of the Seen unless mentioned as part of the Unseen. References
