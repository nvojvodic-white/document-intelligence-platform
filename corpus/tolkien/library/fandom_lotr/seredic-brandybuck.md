# Seredic Brandybuck

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Seredic_Brandybuck

Seredic Brandybuck was a Hobbit of Buckland . Biography Seredic was born in TA 2948 (1348 by Shire Reckoning) to Saradas Brandybuck and his unnamed wife. He married Hilda Bracegirdle and they had three children together: Doderic , Ilberic , and Celandine . He attended Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party .
