# Seven Hoards of the Dwarf-kings

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Seven_Hoards_of_the_Dwarf-kings

"... so small a thing. Such a little thing!" This article is a stub ; please expand it if you can. The Seven Hoards of the Dwarf-kings were legendary treasures of the Dwarves . The name suggests that each hoard belonged to one of the Dwarvish clans . As it is said, the foundation of each was a golden ring, and probably they were gathered thanks to the Seven Rings given to the Dwarves by Sauron in the Second Age . By the Third Age , those hoards were plundered and devoured by Dragons . Smaug had once claimed a dwarven-hoard, but it is not known if it was one of the Seven Hoards. References
