# Sigismond Took

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Sigismond_Took

Sigismond Took was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Sigismond was born in TA 2890 (SR 1290). He was the son of Hildibrand Took . He had two children, Rosamunda and Ferdinand to an unnamed wife. He died in TA 2981 (SR 1381).
