# Southerners

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Southerners

The Southerners were an ill-defined group of people, generally referred to as such by Bree-landers . These people came up from the Greenway into Bree out of lands to the south around Dunland . Most of them had come up into the Bree-lands looking for a place of peace from the unrest in their homes, though there were some untrustworthy folk amongst them, such as the squint-eyed Southerner , who served Saruman as spies. It is possible that many of them were Dunlendings . Other writings The End of Bovadium In the Foreword of The End of Bovadium , Doctor Sarevelk labeled the Vastians who dwelt south of the Via Maxima in Bovadium as A-people since they were the speakers of language A. In Fragment II of the text, the A-people are referred to as Southerners . During the takeover of the city by the Motores , the Southerners were not as affected because their lands were "guarded by great gates and iron bars" that the Motores could not pass. As a consequence, they were still able to enjoy a peace free from the Motores . However, after the Northerners became jealous of them and proposed to the Minister that a new road should be built "through the southern meadows", the Southerners distracted the Minister with their own proposals. But this was futile since the Minister choose not to do anything proposed by the Southerners or the Northerners and would have proceeded with his own plan had he not gotten trapped in the Capital City. The debate of the Southerners and the Northerners never reached a resolution because they were all "slain by the poisonous fumes" of the Motores.
