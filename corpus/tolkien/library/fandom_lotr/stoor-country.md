# Stoor-country

Please expand it if you can. The Stoor-country refers to the regions where the Stoors originally settled. They first lived in the Vales of Anduin , near the Gladden Fields . Later, some of them migrated westward into Eriador and settled in the area that would become the Shire , particularly in the Marish region of the Eastfarthing . Later still, some Stoors moved back to the Stoor-country. These Stoors made boats of reeds and were the ancestors of Sméagol and Déagol , who both lived in the Stoor-country. References
