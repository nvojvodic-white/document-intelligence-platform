# Tanta (Hornblower) Baggins

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Tanta_(Hornblower)_Baggins

This article refers to the wife of Largo Baggins . For other namesakes, see Horn (disambiguation) . Tanta Hornblower was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Tanta Baggins was born around TA 2820 (SR 1220) and was the wife of Largo Baggins . She was the mother of Fosco Baggins and the mother-in law of Ruby (Bolger) Baggins .
