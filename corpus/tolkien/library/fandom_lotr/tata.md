# Tata

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Tata

Tata was an Elf of Cuiviénen , where he was the second Elf to be awakened, during the Years of the Trees . Biography Tata was awakened by Imin , who woke him after he himself was awoken by Ilúvatar . He awoke a group of Elves who took him as their lord. Tata's fate is unknown, but it is presumed that he lived immortally. His folk later became the Ñoldor . His wife was Tatië .
