# Tolman Gardner

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Tolman_Gardner

Tolman Gamgee was a Hobbit of the Shire . Born in FO 21 ( SR 1442 ), he was the seventh son and thirteenth and final child of Samwise Gamgee and his wife, Rosie (née Cotton) Gamgee . His eldest sibling, Elanor , was twenty-one years his senior. There were two other family members with whom Tolman shared his name. First was his uncle Tolman Cotton Jr. , and then his maternal grandfather Tolman Cotton . Both them fought in the Battle of Bywater . His grandfather Tolman was known as Farmer Cotton, and was a great leader for the lower class of the Shire . [ citation needed ] Frodo did not foresee Tolman to Sam.
