# Tosto Boffin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Tosto_Boffin

Tosto Boffin was a hobbit of the Shire . He was born to Griffo Boffin and Daisy (Baggins) Boffin . In TA 3001 (SR 1401), he was present at Bilbo's Farewell Birthday Party . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , Appendix C , "Boffin of the Yale"
