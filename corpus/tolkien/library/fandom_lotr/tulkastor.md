# Tulkastor

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Tulkastor

Tulkastor was an Elf of Tol Eressëa . Biography Tulkastor was a follower of the Vala Aulë . He dwelt long with the Teleri in the early days of the Elves on the island. He married an unnamed woman of unknown kindred and their child was Vairë , wife of Lindo , who was the caretaker of the Cottage of Lost Play . Etymology In the development of tales, Tulkastor's name was originally Turenbor , and then Tulkassë .
