# Turambar's defeat of the Easterlings

Turambar's defeat of the Easterlings was a battle or perhaps a series of battles in which King Turambar of Gondor decisively defeated the Easterling invaders. History On his accession to the throne of Gondor Turambar moved quickly against the Easterlings who had slain his father and invaded his people's lands. Gondor and the Easterling armies clashed somewhere, perhaps to the north of Mordor, and Gondor emerged victorious. Aftermath After the victory, the armies of Gondor moved eastward into Rhûn and proceeded to conquer the area which would become part of Gondor.
