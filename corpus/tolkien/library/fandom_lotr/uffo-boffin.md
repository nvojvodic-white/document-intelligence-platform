# Uffo Boffin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Uffo_Boffin

Uffo Boffin was a hobbit of the Shire . History Uffo was born in TA 2857 (SR 1257). He was the second son of Otto Boffin and Lavender Grubb . He married Sapphira Brockhouse and they had two children, Gruffo and Gerda .
