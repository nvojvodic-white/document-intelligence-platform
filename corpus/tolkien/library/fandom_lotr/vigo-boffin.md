# Vigo Boffin

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Vigo_Boffin

Vigo Boffin was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Vigo was born in TA 2937 (SR 1337) and was the only son of Jago Boffin and his unnamed wife. He married an unnamed woman and had a son named Folco Boffin , who would later help Frodo move to his new home in Crickhollow in Buckland . He died in FO 9 (SR 1430).
