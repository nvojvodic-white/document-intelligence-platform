# Voronwë

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Voronwë

This article is about the Ñoldo . For the other namesakes, see Voronwë (disambiguation) . Voronwë , also called Aranwion , was a Ñoldorin Elf mariner from Gondolin who was one of a crew of several ships sent by Turgon to solicit aid from the Valar . The Doom of Mandos , however, made any such attempt futile, and Voronwë was the only one to survive. Biography Voronwë was relatively young according to the measure of the Elves, having been born in Middle-earth (and specifically in Nevrast ) instead of Valinor . His father was Aranwë , a Ñoldo, but his mother was of the Sindarin Elves of the Falas , and kinswoman of Círdan . He called himself "of the House of Fingolfin ", which in this case means a follower of that house, rather than a relationship of blood. Voronwë was saved by Ulmo with the express purpose of leading Tuor to Gondolin , which he did. During the Fall of Gondolin , Voronwë was asked by Tuor to guard Idril . He escaped the sack, following Tuor and Idril through the secret way . He led them until Sirion and they did not continue, for Voronwë did not know the regions beyond the river. It was said that Voronwë sailed west with Eärendil in Vingilot . Etymology His name is Quenya in origin. Voronwë means "the Steadfast", "the Faithful", or "the Constant", and his surname Aranwion means "son of Aranwë". The name Voronwë also appears as an epessë (honourary title) of various other characters; for example, Mardil Voronwë . He called himself Bronweg , the Sindarin version of his name. It also means "one of steadfastness". Other versions In writings given in The Book of Lost Tales , Bronweg was the father of Ilfrin , gong-warden of Tol Eressëa .
