# Widow Rumble

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Widow_Rumble

Widow Rumble was a servant, or nurse, to Hamfast Gamgee during his later years following the War of the Ring , at which time he lived in number three Bagshot Row . It was also at this time that Samwise moved into Bag End .
