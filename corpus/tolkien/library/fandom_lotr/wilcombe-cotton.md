# Wilcombe Cotton

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Wilcombe_Cotton

Wilcombe Cotton , more commonly known as " Will ", was a Hobbit of the Shire and a member of the Cotton family. Biography Wilcombe was born in TA 2946 ( SR 1346 ) to Holman Cotton and his unnamed wife. He was the brother of Tolman Cotton and an uncle to Rosie Cotton , the eventual wife of Samwise Gamgee . Little else is known of his life, save that he was likely a resident of Bywater .
