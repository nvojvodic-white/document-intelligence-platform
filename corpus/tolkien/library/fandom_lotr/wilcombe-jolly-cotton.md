# Wilcombe (Jolly) Cotton

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Wilcombe_(Jolly)_Cotton

Wilcombe "Jolly" Cotton was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Wilcombe was born in TA 2984 (SR 1384), the same year as his sister Rosie . He was the third child and second son of Tolman Cotton Sr. and Lily (née Brown) Cotton . Whether he and Rosie were twins is unknown. He had two older siblings, Tolman Cotton Jr. and Rosie Gamgee (wife of Samwise Gamgee ), and two younger brothers, Bowman (Nick) and Carl (Nibs) . He and his brothers fought in the Battle of Bywater during the Scouring of the Shire . In adaptations In The Lord of the Rings Online , Jolly Cotton can be found on his family farm, working the soil alongside his brother Nick. He plays no role in the game.
