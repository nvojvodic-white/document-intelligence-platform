# Wilibald Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Wilibald_Bolger

Wilibald Bolger was a Hobbit of the Shire . Biography Wilibald was born to Theobald Bolger and Nina Lightfoot . He married Prisca Baggins . They had three children; Wilimar born in TA 2947 (SR 1347), Heribald born in TA 2951 (SR 1351), and Nora TA 2960 (SR 1360). He died in TA 3000 (SR 1400).
