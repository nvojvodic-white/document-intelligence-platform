# Wilimar Bolger

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Wilimar_Bolger

Wilimar Bolger was a hobbit of the Shire . Wilimar was born in TA 2947 (SR 1347) to Wilibald Bolger and Prisca Baggins . He was the older brother of both Heribald and Nora .
