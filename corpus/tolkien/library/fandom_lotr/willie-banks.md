# Willie Banks

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Willie_Banks

Willie Banks was a Hobbit of the Banks family in Bree . Biography Willie Banks was called to arms when Sharkey and the Ruffians attacked Bree. He was killed among the battle with a few other Bree Hobbits. References ↑ The Lord of the Rings , The Return of the King , Book Six, Chapter VII: " Homeward Bound " See also Banks (family)
