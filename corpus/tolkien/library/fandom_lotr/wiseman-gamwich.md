# Wiseman Gamwich

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Wiseman_Gamwich

Wiseman Gamwich was a hobbit of the Shire . Biography Wiseman Gamwich was born in TA 2800 , son of Hamfast of Gamwich . He had at least one child, Hob Gammidge . He moved from Gamwich to Tighfield . References ↑ The Lord of the Rings : Appendix C , "The Longfather-tree of Master Samwise"
