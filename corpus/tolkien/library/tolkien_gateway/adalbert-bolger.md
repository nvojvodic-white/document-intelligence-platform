# Adalbert Bolger

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Adalbert_Bolger

Adalbert Bolger was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Adalbert was the son of Rudibert Bolger and Amethyst Hornblower. He married Gerda Boffin; their son Filibert, was born in 1342.


== Etymology ==
Adalbert is a Germanic name composed of the elements adal "noble" and beraht "bright".
