# Adaldrida Bolger

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Adaldrida_Bolger

Adaldrida Brandybuck, née Bolger, was a Hobbit of the Shire


== History ==
Adaldrida was the daughter of Gundahar Bolger and Dina Diggle. She was the younger sister of Adalgar. 
Adaldrida married Marmadoc Brandybuck, and had four children: Gorbadoc, Orgulas and two daughters.


== Etymology ==
Adaldrida may be a Germanic name composed of the elements adal "noble" and a 2nd element.
