# Adalgar Bolger

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Adalgar_Bolger

Adalgar Bolger was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Adalgar was the eldest child of Gundahar Bolger and Dina Diggle. He had three children: Rudigar, Rudibert and Ruby.


== Etymology ==
Adalgar is a Germanic name composed of the elements adal "noble" and gar, ger "spear".
