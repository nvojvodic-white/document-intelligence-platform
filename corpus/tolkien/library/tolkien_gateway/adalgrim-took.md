# Adalgrim Took

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Adalgrim_Took

Adalgrim Took was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Adalgrim was the only child of Hildigrim Took and Rosa Baggins. He had five children, four of them daughters. His youngest children were Paladin and Esmeralda, born in S.R. 1333 and S.R. 1336.


== Etymology ==
Adalgrim comes from the Germanic elements adal meaning "noble" and grim meaning "fierce".
