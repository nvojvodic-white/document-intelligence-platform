# Adam Hornblower

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Adam_Hornblower

Adam Hornblower was a smith in Hobbiton in the precursor of the chapter The Shadow of the Past of The Fellowship of the Ring, which, at the time of the writing was called Of Gollum and the Ring.
He was mentioned by Gandalf in conversation with Frodo, about how nothing could melt The One Ring.
