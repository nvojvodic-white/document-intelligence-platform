# Aerandir

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Aerandir

Aerandir was one of the three mariners (together with Erellont and Falathar) to accompany Eärendil on his voyage to Aman.


== Etymology ==
Aerandir is Sindarin, meaning "Sea-wanderer" (aer + randir).
The name for the character appearing in an early manuscript was Airandir, a form later rejected by Tolkien.
