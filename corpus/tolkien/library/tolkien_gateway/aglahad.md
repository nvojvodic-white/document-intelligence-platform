# Aglahad

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Aglahad

Aglahad (Third Age 2827 – 2932, aged 105 years) was the nineteenth Prince of Dol Amroth.


== History ==
Aglahad was a descendant of Galador, the first Prince of Dol Amroth; through Galador's mother Mithrellas, Aglahad had some Elven blood.
Aglahad ruled Dol Amroth around the time of Steward Turgon. During Aglahad's reign as Prince, Gondor had to abandon much of Ithilien.
After his death, his son Angelimir became the twentieth Prince.
