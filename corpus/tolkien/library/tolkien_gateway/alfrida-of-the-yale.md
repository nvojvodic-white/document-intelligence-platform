# Alfrida of the Yale

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Alfrida_of_the_Yale

Alfrida of the Yale was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Alfrida was the wife of Gundolpho Bolger. They had three children: Gundahar, Rudolph and Gundahad.


== Etymology ==
A name of germanic origin.
