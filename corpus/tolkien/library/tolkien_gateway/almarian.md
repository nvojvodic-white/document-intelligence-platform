# Almarian

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Almarian

Almarian (Q, pron. [alˈmari.an]) was the daughter of Vëantur, Queen to Tar-Meneldur, the fifth King of Númenor, and mother to Tar-Aldarion, the sixth King, and to Ailinel and Almiel.  She was said to possess great beauty.


== Etymology ==
Almarian is a Quenya name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it contains almare ("blessedness").
