# Alphros

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Alphros

Alphros (T.A. 3017 - Fo.A. 95) was the  twenty-fourth Prince of Dol Amroth.


== History ==
Alphros was born near the end of the Third Age, and was only a toddler when the War of the Ring raged in Gondor. Growing up in the Fourth Age, he became Prince after his father Elphir's death, and served under King Elessar. He died in Fo.A. 95.


== Etymology ==
Alphros seems to be a Sindarin name. Its meaning is not glossed, but it seems to mean "Swan Foam" and to be a combination of  alph "swan" and ros ("foam").
