# Amethyst Hornblower

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Amethyst_Hornblower

Amethyst Hornblower was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Amethyst married Rudibert Bolger (born S.R. 1260) and was the mother of Adalbert Bolger (S.R. 1301-1397).


== Etymology ==
Amethyst is named after the gemstone.
