# Amon Darthir

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Amon_Darthir

Amon Darthir was a mountain in the range of the Ered Wethrin, the Mountains of Shadow that separated Dor-lómin to the north from Beleriand to the south. It stood near the house of Húrin in the south-east of Dor-lómin, and the springs of Nen Lalaith were among its northern slopes. The secret and difficult passes that ran beneath Amon Darthir's peak were the only way through the Ered Wethrin for many leagues in either direction.


== Etymology ==
Amon Darthir is Sindarin:

amon 'steep-sloped mountain'
dartha 'to wait'
