# Amon Obel

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Amon_Obel

Amon Obel was a hill in the midst of the Forest of Brethil on which was built the settlement of Ephel Brandir, which became the main settlement of the Haladin.


== Etymology ==
Amon Obel is a Sindarin name for "Fortified Hill", composed of the words amon ("hill") and gobel ("walled settlement").
