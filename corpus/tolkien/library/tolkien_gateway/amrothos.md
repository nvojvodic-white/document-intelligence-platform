# Amrothos

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Amrothos

Amrothos was the third son of Imrahil of Dol Amroth.


== History ==
He was born in T.A. 2994. He had one younger sister, Lothíriel and two older brothers; Elphir and Erchirion.


== Etymology ==
Amrothos seems to be a Sindarin name. Its meaning is not glossed, but it could mean "Terror of Amroth" and be a compound of the name Amroth and gos ("dread", "terror").
