# Anson Roper

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Anson_Roper

Anson Roper was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Anson was the son of Andwise Roper, and cousin of Samwise Gamgee. He lived in Tighfield and probably followed his father's trade of ropery.


== Etymology ==
The name likely means "Andy's son".
