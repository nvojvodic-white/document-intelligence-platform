# Arador

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Arador

Arador (S, pron. [ˈarador]; T.A. 2820 - 2930, died aged 110) was the fourteenth Chieftain of the Dúnedain.


== History ==
Arador succeeded his father, Argonui, when he died in 2912.
In his time many Dúnedain of the North lived in woods between the river Hoarwell and the river Loudwater. Arador lived in a house north of the  Trollshaws in woods near the river Hoarwell, which was threatened by a group of Trolls.
His rule was uneventful except for the fact that he was captured by Hill-trolls in the Coldfells north of Rivendell and killed by them. He was succeeded by his young son, Arathorn II, in 2930.


== Etymology ==
Arador is a Sindarin name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Noble-lord" and is a compound of the prefix ar(a)- ("noble", "royal", "high") and the lenited form -dor of taur ("lord"). The Association Tolkiendil suggests it means "Royal Lord".
