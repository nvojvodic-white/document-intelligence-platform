# Aranuir

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Aranuir

Aranuir (T.A. 2084 - 2247, died aged 163) was the third of the Chieftains of the Dúnedain, following the death of his father, Arahael, in 2177.


== History ==
As far as records go, Aranuir's rule was uneventful. Aranuir died in 2247, after having been chieftain for seventy years, and was followed by his son, Aravir.


== Etymology ==
Aranuir is a Sindarin name. Its meaning is not glossed, but the Association Tolkiendil suggests it means "Eternal King". It is probably a compound of aran ("king", "lord", "chief", [lit.] high or noble person) and uir ("eternity").
