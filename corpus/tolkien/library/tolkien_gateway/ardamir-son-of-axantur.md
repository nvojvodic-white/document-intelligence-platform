# Ardamir (son of Axantur)

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Ardamir_(son_of_Axantur)

Ardamir (Q: "Arda-jewel", pron. [ˈardamir], stem Ardamír-) was the second child and first son of Axantur, a nobleman of Númenor. His great-nephew Hallacar would marry Tar-Ancalimë, the first Ruling Queen of Númenor. Though he left descendants, nothing of their lives was recorded.


== Etymology ==
Ardamir is a Quenya name. It means "Jewel of the World". Ardamire (or Ardamir) was the mother-name given to Eärendil, the son of Idril and Tuor. Paul Strack suggests it means "World-jewel" and is a comppound of arda and  -mir ("jewel").
