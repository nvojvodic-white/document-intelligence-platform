# Asphodel Brandybuck

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Asphodel_Brandybuck

Asphodel Burrows, née Brandybuck, was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Asphodel was the daughter of Gorbadoc Brandybuck and Mirabella Took. She married Rufus Burrows, and their child, Milo Burrows, was born in S.R. 1347. They all attended Bilbo's Party in S.R. 1401.


== Etymology ==
"Asphodel" is a plant of the lily family with white, yellow or pink flowers.
