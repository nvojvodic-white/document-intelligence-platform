# Aulendil (son of Vardamir)

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Aulendil_(son_of_Vardamir)

Aulendil was the second son and third child of Vardamir Nólimon, the heir of Elros Tar-Minyatur, first King of Númenor. His older brother took up the Sceptre as Tar-Amandil. Though he left descendants, nothing of their lives was recorded.


== Etymology ==
Aulendil is a Quenya name, meaning "Servant of Aulë" according to Christopher Tolkien. Paul Strack explains that according to ordinary nomenclature, it should mean "Friend of Aulë", because of the suffix -(n)dil ("friend", "lover", "devoted to").
