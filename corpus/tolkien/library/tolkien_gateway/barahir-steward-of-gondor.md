# Barahir (Steward of Gondor)

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Barahir_(Steward_of_Gondor)

Barahir (Third Age 2290 – 2412, aged 122 years) was eighth Ruling Steward of Gondor.
The Watchful Peace continued during his rule.
Barahir ruled as Steward for seventeen years, and was succeeded by his son Dior.


== Etymology ==
For the meaning of his name refer to Barahir - Etymology. Barahir was probably named after the hero of the First Age who was the father of Beren.
