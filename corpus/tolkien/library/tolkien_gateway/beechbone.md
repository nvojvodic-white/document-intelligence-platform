# Beechbone

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Beechbone

Beechbone was a notably tall and handsome Ent.


== History ==
On 3 March T.A. 3019 he was caught in a burning spray sent up by Saruman from beneath the Ring of Isengard, but his fiery end brought his fellow Ents to the peak of their destructive anger.
