# Belba Baggins

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Belba_Baggins

Belba Baggins was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Belba was the eldest daughter, and second-oldest child, of Mungo Baggins and Laura Grubb. This made her the aunt of Bilbo. She married Rudigar Bolger, and they had one child together, Herugar, the grandfather of Fredegar. 


== Portrayal in adaptations ==
2003: The Hobbit (2003 video game):

Five portraits are clustered on a wall in Bag End. One of them is Belba, and one of Bilbo's lines when looking at the portraits is "That's my Aunt Belba".
