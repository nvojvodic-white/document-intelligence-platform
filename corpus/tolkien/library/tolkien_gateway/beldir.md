# Beldir

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Beldir

Beldir (born F.A. 316) was a man of the House of Bëor.


== History ==
Beldir was the son of Belen. He had one son himself, Belemir, who was born in F.A. 339.


== Genealogy ==


== Other versions of the legendarium ==
In the early versions of the genealogy of the House of Bëor there was no mention of Beldir, who did not appear until the revisions made after after publication of The Lord of the Rings in the 1950s.
