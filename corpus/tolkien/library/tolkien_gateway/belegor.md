# Belegor

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Belegor

Belegor (born F.A. 340) was a man of the House of Bëor.


== History ==
Belegor was the second son of Boron, and the younger brother of Boromir. He could trace his ancestry directly to Bëor, who was his great-grandfather.
