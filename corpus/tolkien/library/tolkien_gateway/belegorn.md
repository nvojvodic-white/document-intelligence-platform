# Belegorn

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Belegorn

Belegorn (Third Age 2074 – 2204, aged 130 years) was the fourth Ruling Steward of Gondor.


== History ==
Belegorn became the fourth Ruling Steward at the death of his father, Steward Herion in T.A. 2148.
Belegorn's rule was a quiet time for Gondor during the Watchful Peace, when Sauron was in the East and the Nazgûl stayed quiet in Minas Morgul.
Belegorn ruled as Steward for fiftysix years, and was succeeded by his son, Húrin I..


== Etymology ==
Belegorn is a Sindarin name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Great-tree" and is a compound of beleg ("great", "mighty", "large", "big") and orn ([tall] "tree").
Belegorn was probably named after a renowned heroe in the royal lines of old as recorded in legend.
