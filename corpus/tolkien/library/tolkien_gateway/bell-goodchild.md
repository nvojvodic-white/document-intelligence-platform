# Bell Goodchild

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Bell_Goodchild

Bell Gamgee, née Goodchild, was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Bell married Hamfast Gamgee. She would eventually give birth to Hamson, Halfred, Daisy, May, the famed Samwise and Marigold.


== Etymology ==
Bell is possibly named after bellflowers, such as bluebell and harebell.


== Portrayal in adaptations ==


=== Bell Goodchild in adaptations ===

		
			
			
		

2003: The Hobbit (2003 video game):

Bell Goodchild is a Hobbit child, playing hide-and-seek with Hamfast, Sadoc, Hilda and a somewhat unwilling Bilbo Baggins. She hides on the top floor of the Old Mill, which is only accessible by riding the water-wheel. Bell and Hamfast are shown to have a mutual love interest, but neither of them told the other.
