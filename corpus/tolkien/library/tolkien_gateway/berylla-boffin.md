# Berylla Boffin

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Berylla_Boffin

Berylla Baggins, née Boffin, was a Hobbit of the Shire. 


== History ==
Berylla married Balbo Baggins. Their children were Mungo, Pansy, Ponto, Largo, and Lily.
Berylla's brother was Basso, who is said to have "gone to sea" in S.R. 1195 at the age of 26.


== Etymology ==
Berylla is named after beryl, a type of mineral which includes emerald and aquamarine.
