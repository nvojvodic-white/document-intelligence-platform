# Bilbo Gardner

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Bilbo_Gardner

Bilbo Gardner was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Bilbo was the tenth child of Samwise Gamgee and Rose Cotton. He was named after Bilbo Baggins, his father's old hero.


== Etymology ==
The name Bilbo was meaningless in contemporary Hobbitish Westron. It represented an original name Bilba.
