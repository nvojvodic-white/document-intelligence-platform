# Borlach

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Borlach

Borlach was one of the sons of Bór.


== History ==
Borlach and his kin entered the service of Maedhros and Maglor, the eldest Sons of Fëanor. He fought in the Nirnaeth Arnoediad, and was faithful to the Noldor, taking part in the slaying of Ulfang's sons before he was himself slain.


== Genealogy ==


== Etymology ==
The exact meaning is unclear, but is  tied to the root BOR- ("loyalty") adding the word lach ("flame").


== Other versions of the legendarium ==
In the Later Annals of Beleriand and the Grey Annals, the name of the character was Boromir, a name later used for Boromir of Ladros. The Boromir referred to in the Etymologies (cf. Boromir of Ladros: Etymology) is thus Borlach. In the appendix of The Lost Road it states that Borlach (then called Boromir) was born in the year 145.
