# Bow of Bregor

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Bow_of_Bregor

The Bow of Bregor belonged to Bregor, the Lord of Ladros, and remained an heirloom of the House of Bëor.
The House was decimated, but the Bow somehow survived into the Second Age and the Edain brought it to Númenor. It was kept as an heirloom of the Kings of Númenor, until its downfall, when it was lost during the deluge.
