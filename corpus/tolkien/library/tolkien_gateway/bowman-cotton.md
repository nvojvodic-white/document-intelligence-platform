# Bowman Cotton

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Bowman_Cotton

Bowman "Nick" Cotton was the fourth child of Farmer Tom Cotton.


== History ==
With his father and his brothers, Nick fought in the Battle of Bywater against Sharkey's ruffians.
