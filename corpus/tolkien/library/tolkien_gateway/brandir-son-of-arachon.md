# Brandir (son of Arachon)

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Brandir_(son_of_Arachon)

Brandir was a man of the House of Bëor.


== History ==
Brandir was the elder child of Arachon and Bregil. His younger sister Beldis married Handir of Brethil and their child, known as Brandir the Lame, was named after his uncle.


== Etymology ==
The name Brandir is said to be a compound of brand + dîr, meaning "Noble Man".
