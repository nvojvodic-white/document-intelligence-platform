# Bruno Bracegirdle

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Bruno_Bracegirdle

Bruno Bracegirdle was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Bruno was the son of Blanco and Primrose. He had a younger sister, the infamous Lobelia Sackville-Baggins, and two children, Hugo and Hilda.
Bruno attended Bilbo's Farewell Party.
