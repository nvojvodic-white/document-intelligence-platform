# Cape Andras

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Cape_Andras

Cape Andras was a long cape stretching south and west of the city of Brithombar and forming a small bay shielding the city.
The Cape Andras marked the northwestern limit of the region of Eglamar.


== Etymology ==
The name Andras is in Sindarin, meaning "Long Cape". It is composed of the elements and ("long") + ras(t) ("cape").
