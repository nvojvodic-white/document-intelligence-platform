# Captain of Gondor

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Captain_of_Gondor

The Captain of Gondor was a military title and position of Gondor.
Prince Eärnur held the title Captain of Gondor leading the Host of the West against Angmar in the Battle of Fornost.
A millennium later, Faramir also held the title, as the leader of the Rangers of Ithilien.
