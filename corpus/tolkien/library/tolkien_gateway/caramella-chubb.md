# Caramella Chubb

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Caramella_Chubb

Caramella Chubb was one of the recipients of Bilbo's gifts (a clock, due to her lack of punctuality) during his birthday party, in the drafts for The Lord of the Rings. Her name was initially Caramella Took, before she ultimately became a Chubb.
