# Carl Cotton

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Carl_Cotton

Carl "Nibs" Cotton was a Hobbit of the Shire and the youngest child of Tolman (Farmer) Cotton.


== History ==
Born in S.R. 1389, Carl and his siblings spent much time in Bywater, and would often swim in the Bywater Pool with the Gamgee children. He had not yet come of age when Sharkey's Ruffians entered the Shire, so his father ordered him to guard the house with a pitch fork while his father and brothers fought in the Battle of Bywater.


== Etymology ==
Carl was probably named after his ancestor, Carl, son of Cottar. The nickname "Nibs" has been a term of mock respect in historical English. 


== Portrayal in adaptations ==

2007: The Lord of the Rings Online:

Nibs Cotton appears at his family farm only during the annual Spring Festival, during which he is involved in a festival quest. He has a pet cat named Waldy.
