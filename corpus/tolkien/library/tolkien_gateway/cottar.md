# Cottar

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Cottar

Cottar was the progenitor of the Cotton Family.


== History ==
Cottar was born in S.R. 1220, and had two sons: Cotman and Carl. Cotman married Rose, the daughter of Holman the greenhanded.


== Etymology ==
In Middle English cottar means "cotter, cottager" (Old English cot "cottage").
