# Dagorlad

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Dagorlad

Dagorlad was the dusty plain that lay to the northwest of Mordor, immediately below the Black Gate of the Morannon. This was the site of several important battles in Gondor's history, most notably the defeat of Sauron's forces in the famous Battle of Dagorlad in S.A. 3434, King Calimehtar's victory over the Easterlings in T.A. 1899, and the Battle of the Morannon on 25 March, T.A. 3019.


== Portrayal in adaptations ==

2017: The Lord of the Rings Online:

The Dagorlad is an area in "the Wastes" region located north of the Black Gate.
