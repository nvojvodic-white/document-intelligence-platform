# Daisy Baggins

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Daisy_Baggins

Daisy Boffin, née Baggins, was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Daisy was the daughter of Dudo Baggins, born in S.R. 1350. She married Griffo Boffin and had a child, Tosto Boffin in S.R. 1388. They were all guests at Bilbo's Farewell Party.


== Etymology ==
Daisy, a flower name, derives from Old English dæges eage "day's eye".


== Portrayal in adaptations ==
2007 The Lord of the Rings Online:

Daisy Boffin normally appears in Bywater as a vendor with no unique lines. During the annual Yule Festival, she is found at the Party Tree where she asks the player for assistance with celebration preparations.
