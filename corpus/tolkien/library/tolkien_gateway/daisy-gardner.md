# Daisy Gardner

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Daisy_Gardner

Daisy Gardner was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Daisy was the fourth daughter and eighth child of Samwise Gamgee and Rose Cotton, born in S.R. 1433.
She was named after her father’s sister, Daisy Gamgee.


== Etymology ==
Daisy, a flower name, derives from Old English dæges eage "day's eye".  In Sindarin, the name Daisy is Eirien.
