# Dinodas Brandybuck

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Dinodas_Brandybuck

Dinodas Brandybuck was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Dinodas was the sixth child and youngest son of Gorbadoc Brandybuck and Mirabella Took. He attended Bilbo's Farewell Party.


== Etymology ==
The meaning of the name Dinodas is unknown. J.R.R. Tolkien noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely Celtic style.
Jim Allan suggests that the name Dinodas is a rearrangement of the name Dodinas.
Dodynas and Dinodes are both alternate spellings of the Table Knight Sir Dodinas le Savage.


== Other versions of the legendarium ==
In earlier versions of the family tree of the Brandybuck family he was called Priamus.
