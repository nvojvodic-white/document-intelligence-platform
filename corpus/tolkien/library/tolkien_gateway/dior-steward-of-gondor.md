# Dior (Steward of Gondor)

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Dior_(Steward_of_Gondor)

Dior (Third Age 2328 – 2435, aged 107 years) was the ninth Ruling Steward of Gondor. 


== History ==
Dior was the last Ruling Steward during the period of the Watchful Peace.
Dior, having had no children, was succeeded by his sister Rían's son Denethor I.


== Etymology ==
For the meaning of his name refer to Dior - Etymology. Dior was probably named after the son of Beren and Lúthien of the First Age.
