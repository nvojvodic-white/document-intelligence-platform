# Doderic Brandybuck

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Doderic_Brandybuck

Doderic Brandybuck was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Doderic was a son of Seredic Brandybuck and Hilda Bracegirdle. He attended Bilbo's Farewell Party.


== Etymology ==
The meaning of the name Doderic is unknown. J.R.R. Tolkien noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely Celtic style.
Jim Allan suggests that the name Doderic is a Germanic form of Theuderic with the variatons Theodoric and Dietrich and is a combination of the elements theud ("people") and ric ("rule", "reign").


== Other versions of the legendarium ==
In an earlier version of the family tree of the Brandybuck family he had the name Roderic, the name of a Visigothic king of Spain between A.D. 710 and 711.


== Portrayal in adaptations ==
2007: The Lord of the Rings Online:

Doderic Brandybuck had ventured into the Old Forest along with his friend Rollo, son of Farmer Maggot. Saradoc Brandybuck asks the player to find them and send them back.
