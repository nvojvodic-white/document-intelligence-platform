# Dol Tarlang

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Dol_Tarlang

Dol Tarlang was the northernmost of three peaks (the other two being Cûl Bîn and Cûl Veleg) located in the pass of Tarlang's Neck. The three mountains formed a spur on the southern side of the White Mountains.
According to legend, Dol Tarlang was in origin the head of the giant Tarlang.


== Etymology ==
The name Dol Tarlang is glossed as "Tarlang's Head", consisting of dol ("head") and Tarlang.
