# Donnamira Took

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Donnamira_Took

Donnamira Boffin, née Took, was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Donnamira was one of Old Took's younger children, born in S.R. 1256. She married Hugo Boffin and their children were Jago and Jessamine.


== Etymology ==
Donnamira's name participates in a circular wordplay with the names of her sisters, Belladonna and Mirabella, all Italian names.
