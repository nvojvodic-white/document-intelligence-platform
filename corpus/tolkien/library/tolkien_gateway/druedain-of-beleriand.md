# Drúedain of Beleriand

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain_of_Beleriand

The Drúedain of Beleriand were those Drúedain who followed the ancestors of the Haladin from the White Mountains to the West and reached Beleriand. There they lived and fought as allies of the Edain during the late First Age. Some, like Aghan, lived in the region of the Forest of Brethil.
Like the Edain, those who survived the War of Wrath removed to Elenna.
