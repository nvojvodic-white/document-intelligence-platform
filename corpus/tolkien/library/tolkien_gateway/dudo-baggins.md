# Dudo Baggins

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Dudo_Baggins

Dudo Baggins was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Dudo was the son of Fosco Baggins and Ruby Bolger. He was the brother of Drogo Baggins and had a daughter, Daisy. Dudo, his sister, daughter and son-in-law were guests at Bilbo's Farewell Party.
