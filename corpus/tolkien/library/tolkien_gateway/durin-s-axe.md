# Durin's Axe

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Durin%27s_Axe

Durin's Axe was a great heirloom of the Dwarves.


== History ==
The axe remained lost in Khazad-dûm after it was deserted in T.A. 1981. It was found again by Balin's expedition, but lost again in 2994, when his colony was destroyed.


== Portrayal in adaptations ==
2023: The Lord of the Rings: Return to Moria:

The axe is broken into several fragments that must be recovered by the player in order for the axe to be reforged.
