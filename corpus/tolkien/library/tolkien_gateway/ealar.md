# Ëalar

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/%C3%8Balar

ëalar is a Quenya word which referred to such spirits who did not require a body in order to be complete. Eälar are contrasted with fëar, which are the souls of beings whose natural state is to be incarnated in a body.


== Etymology ==
The word is the plural of Quenya ëala ("being"), and seems to be related to the Quenya verb ëa ("be, exist"). 
It has been suggested that -la is the present participle ending of verbs (cf. "-ing"; but Quenya -la only forms adjectival participles).
