# Eärendil (King of Gondor)

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil_(King_of_Gondor)

Eärendil (Q "sea-friend", pron. [ˌe.aˈrendil]; T.A. 48 – 324, aged 276 years) was the fifth King of Gondor, ruling for 86 years, from 238 until his death.

== Other versions of the legendarium ==
In The Peoples of Middle-earth, Section VII "The Heirs of Elendil", it was explained that three manuscripts existed in the textual history of this section.  Manuscript A contained the Southern Line of Gondor, and appeared to be the first stage of composition.  Manuscript B added the Northern Line, and began as a fair copy of A for the Southern line but became heavily emended.  Manuscript B led to Manuscript C, a fine copy (but with later emendations), which led to a final typescript D.  In the earlier Manuscripts A and B, Eärendil lived from T.A. 40 to 316 instead of T.A. 48 to 324.
