# Eärendur (Lord of Andúnië)

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/E%C3%A4rendur_(Lord_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB)

Eärendur (pron. [ˌe.aˈrendur]) was the fifteenth Lord of Andúnië in Númenor in the days of king Ar-Sakalthôr. Eärendur was the brother of Lindórië, who would be the grandmother of the wise King Tar-Palantir.
As Lord of Andúnië, Eärendur was also the ancestor of the Kings of Gondor and Arnor in Middle-earth. Aragorn II was his direct descendant through no less than forty-four generations.


== Etymology ==
Eärendur is a Quenya name. It means "(professional) mariner". As Paul Strack explains, a more literal translation might be "Servant of the Sea". It is a compound of eär ("sea") and -(n)dur ("servant").
