# Eglantine Banks

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Eglantine_Banks

Eglantine Took (née Banks) was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Eglantine was the wife of Thain Paladin Took, and mother of Pearl, Pimpernel, Pervinca and Pippin. She was present at Bilbo Baggins' Birthday Party.


== Etymology ==
She was named after the flower eglantine.
