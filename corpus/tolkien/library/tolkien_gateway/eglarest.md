# Eglarest

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Eglarest

Eglarest was the southern of the two Havens of the Falas that lay at the mouth of the River Nenning on the western coasts of Beleriand. The northern haven was called Brithombar. They were founded during the Years of the Trees of the First Age by Círdan. Upon the cape west of Eglarest Finrod had built Barad Nimras. The Havens lasted until they were taken by Morgoth in F.A. 473.


== Etymology ==
It has been suggested that the name means "Ravine of the Eglain". Cf. the Noldorin word rhest and Imladris.


== Other names ==
An earlier name for this city is Eldorest, which takes its name from the river Eldor whose mouth is located near the city. This name appears on the Westward extension of The First 'Silmarillion' Map.
