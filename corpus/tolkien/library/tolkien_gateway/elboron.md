# Elboron

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Elboron

Elboron was the second Prince of Ithilien, and Steward of Gondor.


== History ==
Elboron was the son of Faramir and Éowyn. Upon his father's death in Fo.A. 82, he became the second Prince of Ithilien and the Steward of Gondor.
Elboron was either the father or uncle of Barahir, the grandson of Faramir who wrote The Tale of Aragorn and Arwen.


== Etymology ==
Elboron may mean "Enduring Star", from elen ("star") and boron ("long-lasting, enduring").
