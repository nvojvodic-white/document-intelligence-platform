# Elfhild

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Elfhild

Elfhild was the wife of King Théoden.


== History ==
Elfhild died in childbirth, leaving Théoden a widower and Théodred motherless. At the time of the War of the Ring, Rohan had been without a queen for forty years.
