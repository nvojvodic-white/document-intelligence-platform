# Elfstan Fairbairn

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Elfstan_Fairbairn

Elfstan Fairbairn was the son of Fastred of Greenholm and Samwise Gamgee's eldest daughter Elanor, born in the year Fo.A. 33 (1454 by the Shire-reckoning). He succeeded his father as Warden of Westmarch.


== Etymology ==
His name means "Elfstone", and probably he was named so in honour of King Elessar.
