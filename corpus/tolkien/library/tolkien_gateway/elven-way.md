# Elven-way

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Elven-way

The Elven-way was the highroad that ran from Ost-in-Edhil in the Elven realm of Eregion, along the course of the Sirannon stream to the eastern end of Eregion, which was marked by two tall Holly trees that stood before the Doors of Durin of the underground dwarven realm of Moria. In the year T.A. 3019 of the late Third Age, the Fellowship of the Ring used the Elven-way on their approach to the Doors of Durin.
