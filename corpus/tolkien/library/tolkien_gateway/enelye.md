# Enelyë

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Enely%C3%AB

Enelyë was the third spouse to the Elf-fathers to be awakened. She became the spouse to Enel, and so joined the Nelyar clan. She was one of the Three Elderwomen of the Elves, whom Oromë taught the art of making lembas.
