# Eofor

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Eofor

Eofor was a prince of Rohan.


== History ==
Eofor was the third son of king Brego. His older brothers were Baldor and King Aldor. He lived in the town of Aldburg. Éomund was his descendant


== Etymology ==
The name Eofor is likely to have been borrowed from the Old English word  eofor (meaning "boar").
