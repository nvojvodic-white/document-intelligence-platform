# Erchirion

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Erchirion

Erchirion was the son and second child of Imrahil, Prince of Dol Amroth.
He had an older brother, Elphir, and younger siblings Amrothos and Lothíriel.


== Etymology ==
The name is probably derived from ar- hîr (both meaning "noble lord") plus the frequent ending -ion "son of".
Alternatively, it could also derive from er-cirion meaning "lone shipman".
