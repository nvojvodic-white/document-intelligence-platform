# Erellont

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Erellont

Erellont was one of the three mariners who accompanied Eärendil and Elwing to Aman, the other two being Aerandir and Falathar.
Tolkien never specified the race of the three mariners: while it has been suggested that they were Men, others argue that they were Elves.


== Etymology ==
The meaning of Erellont is unknown. It has been speculated that the name means "lonely (star) ship" in Sindarin, or that the name is "possibly a dialectal Sindarin of the Falas" or even a "combination of [Mannish] and Elvish".
