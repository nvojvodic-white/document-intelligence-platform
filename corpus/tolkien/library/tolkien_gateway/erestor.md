# Erestor

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Erestor

Erestor was an Elf of Rivendell, and the chief counsellor of Elrond.


== History ==
Erestor, being the chief counsellor of Elrond's household at the time of the War of the Ring, took part in the Council of Elrond. He did not support the idea of destroying the Ring, and preferred instead the notion of guarding it from Sauron, perhaps with the aid of Iarwain ben-adar. His suggestion was countered by Glorfindel, as well as Galdor, as they did not believe that Iarwain had the power to defy Sauron's. 
Erestor accompanied Arwen to her marriage with Aragorn in Gondor in T.A. 3019.


== Etymology ==
The name Erestor is in Sindarin, but its meaning is unclear.


== Other versions of the legendarium ==
In the earliest introduction of Erestor, he was one of three counsellors of Elrond's (the other two being unnamed); all three were kinsmen of Elrond's, "of that half-elvish folk whom the Elves named the children of Lúthien". In a further update, the other two were made into unnamed Elf-lords of Rivendell, with Erestor being the sole remaining half-elf and kinsman of Elrond. However, Erestor is never called a half-elf in the finished text. 
In the initial selection for the Nine of the Company of the Ring, Erestor was to represent the half-Elves.
Originally, only Elrond, Galadriel, and Celeborn accompany Arwen to her wedding.


== Portrayal in adaptations ==

		
			
			
		
		
			
			
		
		
			
			
		

2001: The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring:

A card from The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game expansion: Realms of the Elf Lords identifies the Elf sitting next to Elrond at the Counci as Erestor. He is portrayed by Jonathan Harding.
2007: The Lord of the Rings Online:

Prior to the Fellowship's departure from Rivendell, Erestor is involved in a quest exclusive to the Elven characters. After the War of the Ring, he joins the procession from Rivendell that travels to Minas Tirith for Lady Arwen's wedding.
2023: The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth:

Erestor is depicted on the "Erestor of the Council" card, illustrated by Syd Mills. The card has the following effects:
Whenever players finish voting, each opponent who voted for a choice you voted for creates a Treasure token. You scry X, where X is the number of opponents who voted for a choice you didn’t vote for. Draw a card.
