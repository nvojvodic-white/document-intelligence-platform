# Erien

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Erien

Erien was the daughter of Fingon according to a very late, but ultimately rejected version of the legendarium.
She had one other sibling, a brother called Finbor.


== Etymology ==
The name Erien is in Sindarin, but it has no clear meaning. A possible explanation of her name would be a combination of the Sindarin adjective er ("one, alone") + the feminine suffix -ien.
An earlier, rejected form of her name was Ernis.
