# Erling

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Erling

Erling was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Erling was the middle child of Holman the greenhanded, born in S.R. 1254.


== Etymology ==
Old English eorling means "descendant of an earl (noble)". It is the same word as Eorlingas but it doesn't refer to Eorl the Young.
