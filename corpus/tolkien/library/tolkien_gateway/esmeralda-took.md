# Esmeralda Took

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Esmeralda_Took

Esmeralda Brandybuck, née Took, was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Esmeralda was the youngest of the four daughters of Adalgrim Took. She married Saradoc Brandybuck, making the Master of Buckland Rorimac Brandybuck, her father-in-law. She and Saradoc had a son, Meriadoc Brandybuck
She attended the Farewell Party of Bilbo Baggins in S.R. 1401 with her family. When Bilbo disappeared, Master Rorimac attempted to calm her, saying that the "Mad Baggins" just left for another adventure.
Later Esmeralda herself became mistress of Buckland and of Brandy Hall. Her older brother Paladin Took II also became the Thain. Her son Meriadoc was cousin with the Thain's children, and especially close friends with his cousin Peregrin Took.


== Etymology ==
Esmeralda is the Spanish word for "emerald".
