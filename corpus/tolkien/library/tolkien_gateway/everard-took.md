# Everard Took

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Everard_Took

Everard Took was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Everard was the youngest son of Adelard Took. At Bilbo's Farewell Party in S.R. 1401, he danced the Springle-ring with Melilot Brandybuck when he thought the Speech was over. 


== Etymology ==
The meaning is "boar-strong" (ever + hard).


== Other versions of the legendarium ==
Everard's name was originally 'Prospero' until Tolkien's sixth draft version of A Long-expected Party.
