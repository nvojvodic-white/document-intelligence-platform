# Fastolph Bolger

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Fastolph_Bolger

Fastolph Bolger was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Fastolp was the son of Rudolph Bolger and Cora Goodbody. He married Pansy Baggins and had various descendants.


== Etymology ==
Composed from fast "firm" and wulf "wolf.
