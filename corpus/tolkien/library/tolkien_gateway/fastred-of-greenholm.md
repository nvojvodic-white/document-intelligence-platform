# Fastred of Greenholm

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Fastred_of_Greenholm

Fastred of Greenholm was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Fastred married Elanor Gardner in Fo.A. 30 on the Far Downs. Together they had a son, Elfstan, and a daughter Fíriel. 
He was made Warden of Westmarch in S.R. 1462 by Thain Peregrin Took, and he relocated to the Undertowers with his family.


== Etymology ==
Fastred in Old English means "fast-rede, firm-counsel".
