# Ferdibrand Took

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Ferdibrand_Took

Ferdibrand Took was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Ferdibrand was the son of Ferdinand Took. He was present at Bilbo Baggins' Farewell Party in September S.R. 1401.


== Etymology ==
The name is composed of ferdi "peace" and brand "sword"
Wikipedia gives the etymology frith "protection"/ farð "journey" and nanth


== Portrayal in adaptations ==
