# Ferdinand Took

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Ferdinand_Took

Ferdinand Took was a Hobbit of the Shire. 


== History ==
Ferdinand was the son of Sigismond Took. He was the father of Ferdibrand. They both attended Bilbo Baggins' Farewell Party in S.R. 1401.


== Etymology ==
The name is composed of ferdi "peace" + nand "daring, brave".
Wikipedia gives the etymology frith "protection"/ farð "journey" and nanth
