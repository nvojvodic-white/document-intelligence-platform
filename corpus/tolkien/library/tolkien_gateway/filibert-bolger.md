# Filibert Bolger

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Filibert_Bolger

Filibert Bolger was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Filibert married Poppy Chubb-Baggins. With his wife, he was present at Bilbo's Farewell Party.


== Etymology ==
The name means "Fullbright" from fili and berht.


== Portrayal in adaptations ==

2001-2007: The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game:

A Decipher card identifies one of the Hobbits at the Bilbo's Party in The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring as Filibert Bolger. However, in the film the same hobbit is seen cheering when Bilbo calls "Grubbs!".
2007: The Lord of the Rings Online:

Filibert Bolger lives in Needlehole. He asks the player to find his cow who had become lost in the Rushock Bog.
