# Fíriel Fairbairn

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/F%C3%ADriel_Fairbairn

Fíriel Fairbairn was a Hobbit of the Westmarch of the Shire.


== History ==
Fíriel was the daughter of Fastred of Greenholm and Elanor Gardner and flourished in the first century of the Fourth Age.


== Etymology ==
Fíriel was likely named after Fíriel, the character from the song The Last Ship and/or the Gondorian princess she was based on. The name means "mortal woman" in Quenya.
