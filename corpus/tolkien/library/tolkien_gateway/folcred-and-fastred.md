# Folcred and Fastred

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Folcred_and_Fastred

Folcred and Fastred were the eldest children, and the twin sons of King Folcwine of Rohan.


== History ==
In the days of Steward Túrin II of Gondor the Haradrim had occupied South Gondor and there were many armed conflicts along the river Poros. In T.A. 2885 emissaries of Sauron stirred up the Haradrim who crossed the Poros invaded Ithilien with a large army. When King Folcwine of Rohan heard of the invasion he fulfilled the Oath of Eorl and sent many men led by his sons Folcred and Fastred to help Túrin II.
Túrin II defeated the Haradrim invaders with the help of the troops from Rohan in a battle at the Crossings of Poros, but Folcred and Fastred fell side by side in battle in Ithilien. 
They were buried together high upon the shore of the river Poros according to the customs of their people in a burial mound, which was called Haudh in Gwanûr. The mound stood there for a long time and the enemies of Gondor were afraid to pass the mound.


== Etymology ==
Folcred means "people-counsel" (folc-rēd) in Old English and contains the components "folc" ("people") and "réd" or "rǽd" ("counsel").
Fastred means "firm-counsel" in Old English and contains the components "fast" ("firm") and "réd" or "rǽd" ("counsel").
