# Fortinbras Took I

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Fortinbras_Took_I

Fortinbras Took I was the twenty-fifth Thain of the Shire.


== History ==
Fortinbras was the son of Ferumbras II. He inherited the Thainship upon his father's death in S.R. 1201, and passed it on to his son Gerontius after his death.


== Etymology ==
Fortinbras is the name of two characters in Shakespeare's Hamlet. It is French for "strong in arms".
