# Free Land

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Free_Land

"Free Land" was the political designation of the Shire from Fo.A. 6 (S.R. 1427).
Following the re-establishment of Arnor by King Elessar in T.A. 3019, the King declared that the Shire was to become a Free Land under the protection of the Northern Sceptre. Furthermore, he declared that no Men are to enter the Shire - a policy he even upheld himself.
