# Galadhon

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Galadhon

Galadhon was a noble Elf of Doriath, the son of Elmo and thus the nephew of King Thingol and Olwë. Little is known of him, except for the fact that he was father to Celeborn, later Lord of Lórien, and Galathil, father of Nimloth.


== Genealogy ==


== Etymology ==
His name is Sindarin and appears to be a combination of galadh ("tree") + -on (masculine suffix).


== Other versions of the legendarium ==
According to one late version of the legendarium, the father of Celeborn is named Gilitīro, a Telerin Elf of Aman. The name is in Telerin, meaning "starwatcher". Its Sindarin equivalent is Gildír.
