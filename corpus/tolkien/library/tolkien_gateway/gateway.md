# Gateway

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Gateway

The Gateway was a short roadway from the Great Gate of Minas Tirith that led into the Pelennor Fields. On 1 May T.A. 3012 Aragorn halted a furlong from the walls of Minas Tirith at the start of the Gateway before advancing to be crowned King of Gondor.


== Portrayal in adaptations ==
2007: The Lord of the Rings Online:

The Gateway runs for only a short distance before splitting into two roads leading towards the northern and southern gates of Rammas Echor.
