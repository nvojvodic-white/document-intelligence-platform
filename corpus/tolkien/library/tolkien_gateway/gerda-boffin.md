# Gerda Boffin

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Gerda_Boffin

Gerda Boffin was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Gerda was the second child of Uffo Boffin and Sapphira Brockhouse. She married Adalbert Bolger and had one son, Filibert.


== Etymology ==
Gerda is a name of the goddess Gerðr, an Old Norse name meaning "fenced-in".
