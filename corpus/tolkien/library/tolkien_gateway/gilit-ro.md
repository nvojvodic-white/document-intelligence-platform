# Gilitīro

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Gilit%C4%ABro

Gilitīro was a Telerin Elf of Aman and the father of Celeborn according to one late version of the legendarium.


== Etymology ==
The name Gilitīro is in Telerin, meaning "starwatcher".
Its Sindarin equivalent is Gildír.
