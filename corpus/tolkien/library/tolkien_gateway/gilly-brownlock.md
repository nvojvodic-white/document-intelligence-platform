# Gilly Brownlock

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Gilly_Brownlock

Gilly Brownlock was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Gilly married Posco Baggins, and they had several children: Ponto, Porto and Peony.
Gilly with her children and grandchildren, was present at Bilbo Baggins' Birthday Party.


== Etymology ==
Gilly is perhaps a reference to the gilliflower.
