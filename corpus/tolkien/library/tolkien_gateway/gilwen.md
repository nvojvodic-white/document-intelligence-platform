# Gilwen

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Gilwen

Gilwen (born F.A. 397) was the fourth child and third daughter of Bregor, the fifth chieftain of the House of Bëor, and the great-great-granddaughter of Bëor the Old. She had two elder sisters Bregil and Hirwen, and two brothers Bregolas the elder and Barahir the younger.
