# Ginglith

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Ginglith

The river Ginglith was a river of Beleriand and a tributary to Narog.
The Ginglith had its wells in the Woods of Núath, under the near the Ered Wethrin in northern West Beleriand. After passing by the lands of Tumhalad it met the Narog about 50 miles north of Nargothrond.
