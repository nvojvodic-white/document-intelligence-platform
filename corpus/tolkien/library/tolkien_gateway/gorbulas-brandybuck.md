# Gorbulas Brandybuck

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Gorbulas_Brandybuck

Gorbulas Brandybuck was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Gorbulas was the son of Orgulas Brandybuck, the younger son of the Master of Buckland. He had one son himself, Marmadas Brandybuck. He died some time before Bilbo's Farewell Party.


== Etymology ==
The meaning of the name Gorbulas is unknown. J.R.R. Tolkien noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely Celtic style. Jim Allan suggests that the name Gorbulas combines the first part of Gorbadoc with the second part of Orgulas.
