# Grim

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Grim

Grim was a Rider of Rohan.


== History ==
Grim settled in a dale that would later carry his name, Grimslade. It would be inhabited by him and his descendants until the days of Grimbold in the War of the Ring.


== Etymology ==
Grim in Old English means "fierce, grim".
