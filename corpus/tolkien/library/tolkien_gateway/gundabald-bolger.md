# Gundabald Bolger

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Gundabald_Bolger

Gundabald Bolger was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Gundabald was the son of Gundahad Bolger. He married Salvia Brandybuck and had one son, Theobald.


== Etymology ==
The name contains gund "battle" and bald "bold".
