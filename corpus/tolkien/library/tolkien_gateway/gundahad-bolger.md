# Gundahad Bolger

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Gundahad_Bolger

Gundahad Bolger was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Gundahad was the youngest son of Gundolpho Bolger and Alfrida of the Yale. He had one son, Gundabald.


== Etymology ==
Gundahad is an Old German name, derived from the elements gunda "war" and hadu "battle, combat".
