# Gundolpho Bolger

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Gundolpho_Bolger

Gundolpho Bolger was the patriarch of the Bolger Family.


== History ==
Gundolpho married Alfrida of the Yale and had three children: Gundahar, Rudolph and Gundahad.


== Etymology ==
Gundolpho may be an Old German name, derived from the elements gunda "war" and olpho (classicized spelling "-olf") from "wolf".


== Other versions of the legendarium ==
In drafts of the Appendix C, Tolkien at one time had Celador Bolger within the Hobbit family trees, though he was ultimately replaced by Gundolpho Bolger.
