# Gundor

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Gundor

Gundor (F.A. 419-456, aged 37) was the third child and the younger son of Hador Lórindol.  
Gundor and his father Hador fought in the Dagor Bragollach and both died, pierced with many arrows at Eithel Sirion.
