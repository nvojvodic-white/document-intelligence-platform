# Haldan

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Haldan

Haldan was the son of Haldar, grandson of Haldad, and nephew to Haleth herself, who went to Brethil where she settled her People. Haleth left no heir of her own, and so as grandson of Haldad her father, Haldan took up the lordship of the Men of Brethil.
