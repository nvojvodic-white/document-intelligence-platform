# Halfred Gamgee

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Halfred_Gamgee

Halfred Gamgee was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Halfred was the second son of Hamfast Gamgee. He moved to the Northfarthing. 


== Etymology ==
The name comes from Old English healfrēad "half-red, reddish" or from healfrǣd "half counsel".
