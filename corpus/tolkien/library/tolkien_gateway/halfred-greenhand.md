# Halfred Greenhand

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Halfred_Greenhand

Halfred Greenhand was the son of Holman the greenhanded.


== History ==
Halfred was a gardener like his father, and the first to use his father's nickname as a family name. He had one son, whom he named after his father: Holman was born in S.R. 1292.


== Etymology ==
The name comes from Old English healfrēad "half-red, reddish" or from healfrǣd "half counsel".
