# Halfred of Overhill

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Halfred_of_Overhill

Halfred Gamgee was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Halfred was the youngest son of Roper Gamgee. He settled in Overhill, and had one son, Halfast, who worked for the local Boffins.


== Etymology ==
The name comes from Old English healfrēad "half-red, reddish" or from healfrǣd "half counsel".
