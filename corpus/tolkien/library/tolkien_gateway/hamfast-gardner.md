# Hamfast Gardner

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Hamfast_Gardner

Hamfast Gardner was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Hamfast was the fourth son and seventh child of Samwise Gamgee and Rose Cotton. He was named after his grandfather, Hamfast Gamgee.


== Etymology ==
The name Hamfast comes from Old English hámfæst, meaning "stay-at-home".  In Sindarin, the name Hamfast is Baravorn.
