# Hamfast of Gamwich

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Hamfast_of_Gamwich

Hamfast of Gamwich was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Hamfast lived in Gamwich. He was the father of Wiseman, who moved to Tighfield, and as such, he was the progenitor of the Gamwich (later Gamgee) family.


== Etymology ==
The name Hamfast comes from Old English hámfæst, meaning "stay-at-home". It was a translation of Westron Ranugad.
