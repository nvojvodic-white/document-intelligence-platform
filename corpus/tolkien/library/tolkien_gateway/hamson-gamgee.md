# Hamson Gamgee

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Hamson_Gamgee

Hamson Gamgee was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Hamson was the eldest son of Hamfast Gamgee and Bell Goodchild. He moved to Tighfield to work with his uncle Andwise as a roper.


== Etymology ==
The name means "Son of Ham", referring to his father, Hamfast.
