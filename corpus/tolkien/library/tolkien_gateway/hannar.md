# Hannar

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Hannar

Hannar was one of the four Dwarves that stayed at Bag End after Bilbo Baggins' birthday party.


== History ==
In the early writings of the chapter "A Long-expected Party", Hannar was given some dialogue and a bit more development.  However, Tolkien later changed his mind, and did not disclose his name in the book nor gave him any dialogue.


== Etymology ==
Hanarr and Hannarr are dwarfs from the Dvergatal. Both their names mean "Skilled".
