# Harding of the Hill

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Harding_of_the_Hill

Harding of the Hill was a Hobbit of The Shire.


== History ==
Harding was the son of Holfast Gardner and a great-grandson of Samwise Gamgee, the hero of the War of the Ring. He became known as "of the Hill".


== Etymology ==
The name Harding comes from Old English hearding ("hard one, strong one").
