# Hatholdir

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Hatholdir

Hatholdir was a Númenórean and a descendant of the House of Hador. He was a close friend of King Tar-Meneldur. He had a son named Orchaldor who married Tar-Meneldur's oldest daughter, Ailinel.


== Etymology ==
Hatholdir is a Sindarin name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Axe-man" and is a compound of hathol ("axe") and dîr ("man").
