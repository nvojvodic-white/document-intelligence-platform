# Heribald Bolger

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Heribald_Bolger

Heribald Bolger was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Heribald was the second son of Wilibald Bolger and Prisca Baggins. He was present at Bilbo's Farewell Party.


== Etymology ==
Heribald is an Old German name, derived from the elements heri "army" and bald "bold, brave".
