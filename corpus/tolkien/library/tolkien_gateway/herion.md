# Herion

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Herion

Herion (Third Age 2037 – 2148, aged 111 years) was the third Ruling Steward of Gondor.


== History ==
He ruled for thirty-two years during the time known as the Watchful Peace, and was succeeded by his son Belegorn.


== Etymology ==
Herion is a Sindarin name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack and David Salo suggest it is a compound of hîr ("lord") and -ion ("son").
