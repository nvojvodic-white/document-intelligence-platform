# Herugar Bolger

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Herugar_Bolger

Herugar Bolger was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Herugar was the only child of Rudigar Bolger and Belba Baggins. He married Jessamine Boffin and they had one child together, Odovacar.


== Etymology ==
The name Herugar means "war-spear".
