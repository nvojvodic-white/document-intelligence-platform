# Hild

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Hild

Hild was a noble woman of Rohan.


== History ==
Hild was the daughter of King Gram, and sister of Helm Hammerhand. Her son Fréaláf took the crown after the death of Helm and his two sons.


== Etymology ==
Hild is a poetic word in Old English for "battle".
