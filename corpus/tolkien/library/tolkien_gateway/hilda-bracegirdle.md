# Hilda Bracegirdle

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Hilda_Bracegirdle

Hilda Brandybuck, née Bracegirdle was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Hilda married Seredic Brandybuck, and together they had three children: Doderic, Ilberic and Celandine. She was a guest at Bilbo's Farewell Party.
