# Hildibrand Took

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Hildibrand_Took

Hildibrand Took was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Hildibrand was a son of the Old Took. He also had a son, Sigismond, born in S.R. 1290.


== Etymology ==
The name contains hild "battle" and brand "sword". Hildebrand is a figure from German legends.
