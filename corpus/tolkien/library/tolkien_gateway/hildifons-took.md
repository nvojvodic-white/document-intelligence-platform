# Hildifons Took

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Hildifons_Took

Hildifons Took was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Hildifons was the sixth of the twelve children of the Old Took. He went on a journey outside the Shire and never returned.  When Bilbo Baggins was young he used to pester Gandalf for information about Hobbits who had "gone off", such as his uncle Hildifons.


== Etymology ==
The name contains hild "battle" and fons "ready".


== Portrayal in adaptations ==


=== Hildifons Took in adaptations ===
