# Hildigard Took

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Hildigard_Took

Hildigard Took was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Hildigard was the second child of the Old Took and Adamanta Chubb. Hildigard died at a young age.


== Etymology ==
"Hildigard" come from the Germanic roots hild meaning "battle" and gard meaning "protection".
The hobbit's gender is not mentioned (Robert Foster, The Complete Guide to Middle-earth for example treats Hildigard as a male hobbit), however Hildegard is a Germanic female name.
