# Hildigrim Took

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Hildigrim_Took

Hildigrim Took was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Hildigrim was the third son of the Old Took. He married Rosa Baggins, with whom he had one son, Adalgrim.


== Etymology ==
"Hildigrim" is of Germanic origin, coming from hild meaning "battle", and grim meaning "fierce".
