# Hiril (daughter of Barahir)

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Hiril_(daughter_of_Barahir)

Hiril was the name of Beren's sister in a later emendation to the genealogical table of the House of Bëor. Nothing else is known about her.


== Etymology ==
Hiril means "lady" in Sindarin (Hîr + -il).
