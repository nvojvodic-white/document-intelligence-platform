# Hiril

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Hiril

Hiril was the younger daughter of Halmir, third Halad or warden of the Haladin. She was the sister of Haldir, Hareth, and Hundar. She was married to Enthor and she bore him a daughter Meleth.


== Etymology ==
Hiril means "lady" in Sindarin (Hîr + -il).
