# Hirwen

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Hirwen

Hirwen (born F.A. 389) was the second child and second daughter of Bregor the fifth chieftain of the House of Bëor, and the great-great-granddaughter of Bëor the Old. Bregolas and Barahir were her two younger brothers, Bregil was her elder sister, and Gilwen was her younger sister.
