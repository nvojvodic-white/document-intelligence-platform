# Holdwine

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Holdwine

Holdwine of the Shire was the name given to Meriadoc Brandybuck among the Rohirrim by King Éomer, and used by them to record his deeds in their annals. 


== Etymology ==
The word is Old English but its meaning is never given explicitly; and there are several possible interpretations, of which one is "faithful friend". The name Holdwine is composed of the Old English elements hold ("faithful" or "loyal") and wine ("friend").
