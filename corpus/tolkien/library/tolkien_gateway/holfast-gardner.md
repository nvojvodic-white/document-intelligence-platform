# Holfast Gardner

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Holfast_Gardner

Holfast Gardner was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Holfast was the son of Frodo Gardner and became the head of the Gardner Family. He was the father of Harding of the Hill. 
He probably lived in Bag End.


== Etymology ==
Old English holfæst means "hole-firm" or "hole dweller".
