# Horn of the Mark

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Horn_of_the_Mark

The Horn of the Mark was a silver horn made by the Dwarves. It was among their treasure that the Long-worm Scatha took from them.
When Fram killed Scatha and took the horn, his hoard became a matter of dispute between the Éothéod and the Dwarves. Nonetheless, the Horn was taken by the Éothéod and apparently became an heirloom of the House of Eorl, even when they settled Rohan.
More than a thousand years later, at the end of the War of the Ring, Lady Éowyn gave the Horn to Holdwine (Meriadoc Brandybuck) as a farewell gift for his services to Rohan.
On their return to the Shire, Merry used the Horn to sound the Horn-call of Buckland on 2 November 3019, rousing the Shire-folk against the Ruffians who had taken control of the Shire.
In commemoration of those events and the liberation of the Shire, the Horn was sounded ceremonially every year at sundown on 2 November in Buckland, commencing celebrations with bonfires and feasts.
