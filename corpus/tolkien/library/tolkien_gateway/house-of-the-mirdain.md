# House of the Mírdain

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/House_of_the_M%C3%ADrdain

The House of the Mírdain was the guildhouse and chief dwelling of Celebrimbor and the jewelsmiths of Eregion - the Gwaith-i-Mírdain. It contained the smithies and the treasures of the Mírdain. It was destroyed by the forces of Sauron in S.A. 1697 when he invaded Eriador.


== Portrayal in adaptations ==
2022: The Lord of the Rings Online:

The House of the Mírdain stood at the center of Caras Gelebren. Its fall is depicted in a flashback, where the players play the parts of the defenders.
