# Hugo Bracegirdle

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Hugo_Bracegirdle

Hugo Bracegirdle was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Hugo was the eldest child of Bruno Bracegirdle, born in S.R. 1350. In his lifetime, he was notorious for borrowing books from Bilbo Baggins, though he rarely returned them. In S.R. 1401 he was a guest at Bilbo's Farewell Party, after which Bilbo left Hugo an empty bookcase in which he could store all those books.


== Inspiration ==
J.R.R. Tolkien likened himself to Hugo Bracegirdle.


== Portrayal in adaptations ==
2002: The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game):

Hugo lives at Number Eight, Bagshot Row, a large house overlooking Sandyman's mill.
