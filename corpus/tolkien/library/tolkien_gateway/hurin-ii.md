# Húrin II

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/H%C3%BArin_II

Húrin II (Third Age 2515 – 2628, aged 113 years) was the fourteenth Ruling Steward of Gondor.


== History ==
Húrin ruled Gondor as its Steward for twenty-three years, and was succeeded by his son, Belecthor I.


== Etymology ==
For the meaning of his name refer to Húrin of Emyn Arnen - Etymology. Húrin II was probably named after the hero of the First Age Húrin Thalion, the Lord of Dor-lómin.
