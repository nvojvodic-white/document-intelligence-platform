# Hyarmendacil II

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Hyarmendacil_II

Hyarmendacil II, born with the name Vinyarion (T.A. 1391 - T.A. 1621), was the twenty-fourth King of Gondor.


== History ==
Vinyarion ascended the throne after his father Aldamir was killed in a battle with the Harad and the Corsairs of Umbar in T.A. 1540. Eleven years later, in T.A. 1551, he defeated the Men of the Harad and took the name Hyarmendacil II.


== Etymology ==
Vinyarion is a Quenya name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests that it means "New Son" and is a compound of vinya ("new") and the suffix ion ("son").
Hyarmendacil is a Quenya name. It means "South-victor". It is a compound of hyarmen ("south") and -(n)dacil ("victor").

== Other versions of the legendarium ==
