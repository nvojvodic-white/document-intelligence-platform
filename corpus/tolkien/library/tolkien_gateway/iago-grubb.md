# Iago Grubb

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Iago_Grubb

Iago Grubb was one of Frodo's lawyers in The Return of the Shadow (at that stage of the writing Frodo was called Bingo). Iago, along with Bingo's other lawyer Folco Burrowes, eject Sancho Proudfoot as he tries to excavate the pantry at Bilbo's Farewell Party.
