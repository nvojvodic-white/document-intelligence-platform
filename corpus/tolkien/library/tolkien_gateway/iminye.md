# Iminyë

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Iminy%C3%AB

Iminyë was the first spouse to the Elf-fathers to be awakened at the bay of Cuiviénen. She lay by Imin, the first elf to be awakened. Iminyë later married Imin. She was one of the Three Elderwomen of the Elves, who Oromë taught the art of making lembas.
