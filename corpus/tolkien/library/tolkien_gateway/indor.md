# Indor

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Indor

Indor was a Man of Dor-lómin and the father of Aerin in the First Age.


== Genealogy ==


== Other versions of the legendarium ==
In the earliest versions of the legendarium, Indor was the father of Peleg, in turn the father of Tuor. Therefore like Tuor he probably belonged to the House of the Swan. In The Silmarillion Tuor's grandfather is Galdor the Tall.
