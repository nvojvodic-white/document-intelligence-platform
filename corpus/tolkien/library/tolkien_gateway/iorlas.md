# Iorlas

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Iorlas

Iorlas was a man of Gondor.


== History ==
Iorlas was an uncle of Bergil, either Beregond's brother or brother-in-law. When Pippin mentioned to Bergil he was 29 years old, this surprised him, as his uncle Iorlas was also 29, and nigh twice as tall as the Hobbit.


== Etymology ==
Iorlas could either mean "Old Leaf" or "Old Joy" in Sindarin.

2003: The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King:

A card from The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game identifies one of the Gondorians near the gate of Minas Tirith as Iorlas.

Iorlas is Beregond's brother and is first found  on the seventh level of pre-battle Minas Tirith. During the siege of the city he is struck by a fallen stone, but is protected by his helmet, suffering only what he calls a "dizziness".
