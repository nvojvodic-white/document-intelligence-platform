# Isembard Took

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Isembard_Took

Isembard Took was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Isembard was the seventh child of Gerontius Took and Adamanta Chubb. He had one son, Flambard Took, born in S.R. 1287.


== Etymology ==
The name means "iron battle-axe" (isen + bard).
