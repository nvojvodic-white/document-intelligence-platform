# Isembold Took

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Isembold_Took

Isembold Took was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Isembold was the fifth child of Gerontius Took and Adamanta Chubb. He had many descendants, and died in S.R. 1346.


== Etymology ==
The name contains isen "iron" and bold "bold".
