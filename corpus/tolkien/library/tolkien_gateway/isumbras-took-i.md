# Isumbras Took I

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Isumbras_Took_I

Isumbras Took was a the thirteenth Thain of the Shire.


== History ==
Isumbras was elected Thain in S.R. 740, after Gorhendad Oldbuck relocated to Buckland. From then on, the Thain was a title held by the Took Family.


== Etymology ==
"Isumbras" is of French and Anglo-Saxon origin and means "iron-arm". It comes from the French bras meaning "arm", and the Anglo-Saxon and Middle High German word isen which means "iron".
Tolkien likely got the name from Sir Isumbras, an English medieval romance about a man who suffers a lifetime of hardship but who finds happiness in his latter years.
