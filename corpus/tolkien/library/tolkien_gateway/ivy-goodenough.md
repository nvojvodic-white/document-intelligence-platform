# Ivy Goodenough

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Ivy_Goodenough

Ivy Boffin, née Goodenough, was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Ivy was the wife of Buffo Boffin and mother of Bosco (S.R. 1167-S.R. 1258), Basso (born S.R. 1169), Briffo (born S.R. 1170), and Berylla (born S.R. 1172).
