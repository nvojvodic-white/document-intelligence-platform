# Jessamine Boffin

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Jessamine_Boffin

Jessamine Bolger, née Boffin, was a Hobbit of the Shire. 


== History ==
Jessamine was the daughter of Hugo Boffin and Donnamira Took. She married Herugar Bolger, and had one child, Odovacar.


== Etymology ==
Jessamine is a fragrant plant. The name is exotic but comes to English through French.
