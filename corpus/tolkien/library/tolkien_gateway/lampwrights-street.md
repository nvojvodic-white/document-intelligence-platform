# Lampwrights' Street

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Lampwrights%27_Street

Lampwrights' Street, or Rath Celerdain in Sindarin, was a street on the lowest tier of Minas Tirith. Other than the famed Old Guesthouse, it housed the fabricators of the city's many lamps. 


== Portrayal in adaptations ==
2003: The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King:

One of the streets in the Minas Tirith set was christened the Lampwrights' Street. It had a sign of a lamp in framework, and according to Grant Major it housed the metalworkers of the city.
