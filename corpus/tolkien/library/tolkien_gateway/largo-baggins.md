# Largo Baggins

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Largo_Baggins

Largo Baggins was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Largo was a son of Balbo Baggins. He married Tanta Hornblower, and had one child, Fosco.
The last years of his life the Shire suffered from the Fell Winter and around that time he lost his elder brother Ponto. Largo died the next year, as did his younger sister Lily.
