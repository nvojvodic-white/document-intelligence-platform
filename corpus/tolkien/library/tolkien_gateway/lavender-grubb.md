# Lavender Grubb

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Lavender_Grubb

Lavender Grubb was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Lavender was the sister of Laura Grubb and married Otto Boffin. They had four children: Hugo, Uffo, Rollo and Primrose.


== Etymology ==
Lavender is a fair-scented purple flower.
