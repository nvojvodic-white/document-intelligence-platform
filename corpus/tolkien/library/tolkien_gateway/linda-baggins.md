# Linda Baggins

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Linda_Baggins

Linda Proudfoot, née Baggins, was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Linda was the fourth child of Mungo Baggins and Laura Grubb. She married Bodo Proudfoot and was the mother of Odo.


== Portrayal in adaptations ==
2003: The Hobbit (2003 video game):

Five portraits are clustered on a wall in Bag End. One of them is Linda, and one of Bilbo's lines when looking at the portraits is "That's my aunt Linda when she was a child".
