# Longo Baggins

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Longo_Baggins

Longo Baggins was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Longo was the third child and second son of Mungo Baggins and Laura Grubb. He married Camellia Sackville, the daughter of the Sackville Family patriarch. In S.R. 1310, their son Otho Sackville-Baggins was born.
