# Lóni

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/L%C3%B3ni

Lóni was a dwarf of Erebor.


== History ==
Lóni was part of Balin's Colony to reclaim Moria. Their mission at first seemed successful, and Balin went so far as to claim the title Lord of Moria. This all changed in their fifth year there. On November 10, the Orcs came up the Silverlode and killed Balin while he was alone, unaware, and looking into Mirrormere. The Dwarves fought back but eventually lost East-gate and First Hall. 
The Orcs made it across the Bridge of Khazad-dûm, there was a battle in the Second Hall, and Lóni and his companions Frár and Náli died bravely while the rest of the colonists retreated.


== Etymology ==
Lóni is a dwarf from the Dvergatal. His name most likely means "lazy person".
