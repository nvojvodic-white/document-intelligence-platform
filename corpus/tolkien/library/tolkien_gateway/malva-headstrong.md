# Malva Headstrong

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Malva_Headstrong

Malva Headstrong was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Malva married Gormadoc Brandybuck. They had three children: Madoc, Sadoc and Marroc.


== Etymology ==
Malva is Latin for "mallow" and is a genus of plants with pink or yellow flowers.
