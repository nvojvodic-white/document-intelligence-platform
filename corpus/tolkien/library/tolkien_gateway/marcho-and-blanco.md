# Marcho and Blanco

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Marcho_and_Blanco

Marcho and Blanco were brothers,  Bree-hobbits of Fallohide stock who founded the Shire in Third Age 1601.


== History ==
In T.A. 1601, the two brothers led a group of mostly Harfoot hobbits westwards from Bree. They crossed the river Baranduin at the Bridge of Stonebows, and with the permission of King Argeleb II they settled in the royal hunting grounds of Arthedain and founded the Shire. 
Soon after (c. T.A. 1630) they are joined by Stoors, who come up from Dunland and settle in the Shire.


== Etymology ==
Marcho contains the Old English element *marh ("horse") and Blanco is derived from Old English blanca ("white horse", "grey horse").


== Inspiration ==
The two brothers are perhaps based on Hengest and Horsa, who brought the Angles, Saxons and Jutes to England. It is noteworthy that both hengest and horsa mean "stallion" and "horse", respectively, in Old English.


== Other versions of the legendarium ==
In an earlier typescript version of the Prologue and in an earlier manuscript version of the family tree of the Baggins family the two Hobbit brothers whe founded the Shire were called Marco and Cavallo.
