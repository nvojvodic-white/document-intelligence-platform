# Marmadas Brandybuck

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Marmadas_Brandybuck

Marmadas Brandybuck was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Marmadas was the son of Gorbulas Brandybuck. His three children were Merimas, Mentha and Melilot. He and his children attended Bilbo's Farewell Party.


== Etymology ==
The meaning of the name Marmadas is unknown. J.R.R. Tolkien noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely Celtic style.
Jim Allan suggests that the name Marmadas is a varation of the name Marmadoc with the ending -as like in in the names Merimas and Saradas.
