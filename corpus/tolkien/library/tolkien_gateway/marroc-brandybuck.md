# Marroc Brandybuck

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Marroc_Brandybuck

Marroc Brandybuck was a Hobbit of Buckland.


== History ==
Marroc was the youngest son of Gormadoc Brandybuck and Malva Headstrong.  He had many descendants.


== Etymology ==
The meaning of the name Marroc is unknown. J.R.R. Tolkien noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely Celtic style.
A Kngiht of the Round Table named Marrok is mentioned in Malory's Le Morte Darthure.
