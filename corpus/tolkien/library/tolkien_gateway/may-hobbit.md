# May (hobbit)

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/May_(hobbit)

May was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
May was a daughter of Roper Gamgee.


== Etymology ==
May could refer to the hawthorn blossoms, so called because they blossom in the month May. It was a common girl's name in the Shire.
