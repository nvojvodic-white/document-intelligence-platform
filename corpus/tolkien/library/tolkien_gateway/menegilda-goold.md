# Menegilda Goold

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Menegilda_Goold

Menegilda Brandybuck, née Goold, was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Menegilda married Rorimac Brandybuck, and with him produced two children, Saradoc and Merimac. 
Unlike her husband and sons, she was not present at Bilbo Baggins' Birthday Party in S.R. 1401.


== Etymology ==
The name contains meneg "much" and hild "battle".
