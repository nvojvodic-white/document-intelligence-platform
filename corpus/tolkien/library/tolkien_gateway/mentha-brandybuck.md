# Mentha Brandybuck

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Mentha_Brandybuck

Mentha Brandybuck was a Hobbit of Buckland.


== History ==
Mentha was the second child of Marmadas Brandybuck. She was present at Bilbo's Birthday Party.


== Etymology ==
Mentha is the Latin name of the genus "mint".
