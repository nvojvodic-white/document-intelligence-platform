# Meril (mother of Gil-galad)

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Meril_(mother_of_Gil-galad)

Meril was the wife of Felagund and the mother of Gil-galad, according to a version of the legendarium following the completion of The Lord of the Rings in c. 1950.
She was sent away by Felagund along their son Gil-galad (still a child) to her own folk in Eglorest during the Dagor Bragollach, due to Felagund fearing "that all strong places were doomed to fall at last before the might of Morgoth".


== Etymology ==
The name Meril is the Sindarin word for rose used as a name.
