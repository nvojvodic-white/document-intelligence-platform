# Merimac Brandybuck

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Merimac_Brandybuck

Merimac Brandybuck was a Hobbit of Buckland.


== History ==
Merimac was the youngest son of Rorimac Brandybuck and Menegilda Goold. He had one son, Berilac, and they were both present at Bilbo's Farewell Party.


== Etymology ==
The meaning of the name Merimac is unknown. J.R.R. Tolkien noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely Celtic style.
The name Merimac is, like most male Brandybuck names, Celtic in sound, but apparently meaningless. The first element, Meri also occurrs in the name Meriadoc. Mac is a Celtic word meaning "son", though it is unknown whether Tolkien used it with that meaning in mind.
The entire name also resembles Merrimac, a name of a river and several towns in the United States. That word is of Native American origin.
