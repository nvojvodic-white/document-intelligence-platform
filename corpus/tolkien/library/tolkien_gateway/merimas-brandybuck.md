# Merimas Brandybuck

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Merimas_Brandybuck

Merimas Brandybuck was a Hobbit of Buckland.


== History ==
Merimas was the eldest child, and only son, of Marmadas Brandybuck. He was present at Bilbo's Birthday Party.


== Etymology ==
The meaning of the name Merimas is unknown. J.R.R. Tolkien noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely Celtic style.
Jim Allan suggests that Merimas is a variation of the name Merimac with an -as ending.
The first element, Meri also occurrs in Meriadoc.


== Portrayal in adaptations ==
2007: The Lord of the Rings Online:

Merimas Brandybuck can be found in Buckland. He occupies the role of a crafting vendor in the game and plays no role in the storyline.
