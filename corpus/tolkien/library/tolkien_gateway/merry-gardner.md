# Merry Gardner

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Merry_Gardner

Merry Gardner was a Hobbit of the Shire, the second son and fourth child of Samwise Gamgee and Rose Cotton.


== History ==
At the suggestion of Frodo Baggins, Sam named one of his children after Merry Brandybuck. Merry was born in S.R. 1427.


== Etymology ==
Merry is a translation of Hobbitish Westron (specifically, the Buckland dialect) Kali, meaning "jolly, gay".  In Sindarin, the name Merry is Gelir.
