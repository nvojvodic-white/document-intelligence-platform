# Methiriad

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Methiriad

Methiriad, also known as Mid-Beleriand, was a region of West Beleriand that was located between Doriath and the river Narog.
Methiriad consisted of the following locations:

Dor-Cúarthol
Falls of Sirion
Gates of Sirion
Moors of the Nibin-noeg
Nan-tathren
Nivrim
Talath Dirnen
Vales of Sirion
According to Christopher Tolkien, both of these names only appear on the The Second 'Silmarillion' Map.


== Etymology ==
Methiriad is a Sindarin word, but it is not glossed. As Paul Strack explains, it could mean "End of Two Rivers", from Meth ("last") + a mutated form of Sîr ("river, stream") + -ad ("dual").
