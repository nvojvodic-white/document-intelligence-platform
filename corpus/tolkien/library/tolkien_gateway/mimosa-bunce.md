# Mimosa Bunce

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Mimosa_Bunce

Mimosa Bunce was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Mimosa married Ponto Baggins. They had two children; Rosa was born in S.R. 1256, and Polo Baggins shortly thereafter.


== Etymology ==
Mimosa is a genus of plants (trees, shrubs and herbs) of the legume family.
