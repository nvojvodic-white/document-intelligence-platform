# Mirabella Took

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Mirabella_Took

Mirabella Brandybuck, née Took, was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Mirabella was one of the Old Took's many children, and his youngest daughter. She married Gorbadoc Brandybuck, and had seven children: Rorimac, Amaranth, Saradas, Dodinas, Asphodel, Dinodas and Primula.


== Etymology ==
The name is French and refers to the palm tree fruit.
The name participates in a circular wordplay with the names of her sisters, Donnamira and Belladonna, all Italian names.
