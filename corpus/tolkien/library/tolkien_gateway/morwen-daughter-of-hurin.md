# Morwen (daughter of Húrin)

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Morwen_(daughter_of_H%C3%BArin)

Morwen was the daughter of Húrin (Warden of the Keys), in the drafts of the Appendices. She was to be wed to Éomer, but her character was ultimately abandoned and replaced with that of Lothíriel.
