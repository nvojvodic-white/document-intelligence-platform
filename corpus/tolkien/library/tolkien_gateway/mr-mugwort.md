# Mr. Mugwort

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Mr._Mugwort

Mr. Mugwort was a hobbit of Bree.


== History ==
During Frodo Baggins' stay at The Prancing Pony, Mr. Mugwort witnessed Frodo put on The One Ring and dissapear, and in the ensuing confusion he got into an argument with the innkeeper Barliman Butterbur, not quite believing what he had just seen.
