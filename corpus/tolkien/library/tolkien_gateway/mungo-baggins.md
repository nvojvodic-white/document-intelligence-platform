# Mungo Baggins

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Mungo_Baggins

Mungo Baggins was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Mungo was the eldest child of Balbo Baggins and Berylla Boffin. He married Laura Grubb, and they had five children together: Bungo, Belba, Longo, Linda and Bingo.
