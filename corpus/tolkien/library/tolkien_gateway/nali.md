# Náli

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/N%C3%A1li

Náli was a dwarf of Erebor.


== History ==
Náli was part of Balin's Colony to reclaim Moria. Their mission at first seemed successful, and Balin went so far as to claim the title Lord of Moria. This all changed in their fifth year there. On November 10th, the Orcs came up the Silverlode and killed Balin while he was alone, unaware, and looking into Mirrormere. The Dwarves fought back but  eventually lost East-gate and First Hall. 
The Orcs made it across the Bridge of Khazad-dûm and there was a battle in the Second Hall, and Náli and his companions Frár and Lóni died bravely while the rest of the colonists retreated.


== Etymology ==
Náli is a dwarf from the Dvergatal. His name most likely means "axle of a hand-mill".
