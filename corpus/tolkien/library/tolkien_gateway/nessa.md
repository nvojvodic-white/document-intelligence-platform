# Nessa

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Nessa

Nessa was the wife of Tulkas and sister of Oromë. In "due order," Nessa was the last named Valië. 


== History ==
Nessa was noted for her speed and was said to be "swift as an arrow" and she was able to outrun the deer who followed her in the wild. She was also renowned for her dancing ability, as she danced on the ever-green lawns of Valimar. She wed Tulkas upon the Isle of Almaren in the Year of the Valar 3400.

== Etymology ==
Nessa ([ˈnesːa]) is a Quenya word meaning "young"; in the same text, Nessa has also the name Indis "bride".:14 
The Noldorin names of Nessa were Neth (a cognate of the Quenya/Qenya word) or Dineth, a Noldorin word meaning "bride", related to the Quenya word indis.
In a later text, Pengolodh notes that Nessa's name is "not elvish" and probably represents a Valarin title, much like the name of her spouse, adopted in Quenya, but there is no record.


== Other versions of the legendarium ==


=== The Book of Lost Tales ===
In The Book of Lost Tales, Nessa was one of the Valarindi, daughter of Aulë and Yavanna. Her son with Tulkas was Telimektar.
Nessa was seen at the house of Tulkas bringing goblets of wine and drinks to his champions.:75
Nessa loved to retire to the green swards which her brother, Oromë, had cultivated from the richness of his forest.:75 When creating the forests of Oromë, Yavanna had planted forest glades with spells so that the glades would always remain green and smooth. Nessa, along with her maidens, would dance there as long as Laurelin was in bloom, and she was said to be an even better dancer than Vána.


=== The Annals of Aman ===
In revisions to The Annals of Aman "Nessa the Young" was removed from the ranks of the Valar and, along with Estë, made to be among the highest of the Maiar.:§3
In further emendations, Tolkien split the character between "Nessa the Evermaid", and "Lëa the Young" ("Lëa-vinya") who was the bride of Tulkas.:§3 Lëa was the fairest of the maidens of Yavanna.:§18


=== Early etymology ===
Her other names in Qenya were Helinyetille meaning "Eyes of Heartsease", from helin ("violet, pansy"), and Melesta, from mele ("to love").
