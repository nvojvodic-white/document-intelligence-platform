# Nina Lightfoot

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Nina_Lightfoot

Nina Lightfoot was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Nina was the wife of Theobald Bolger and the mother of Wilibald.


== Etymology ==
The name Nina was meaningless in contemporary Hobbitish Westron. It represented an original name Nino or Nine.
