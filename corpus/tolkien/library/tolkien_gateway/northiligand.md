# Northiligand

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Northiligand

Northiligand was the lord of the people of the Delta in the early drafts of The Lord of the Rings. He is mentioned in a sketch alongside Benrodir and Inram as one of the leaders of Gondor that came to the aid of Minas Tirith during the War of the Ring. Nothing else is known about him, as he appears nowhere else in the legendarium.


== Etymology ==
Earlier, his name was spelled Nosdiligand, though Christopher Tolkien mentioned that a few of the letters are illegible.
According to Eldamo, the name Nosdiligand is in Noldorin, but its meaning is unclear.
