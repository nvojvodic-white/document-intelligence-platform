# Odovacar Bolger

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Odovacar_Bolger

Odovacar Bolger (S.R. 1336—S.R. 1431) was a hobbit of the Shire. 


== History ==
Odovacar was the son of Herugar Bolger and Jessamine Boffin. He became head of the family after his father's death. Odovacar married Rosamunda Took, and had two children: Fredegar and Estella. They were all present at Bilbo's Farewell Party.


== Etymology ==
Odovacar contains aud "wealth" and vacar "watchful". It was the name of a legendary germanic king of Rome.


== Portrayal in adaptations ==
2007: The Lord of the Rings Online:

Odovacar Bolger is a pig farmer living in Budgeford. He asks the player to investigate the rumour of a Black Rider seen at his farm at night, but it turns out to be simply another hobbit playing pranks.
