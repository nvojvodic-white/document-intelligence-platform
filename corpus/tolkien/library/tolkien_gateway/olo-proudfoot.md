# Olo Proudfoot

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Olo_Proudfoot

Olo Proudfoot (S.R. 1346—S.R. 1435) was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Olo was the son of Odo Proudfoot, and father of Sancho. Together, they attended Bilbo Baggins's Farewell Party.


== Portrayal in adaptations ==
2007: The Lord of the Rings Online:

Olo Proudfoot owns a farm between Hobbiton and Bywater. He occupies the role of a crafting vendor in the game and plays no role in the storyline.
