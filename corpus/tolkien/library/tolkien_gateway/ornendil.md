# Ornendil

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Ornendil

Ornendil was the older son of King Eldacar of Gondor. 


== History ==
In T.A. 1432 Ornendil's grandfather, King Valacar died and the civil war of the Kin-strife began, because some of the Dúnedain, especially in the southern provinces refused to accept Eldacar as their new king, who had been born in an alien country and whose mother Vidumavi was perceived by them to be of a lesser and alien race. Castamir the Usurper, one of the leaders of the rebels against Eldacar, besieged Osgiliath, which Eldacar held for a long time. In T.A. 1437 the forces of Castamir took and burnt Osgiliath. Though Eldacar managed to escape to his kinfolk in Rhovanion, Ornendil was captured, and put to death by Castamir. Ten years later, in T.A. 1447, Eldacar managed to avenge his son's death by killing Castamir in the Battle of the Crossings of Erui.


== Etymology ==
Ornendil is a Quenya name. It means "Lover of Trees". It is a compound of orne ("tree") and -(n)dil ("lover", "friend").
