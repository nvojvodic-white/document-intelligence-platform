# Ornil

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Ornil

Ornil was an Elf of Nargothrond who met with Túrin after the Battle of Tumhalad.


== History ==
After Gwindor's death during the Battle of Tumhalad, Túrin rushed back to Nargothrond, gathering any survivors of the battle in the process, including Ornil. As leaves were falling from trees in a great wind, Ornil compared the changing of the seasons to the Fall of Nargothrond, causing Túrin's pace to hasten. Nothing else is known about Ornil.


== Etymology ==
The name Ornil is in Sindarin. While its meaning is not entirely clear, according to Eldamo it might contain the element orn ("tree").
