# Orod-na-Thôn

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Orod-na-Th%C3%B4n

Orod-na-Thôn ("Pine-mountain" or "Mount of the Pine Tree(s)") was a mountain in Dorthonion. It is mentioned by Treebeard in his song In the Willow-meads of Tasarinan:

Ah! the wind and the whiteness and the black branches of Winter upon Orod-na-Thôn!
It has also been assumed that Orod-na-Thôn is a synonym for Dorthonion 'Land of Pines', but Tolkien states clearly in his unfinished index of place-names occurring in The Lord of the Rings that it is a separate mountain in this region.


== Etymology ==
Elements of the Sindarin name are:

orod 'mountain'
-na- 'with' in sense of possessing
thôn 'pine'
