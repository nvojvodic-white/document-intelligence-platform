# Otto Boffin

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Otto_Boffin

Otto Boffin, colloquially known as Otto the Fat, was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Otto was the only child of Bosco Boffin. He married Lavender Grubb and had four children: Hugo, Uffo, Rollo, and Primrose.
