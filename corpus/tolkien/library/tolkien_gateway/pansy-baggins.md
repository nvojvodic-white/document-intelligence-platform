# Pansy Baggins

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Pansy_Baggins

Pansy Bolger, née Baggins, was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Pansy was the daughter of Balbo Baggins and Berylla Boffin. She married Fastolph Bolger, and had various descendants.


== Etymology ==
Pansy is a small garden plant of the violet family. The word derives from French pensée "she who is thought".
