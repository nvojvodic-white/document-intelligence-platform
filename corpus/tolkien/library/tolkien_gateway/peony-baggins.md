# Peony Baggins

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Peony_Baggins

Peony Baggins (born S.R. 1350) was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Peony was a daughter of Posco Baggins and Gilly Brownlock, the younger sister of Ponto and Porto. She married Milo Burrows and they had several children: Mosco, Moro, Myrtle and Minto. With her family she attended Bilbo's Farewell Party.


== Etymology ==
Peony is a genus of the buttercup family of perennial plants with flowers.
