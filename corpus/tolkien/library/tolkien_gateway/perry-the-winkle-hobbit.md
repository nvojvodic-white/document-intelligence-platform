# Perry-the-Winkle (hobbit)

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Perry-the-Winkle_(hobbit)

Perry-the-Winkle was a Hobbit of Michel Delving, and the main character of the hobbit-poem Perry-the-Winkle.
In the poem, Perry was the only hobbit who treated the Lonely Troll kindly, and in him the Troll found the friend he always wanted; the Troll often fed him and taught him how to be a great cook.


== Other versions of the legendarium ==
The poem Perry-the-Winkle is a revision of a earlier story named The Bumpus. In The Bumpus, Perry is named William "Bill" Winkle. 


== Etymology ==
Periwinckle is a marine mollusc and a creeping plant.
