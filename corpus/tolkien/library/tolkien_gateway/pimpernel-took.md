# Pimpernel Took

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Pimpernel_Took

Pimpernel Took was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Pimpernel was the second child of Thain Paladin Took II and Eglantine Banks. She and her family were guests at Bilbo's Farewell Party.


== Etymology ==
Pimpernel is a genus of the primrose family and its fruits resemble peppercorns. The name derives from Latin pimpernella meaning "little pepper".
