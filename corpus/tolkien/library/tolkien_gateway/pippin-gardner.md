# Pippin Gardner

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Pippin_Gardner

Pippin Gardner was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Pippin was the third son and fifth child of Samwise Gamgee and Rose Cotton, born in S.R. 1429. He was named after Pippin Took at the suggestion of Frodo Baggins.


== Etymology ==
Pippin is a translation of Razan, a Westron word for a small type of apple.  In Sindarin, the name Pippin is Cordof.
