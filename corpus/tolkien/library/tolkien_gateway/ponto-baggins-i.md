# Ponto Baggins I

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Ponto_Baggins_I

Ponto Baggins was a Hobbit of The Shire.


== History ==
Ponto was the middle child of Balbo and Berylla Baggins. He married Mimosa Bunce, with whom he had two children: Rosa and Polo.
Ponto died in S.R. 1311, the year when the Shire suffered from the Fell Winter.
