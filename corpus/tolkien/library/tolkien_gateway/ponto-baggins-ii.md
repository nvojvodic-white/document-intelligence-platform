# Ponto Baggins II

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Ponto_Baggins_II

Ponto Baggins II was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Ponto was the eldest son of Posco Baggins and Gilly Brownlock and was named after his great-grandfather, Ponto. He was the father of Angelica and they attended the Birthday Party together.
After Frodo Baggins left the Shire in S.R. 1422, Ponto "presumably" became the head of the Baggins Family, though in title only, as Bag End had passed to Samwise Gamgee.


== Portrayal in adaptations ==
2002: The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game):

Ponto Baggins lives on Number 2, Bywater Road, the first house in Bywater.
