# Poppy Chubb-Baggins

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Poppy_Chubb-Baggins

Poppy Chubb-Baggins was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Poppy was the daughter of Falco Chubb-Baggins. She married Filibert Bolger. She was a guest at Bilbo's Farewell Party. 


== Etymology ==
Poppy is a common family of annual and perennial plants. The name derives from Old English popæg, ultimately from Latin papaver.
