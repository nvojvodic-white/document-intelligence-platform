# Posco Baggins

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Posco_Baggins

Posco Baggins was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Posco was the son of Polo Baggins and a cousin of Bilbo Baggins. He married Gilly Brownlock, and together they had several children: Ponto, Porto, Peony. While his family and descendants attended his cousin's Bilbo's Farewell Party in S.R. 1401, he did not.
