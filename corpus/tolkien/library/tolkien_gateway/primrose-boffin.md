# Primrose Boffin

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Primrose_Boffin

Primrose Bracegirdle, née Boffin was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Primrose was the daughter of Otto Boffin and Lavender Grubb. She married Blanco Bracegirdle and had two children, Bruno in S.R. 1313 and Lobelia in S.R. 1318.


== Etymology ==
Primrose (from Latin primula and English rose) are plants of the primula genus of the primrose family.
