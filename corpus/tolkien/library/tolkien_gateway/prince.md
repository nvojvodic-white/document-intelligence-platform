# Prince

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Prince

Prince, or Ernil in Sindarin, was a hereditary title conferred by the kings of Gondor.


== History ==
The title was first given by Elendil to a house of Númenórean nobles, to whom he was related, that had settled in Belfalas in or near to Edhellond. Some two thousand years later, after the drowning of Amroth, Galador was the first of this line to use the title Prince of Dol Amroth.
In T.A. 3019 King Elessar of Gondor created the title Prince of Ithilien and gave it to Faramir, the last Ruling Steward.
Peregrin Took was also informally known as the Prince of the Halflings by the people of Minas Tirith during his time there.
