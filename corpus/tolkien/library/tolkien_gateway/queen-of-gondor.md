# Queen of Gondor

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Queen_of_Gondor

Queen of Gondor was the title given to the spouse of the ruling King of Gondor (no Queen ruled Gondor in her own name at any time during its recorded history). The most famous Queens were Berúthiel (Queen to King Tarannon Falastur) and Arwen Evenstar, the consort of Aragorn Elessar.
