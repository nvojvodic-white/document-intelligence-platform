# Ragnir (servant)

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Ragnir_(servant)

Ragnir was a blind servant of Morwen in Húrin's house in Dor-lómin.


== History ==
When Morwen informed her young son Túrin that it was time for him to leave Dor-lómin he objected, and part of his objection was that his mother would be left with only old women and two male servants, Sador who was lame and Ragnir who was blind.  Nevertheless, Túrin did leave in fulfillment of his mother's command.
