# Ragnor

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Ragnor

Ragnor (died F.A. 460) was a one of the twelve loyal Bëorings who followed Barahir as outlaws.


== History ==
Ragnor fought in the Dagor Bragollach under the command of Barahir. He was part of a small group of men loyal to their lord that survived. The survivors formed a small band and lived as outlaws in Dorthonion, near Tarn Aeluin. 
They lived in hiding for some time, until Morgoth's agents captured Gorlim and coerced him into giving the location of their hideaway. Orcs were sent to raid it, and succeeded. Ragnor and the other outlaws were killed. Only Beren survived, as he had been away on an errand.
