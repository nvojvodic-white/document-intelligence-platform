# Ras Mewrim

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Ras_Mewrim

Ras Mewrim was a headland on the coastline south-west of Eglarest, in the land of the Falas in Beleriand. The name only appears in the later additions to the Second 'Silmarillion' Map.
Bar-in-Mŷl, the "Home of the Gulls", was another name for the headland in the essay "Quendi and Eldar". The name was first typed Bar-in-Gwael, but Tolkien later changed it to Bar-in-Mŷl and added the translation.


== Etymology ==
The Sindarin name Bar-in-Mŷl consists of bar ("home") and mŷl ("gulls").
