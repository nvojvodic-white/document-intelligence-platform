# Ras Morthil

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Ras_Morthil

Ras Morthil must be another name for Andrast, the great headland at the end of the northern arm of the Bay of Belfalas.


== Etymology ==
The name Ras Morthil likely consists of the Sindarin word ras ("cape, shore") and the word Morthil. Morthil likely contains the elements mor ("dark, black") and the nasally mutated til ("point, spike, (sharp) horn, tine, ending").
