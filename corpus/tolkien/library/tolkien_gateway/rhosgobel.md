# Rhosgobel

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Rhosgobel

Rhosgobel was the old home of the wizard Radagast.


== Location ==
Rhosgobel was located near the borders of Mirkwood near the southern end of Mirkwood, probably on its western side south of the Gladden Fields and opposite of the East Bight of the forest, because scouts sent by Elrond used the pass at the sources of the Gladden River, went down into Wilderland and over the Gladden Fields to reach Rhosgobel and returned over the high pass that was called the Redhorn Gate to Rivendell.


== History ==
At some point in time after his arrival in Middle-earth around T.A. 1000 the wizard Radagast made his home at Rhosgobel.
According to the wizard Gandalf Radagast lived at Rhosgobel in T.A. 2941.
After the Council of Elrond, some scouts from Rivendell crossed over the Misty Mountains using the pass at the sources of the Gladden River to come down into Wilderland and over the Gladden Fields to reach Rhosgobel, but they found that Radagast was not there.


== Etymology ==
Rhosgobel is a Sindarin name, which means "russet village or 'town' (enclosure)". Helge Fauskanger, Paul Strack, Wayne G. Hammond and Christina Scull suggest that it is a compound of rhosc ("russet", "brown") and gobel ("walled house" or "village", "town"). The name of the house was perhaps a reference to its resident, the Brown Wizard.


== Other versions of the legendarium ==
In a very late note on the names of the Istari Rhosgobel was said to have been in the forest borders between the Carrock and the Old Forest Road.


== Portrayal in adaptations ==


=== Rhosgobel in adaptations ===

		
			
			
		
		
			
			
		

1982-97: Middle-earth Role Playing:

A layout, and a detailed description, is given of Rhosgobel. It is built around a large furry oak tree.
2011: The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game:

Rhosgobel is depicted as a u-shaped wooden house nestled in the woods surrounding a small blue pool of water or pond.
2012: The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey:

Rhosgobel appears in a single scene, when it is attacked by spiders. Radagast later discovers that these spiders came from Dol Guldur.
2014: The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies:

Rhosgobel appears in a single scene, when Radagast takes Gandalf from Dol Guldur to Rhosgobel after alerting the White Council to his presence there.
2019: The Lord of the Rings Online:

Rhosgobel, located in the Vales of Anduin region, is a large set of ruins located in the region of "Laerlad", once occupied by the Wood-elves, but abandoned long ago. The cottage of Radagast is located inside the ruins, which, after briefly being overrun by the "Ungoladan" tribe in the aftermath of the War of the Ring, was reclaimed by Radagast, who restored it as a refuge and sanctuary for the animals of Middle-earth.
