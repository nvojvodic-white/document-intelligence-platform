# Rían (daughter of Steward Barahir)

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/R%C3%ADan_(daughter_of_Steward_Barahir)

Rían was, according to "The Heirs of Elendil", the daughter of the Steward Barahir of Gondor. After her brother Dior died childless, her son Denethor I became the tenth Ruling Steward. In "The Tale of Years of the Third Age" Denethor I is told to be Dior's son.
Rían was probably named after the daughter of Belegund of the House of Bëor of the First Age.
