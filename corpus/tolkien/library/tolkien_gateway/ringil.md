# Ringil

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Ringil

Ringil was the name of the sword of Fingolfin, said to have "glittered like ice". With it he wounded Morgoth seven times in single combat after the Dagor Bragollach.


== Etymology ==
Ringil is a Quenya name, an elaboration of ringë ("cold").
