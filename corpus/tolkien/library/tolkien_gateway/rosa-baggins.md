# Rosa Baggins

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Rosa_Baggins

Rosa Baggins was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Rosa was the daughter of Ponto Baggins and Mimosa Bunce. She married Hildigrim Took, son of the Old Took. They had only one child, Adalgrim, born in 1280.


== Etymology ==
Rosa is the Latin name for the rose.
