# Rosamunda Took

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Rosamunda_Took

Rosamunda Bolger, née Took, was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Rosamunda was the daughter of Sigismond Took. She married Odovacar of the Bolger Family, and had two children: Fredegar and Estella. The couple and their children attended Bilbo's Farewell Party in S.R. 1401.


== Etymology ==
In the middle ages, the name was believed to mean rosa munda, Latin for "pure rose", but it derived from hros "horse" and mund "hand, protection".
