# Rose Gardner

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Rose_Gardner

Rose Gardner was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Rose was the second daughter and third child of Samwise Gamgee and Rose Cotton, named after her mother at the suggestion of Frodo Baggins.


== Etymology ==
Roses were common flowers in Middle-earth, and flower names were common in the Shire.  In Sindarin, the name Rose is Meril.
