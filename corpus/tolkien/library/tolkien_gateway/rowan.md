# Rowan

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Rowan

Rowan was the oldest child of Holman the greenhanded.


== History ==
Rowan married Hob Gammidge, a roper from Tighfield. They had one son, Hobson, who became a roper like his father.


== Etymology ==
A Rowan is a type of tree, the European mountain ash. The name is of Scandinavian origin akin to Norse rauð ("red"), because of its red berries.
