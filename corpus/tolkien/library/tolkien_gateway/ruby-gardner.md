# Ruby Gardner

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Ruby_Gardner

Ruby Gardner was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Ruby was the eleventh child of Samwise Gamgee and Rose Cotton. 


== Etymology ==
Ruby follows the custom of noble Hobbit women to be named after jewels, e.g. Ruby Bolger. The jewel ruby derives from Latin rubeus "reddish".
