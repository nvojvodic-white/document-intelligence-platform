# Rudigar Bolger

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Rudigar_Bolger

Rudigar Bolger was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
The son of Adalgar Bolger and husband of Belba Baggins, Rudigar became head of the Bolger Family as the eldest heir. He had one son, Herugar.


== Etymology ==
The name contains hrod "fame" and gar "spear". It is the Middle High German form of Old English Hrothgar and modern Roger.
