# Rudolph Bolger

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Rudolph_Bolger

Rudolph Bolger was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Rudolph was the second son of Gundolpho Bolger and Alfrida of the Yale. He married Cora Goodbody and had one son, Fastolph.


== Etymology ==
Rudolph contains the elements hrod "fame" and wulf "wolf".
