# Saelon (daughter of Malach)

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Saelon_(daughter_of_Malach)

Saelon the Wise was the younger daughter of Malach Aradan, in a discarded concept of the legendarium.


== Genealogy ==


== Other versions of the legendarium ==
The name Saelon is an emendation from an earlier name Sael..th (which wasn't completely legible). Christopher Tolkien speculates that in this characters are seen the roots of a story which would eventually become Athrabeth Finrod ah Andreth - especially since Saelon was an earlier name of Andreth in the drafts of that story.
