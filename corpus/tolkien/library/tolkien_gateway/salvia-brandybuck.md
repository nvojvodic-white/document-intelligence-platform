# Salvia Brandybuck

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Salvia_Brandybuck

Salvia Bolger, née Brandybuck, was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Salvia was the third child and first daughter of Sadoc Brandybuck. She married Gundabald Bolger and had one son, Theobald Bolger.


== Etymology ==
Salvia is the latin name for Sage.
