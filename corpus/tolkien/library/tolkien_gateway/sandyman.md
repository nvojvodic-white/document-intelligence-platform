# Sandyman

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Sandyman

Sandyman was the miller of Hobbiton.


== History ==
Sandyman owned and ran the Old Mill in Hobbiton around S.R. 1401 and well before. The Gaffer was not very fond of Sandyman, or his habits of telling tall tales and having quick judgment of others.
Prior to Bilbo's Farewell Party, while gossiping about Frodo Baggins and the death of his parents, Sandyman related the rumour that his mother had pushed his father from a boat and he pulled her down; and was scolded by Gaffer for believing such rumours. He wondered about Bilbo augmenting his wealth by going out for journeys, and Gandalf and Dwarves visiting him.
Some time before the War of the Ring, Sandyman died and passed his mill on to his son Ted.


== Inspiration ==
Possibly the miller at Sarehole Mill, Warwickshire, England

Sandyman is the Miller of Hobbiton. The player can invite him to a meal.
