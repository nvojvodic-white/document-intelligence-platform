# Sapphira Brockhouse

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Sapphira_Brockhouse

Sapphira Boffin, née Brockhouse, was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Sapphira married Uffo Boffin and had two children: Gruffo and Gerda.


== Etymology ==
A Sapphire is a blue gemstone. The name derives from Greek σάπφειρος through Latin and Greek.
