# Saradas Brandybuck

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Saradas_Brandybuck

Saradas Brandybuck was a Hobbit of the Shire. 


== History ==
Saradas was the third child and second son of Gorbadoc Brandybuck, and had one son, Seredic. He was present at Bilbo's Farewell Party.


== Etymology ==
The meaning of the name Saradas is unknown. J.R.R. Tolkien noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely Celtic style.
Jim Allan suggests that the name Saradas is a variation of the name Saradoc with an -as ending like in Marmadas and Merimas.
