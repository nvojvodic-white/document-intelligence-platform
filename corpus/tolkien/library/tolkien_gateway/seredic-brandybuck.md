# Seredic Brandybuck

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Seredic_Brandybuck

Seredic Brandybuck was a Hobbit of Buckland.


== History ==
Seredic was the son of Saradas Brandybuck. He married Hilda Bracegirdle and had three children: Doderic, Ilberic and Celandine. They all attended Bilbo's Farewell Party.


== Etymology ==
The meaning of the name Seredic is unknown. J.R.R. Tolkien noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely Celtic style.
In an earlier version of the family tree for the Brandybucks, the name of Seredic was Ceredic.
Ceredic is a real Welsh name and a variant of the name Caradoc. Ceredic Guletic was the son of Cunedag Guletic, a chief from the fifth century who drove the Goidels/Gaels from North Wales. Ceredic Guletic ruled a region that was named Ceredigion after him in Welsh and Cardiganshire in English.
