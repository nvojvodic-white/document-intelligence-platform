# Sigismond Took

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Sigismond_Took

Sigismond Took was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Sigismond was the son of Hildibrand Took. He had two children, Rosamunda and Ferdinand.


== Etymology ==
Sigismond contains sigis "victory" and mund "hand, protection".
