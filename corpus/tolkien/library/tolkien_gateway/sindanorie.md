# Sindanórië

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Sindan%C3%B3ri%C3%AB

Sindanórie is a said to be the name of a "mythical region of shadows", situated at the outer feet of the Pelóri Mountains in Valinor.
In her lament, Galadriel said that out of a Grey Country (sindanóriello) darkness lay on the waves that separated herself and Varda.


== Etymology ==
The Quenya name Sindanórie consists of sinda ("grey") and nōre ("land").
