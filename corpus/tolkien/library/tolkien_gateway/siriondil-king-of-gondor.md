# Siriondil (King of Gondor)

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Siriondil_(King_of_Gondor)

Siriondil (T.A. 570 – 830, aged 260) was the eleventh King of Gondor.


== History ==
Siriondil's rule was apparently uneventful. He had two children, Tarannon and Tarciryan.
He was succeeded by his illustrious son Tarannon Falastur, the first of the four Ship-kings of Gondor.


== Etymology ==
Siriondil is Quenya. It is not glossed, but as Paul Strack explains, it probably means "Sirion-lover", from the river name Sirion and the suffix -(n)dil ("lover, friend").
