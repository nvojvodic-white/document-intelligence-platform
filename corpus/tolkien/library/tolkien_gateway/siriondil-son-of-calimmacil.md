# Siriondil (son of Calimmacil)

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Siriondil_(son_of_Calimmacil)

Siriondil was a Gondorian nobleman.


== History ==
Siriondil was the son of Calimmacil, and a nephew of King Narmacil II (d. T.A. 1856) and as such, a distant cousin of King Ondoher of Gondor. He was probably named after his ancestor Siriondil, a former King of Gondor. In T.A. 1883 he had a son, Earnil.
After Ondoher and his sons were slain in a battle against the Wainriders in T.A. 1944, the next year Eärnil was elected the thirty-second King of Gondor.
