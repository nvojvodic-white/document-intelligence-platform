# Snowmane's Howe

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Snowmane%27s_Howe

Snowmane's Howe was the place on the Pelennor Fields where the horse of King Théoden, Snowmane, fell and was subsequently buried by the Rohirrim. Upon that hollow, a stone was erected and there the grass grew the most green and long ever after.


== Portrayal in adaptations ==
2016: The Lord of the Rings Online:

Snowmane's Howe is a memorial on the post-battle version of the Pelennor Fields.
