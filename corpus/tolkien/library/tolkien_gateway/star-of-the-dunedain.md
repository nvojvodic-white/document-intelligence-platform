# Star of the Dúnedain

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Star_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain

The Star of the Dúnedain was a gift that was given by King Elessar to Samwise Gamgee in Fo.A. 15.
It is very probable that the Star of the Dúnedain was the same as the silver brooch that was shaped like a rayed star that was worn on their cloaks on their left shoulder by the Rangers of the North who came to Rohan to aid their Chieftain Aragorn and the silver star that was worn by Aragorn on his cloak when he served Steward Ecthelion II of Gondor.
