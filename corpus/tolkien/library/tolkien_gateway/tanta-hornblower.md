# Tanta Hornblower

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Tanta_Hornblower

Tanta Hornblower was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Tanta married Largo Baggins, and was the mother of Fosco Baggins.


== Etymology ==
The name Tanta was meaningless in contemporary Hobbitish Westron. It represented an original name Tante or Tanto.
