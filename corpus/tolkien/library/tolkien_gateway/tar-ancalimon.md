# Tar-Ancalimon

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Tar-Ancalimon

Tar-Ancalimon was the fourteenth King of Númenor.


== History ==
During the reign of Tar-Ancalimon the rift between the King's Men, the larger part of the population that followed the Kings in their growing opposition to the Ban of the Valar, and the Faithful, those who maintained friendship with the Eldar, became wider. Many of the King's Men began to stop using the Elven-tongues and no longer taught them to their children. Despite the rejection of the Elven-tongues, the royal titles were still given in Quenya. This was done out of custom and for fear that breaking the time-honoured usage would create ill-fortune.
Several notable events occurred during his reign:

The Ringwraiths first appeared in Middle-earth
Umbar, a haven of the Númenórean King's Men, was turned into a fortress
The city of Pelargir was built and became the chief haven of the Faithful Númenóreans
The great Eagles of Manwë left Númenór; previously a pair had dwelt in an eyrie in the summit of the tower of the King's palace in Armenelos, living on the bounty of the King, since the days of Elros.


== Etymology ==
Ancalimon ([ˌtaraŋˈkalimon]) is a Quenya name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Brightest", and seems to be a masculinised form of ancalima ("brightest"), which consists of the superlative or intensive prefix an- and the element calima ("bright"). Like all the rulers of Númenor who took their royal names in Quenya, Ancalimon added the prefix tar- ("high") to his name when he received the Sceptre.

== Other versions of the legendarium ==
In the Tale of Years, it said in S.A. 2251 "Tar-Atanamir takes the sceptre", however, Atanamir died in 2221. 2221 is itself an emendation of 2251, and the former (2221) appears in the later tables, while the latter (2251) in the earlier tables: therefore 2251 (properly 2221) should have read "Death of Tar-Atanamir. Tar-Ancalimon takes the sceptre".
