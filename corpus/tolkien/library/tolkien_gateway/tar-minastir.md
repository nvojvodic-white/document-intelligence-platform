# Tar-Minastir

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Tar-Minastir

Tar-Minastir (S.A. 1474 – 1873, aged 399 years) was the eleventh King of Númenor, ruling for 138 years, from S.A. 1731 to 1869.


== History ==
Tar-Minastir was the grandson of King Tar-Súrion. Before becoming King he used the name Minastir. He succeeded his aunt, Queen Tar-Telperiën. He acquired his name Minastir after building a high tower upon the hill of Oromet near Andúnië and the west shores of Númenor, to spend time gazing westwards.
During his time, in Middle-earth, the War of the Elves and Sauron began in S.A. 1693, Sauron invaded Eriador in S.A. 1695 and the realm of Eregion fell. Sauron then advanced to Lindon and besieged Rivendell. Minastir, who loved the Eldar, but envied them, sent a navy under the command of Ciryatur to Lindon to aid the Elves in 1700. Ciryatur's forces utterly routed Sauron's army in the Battle of the Gwathló and reinforcements from Lindon and Tharbad helped defeat the enemy. Sauron retreated back to Mordor.
The Númenóreans who had tasted power in Middle-earth in the battles with Sauron, began establishing dominions on the coasts of Middle-earth starting around the year 1800.
Tar-Minastir was one of the last "good" Kings of the realm, as his successors started becoming greedy of wealth and power.
In 1869 his son, Tar-Ciryatan, constrained Tar-Minastir to surrender the sceptre before he would have done of his free will.


== Etymology ==
Minastir is a Quenya name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it might mean "Watch Tower" as a compound of a shortened form minas of minassë ("tower"), and a form of tir ("to watch"). Like all the rulers of Númenor who took their royal names in Quenya, Minastir added the prefix tar- ("high") to his name when he received the Sceptre.
