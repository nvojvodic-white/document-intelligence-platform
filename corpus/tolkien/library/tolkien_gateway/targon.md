# Targon

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Targon

Targon was a man of Gondor during the War of the Ring.


== History ==
Targon worked at the storehouse and buttery for the Guards of the Citadel in Minas Tirith. On the morning of 9 March, T.A. 3019 he gave Beregond and Peregrin Took food and drink for a picnic.


== Portrayal in adaptations ==

		
			
			
		
		
			
			
		

2003: The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King:

A card from The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game identifies one of the Gondorian soldiers riding with Faramir as Targon.
2015: The Lord of the Rings Online:

Targon can be found on the sixth level of the pre-battle Minas Tirith. He has no lines and plays no role in the storyline.
