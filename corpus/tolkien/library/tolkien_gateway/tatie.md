# Tatië

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Tati%C3%AB

Tatië was the second spouse to the Elf-fathers to be awakened. She laid by Tata, and later married him. She was one of the Three Elderwomen of the Elves, who Oromë taught the art of making lembas.
