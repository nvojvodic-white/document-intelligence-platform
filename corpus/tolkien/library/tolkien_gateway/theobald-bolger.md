# Theobald Bolger

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Theobald_Bolger

Theobald Bolger was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Theobald was the son of Gundabald Bolger and Salvia Brandybuck. He married Nina Lightfoot and had one son, Wilibald, who was born in S.R. 1304.


== Etymology ==
Theobald contains theud "people" and bald "bold".
