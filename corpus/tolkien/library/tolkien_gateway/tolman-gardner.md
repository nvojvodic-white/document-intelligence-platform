# Tolman Gardner

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Tolman_Gardner

Tolman "Tom" Gardner was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Tom was the thirteenth and youngest child of Samwise Gamgee and Rose Cotton, born the same year his parents visited King Elessar in Gondor.
He was named after his mother's brother Tolman Cotton.


== Etymology ==
Tolman comes from Old English tōl + mann meaning "tool person". In early phases of writing Appendix F, it was a translation of original Westron Tomacca or (earlier) Tomakli.
