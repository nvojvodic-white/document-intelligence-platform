# Torhir Ifant

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Torhir_Ifant

Torhir Ifant wrote the Dorgannas Iaur, a book of lore of the realms of Beleriand. Nothing else is known about this character.


== Etymology ==
David Salo has suggested that Ifant is the same gloss as the Noldorin word ifant ("old").
