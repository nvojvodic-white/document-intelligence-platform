# Túrin I

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/T%C3%BArin_I

Túrin I (Third Age 2165 – 2278, aged 113 years) was the sixth Ruling Steward of Gondor.


== History ==
Túrin's stewardship occurred during the period of the Watchful Peace (T.A. 2063-2460).
Túrin ruled as Steward for thirty-four years. Very unusually for a noble of Gondor, he married twice and had several daughters. Only his last child, born in his old age, was a son, Hador by whom he was succeeded.


== Etymology ==
For the meaning of his name refer to Túrin - Etymology. Túrin I was probably named after Túrin Turambar, a tragic hero of the Edain of the First Age.
