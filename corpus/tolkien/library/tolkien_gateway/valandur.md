# Valandur

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Valandur

Valandur (T.A. 462-652, age 190 years) was the eighth King of Arnor.


== History ==
In 552 he had a son, Elendur. He succeeded his father, Tarondor, to the throne in 602. 
Little record remained of his reign of fifty years, except that it was cut short in T.A. 652 when he died prematurely. He was the first and only King of Arnor after Isildur who died violently.
He was succeeded by his son, Elendur, upon his death.


== Etymology ==
Valandur is a Quenya name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Servant of the Vala" and is a compound of vala ("Vala") and -(n)dur ("servant").
