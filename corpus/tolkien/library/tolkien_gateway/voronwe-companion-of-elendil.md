# Voronwë (companion of Elendil)

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB_(companion_of_Elendil)

Voronwë was a Man of Númenor. He accompanied Elendil when he was fleeing from the destruction of Númenor. Wilfrid Trewin Jeremy, a member of the Notion Club was said to be his descendant.
