# White Downs

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/White_Downs

The White Downs were a range of chalk downs in the Westfarthing of the Shire. Michel Delving, the chief town of the Shire, was located there. Every seven years the Free Fair was held on the White Downs at Lithe, during which the Mayor of Michel Delving was elected.
