# Widow Rumble

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Widow_Rumble

Widow Rumble was a hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Widow Rumble looked after Hamfast Gamgee after the War of the Ring when his son Sam moved from Bagshot Row to Bag End.


== Etymology ==
Tolkien mentions that "rumble" is not a meaningful word in the Hobbitish of that era.
