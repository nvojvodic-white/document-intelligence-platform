# Wilcome Cotton I

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Wilcome_Cotton_I

Wilcome "Will" Cotton was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Will was the youngest son of Holman Cotton. His brother Tom named his third child, Wilcome, after him.


== Etymology ==
From Old English willa "pleasure" + cuma "guest"; Wilcuma is a welcome guest.
