# Wilcome Cotton II

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Wilcome_Cotton_II

Wilcome "Jolly" Cotton was a Hobbit of Bywater.


== History ==
Wilcome was born in S.R. 1384, named after his uncle Will. Jolly and his brother helped their father fend off Sharkey's ruffians, and actively participated in the revolt and Battle of Bywater.


== Etymology ==
From Old English willa "pleasure" + cuma "guest"; Wilcuma is a welcome guest. Jolly comes from Old French jolif "merry, happy", which is cognate to the word yule.
