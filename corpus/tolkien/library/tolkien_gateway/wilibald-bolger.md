# Wilibald Bolger

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Wilibald_Bolger

Wilibald Bolger was a Hobbit of the Shire.


== History ==
Wilibald was the son of Theobald Bolger and Nina Lightfoot. He married Prisca Baggins, and had three children, Wilimar, Heribald and Nora.


== Etymology ==
The name Wilibald contains wilja "wish, desire" and bald "bold".
