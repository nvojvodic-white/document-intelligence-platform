# Yávien

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Y%C3%A1vien

Yávien was the only daughter and oldest child of Nolondil, the brother of Tar-Amandil, King of Númenor. Almost nothing is known of her life.


== Etymology ==
Yávien is a Quenya name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it is a compound of yávë ("fruit") and the feminine suffix ien (feminine ending, feminine patronymic, "-daughter").
