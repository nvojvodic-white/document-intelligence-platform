# Zimrahin

Source: https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Zimrahin

Zimrahin was a woman from the Folk of Marach.
She wedded Malach Aradan in F.A. 337, after his return from fourteen years in Hithlum. After marriage she took the Elvish name of Meldis and became the mother of Adanel and Magor.


== Etymology ==
Zimrahin is obviously a Mannish name of the Folk of Marach. The name seems to contain the later Adûnaic element zimra "jewel", and possibly means "jewel child". Adûnaic would emerge as a descendant of the language Zimhrahin was speaking.

Meldis is the Sindarin name of Zimrahin. The meaning of the name is "friend".
